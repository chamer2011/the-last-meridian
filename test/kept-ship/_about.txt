The ship as she was drawn on 2026-11-16, before deck 1's rooms were laid out again with the pixel-art packs' pieces
(step 6 of the map's rework): a whole copy of world/maps and world/life as they stood that day.

The automatic tests run on this copy, so that redrawing the real decks, by the owner in the Map editor or by anyone
else, can't break them: `npm test` loads test/kept-ship.mjs first, which points the game at these two folders. The
tests in files with "as-drawn" in their names look at the real decks instead, and check what must hold however she's
drawn: every room reached, everyone's places on floor or in a seat or a bed, the opening battle fought to an end.

Nothing here is read by the game itself. Don't redraw these: redraw world/maps.
