# project-combat-on-the-map

*On 2026-11-10 the owner asked for combat directly on the map; from four playable examples they said "Implement C" (real time with a pause), built the same day in the isometric room (src/live-fight.js, public/live-fight.js) on top of src/combat.js's rules, untried by them; the simulator and other views keep the fight window*

On 2026-11-10 they said, verbatim: "Now let's overhaul combat. I want it happenning directly on the map. Turn based or real time, i'm not sure for now. Could you show me different exemple of what it could be?" This was the owner reopening the old rule that fights stay the way they are ([project-world-engine](project-world-engine.md)). CLAUDE.md's rule now says fights play in the room at their word.

**The examples page** is https://claude.ai/artifact/Tne8aM6MmiPKox95jibpKP (private), "Combat on the Map": one fight on the Meridian's bridge (the player and Voss against three Fleet Mutineers, one with a boarding axe), playable four ways from four tabs: A in place (today's rounds, nobody walks), B turns with walking, C real time with a pause, D plan then all at once (my pick). Its sources are in the session scratchpad's `combat-page/` folder.

**Their pick:** "Implement C", the same day. Built the same day, untried by them:

- **Where it happens:** only in the isometric view (`roomShownForFight` in `public/walk.js`). The page turns a new fight into a live one with `/api/combat {action:"live"}` on first showing it (`goLive` in `src/live-fight.js`, refused for a simulated fight and for a fight already past round 1). The simulator and every other view keep the fight window (`public/fight.js` closes itself when `app.inRoom` says the room has it); in Walk-around and the rest, a live fight shows a notice with **Show the room**.
- **Time:** the page sends `{action:"tick", ms, stopWhenReady}` every 120 ms while running; the server steps 50 ms at a time, at most half a second an ask, so nothing moves while it's stopped or the page is away. It starts stopped. Space or the bar's button stops and goes on; the tick box "Stop when I'm ready to act" (localStorage `meridian-fight-stop`) stops it each time the player's bar fills.
- **Where everyone stands:** the player where they were; a person attacked from the right-click menu, or named enemies already in the room, where they stood (`fighter.person`, and the room hides their usual figure); others come in by the room's way furthest from the player; allies beside the player. `fightGround` in `src/walk.js` gives the room, its ways and people. At the end the player stays where they ended up (`standAfterFight`, which keeps `game.walk` even in a game that hadn't walked yet: the first test caught it doing nothing there).
- **Rules, mine, unconfirmed by them:** being ready again takes max(1.8, 2.6 − 0.15 × Agility mod) seconds for the player, 2.8 for allies, 2.5 for enemies who close in, 3 for those who shoot, 2.6 for bosses; walking 3.6 tiles a second, no acting while walking; melee reach 1.6 tiles; talking reaches 12; cover +3 defense when something low (scenery or a solid thing) is within 1.5 tiles of the target on the line of fire, and walls stop shots; Take cover is gone in the room (the order is refused); whether an enemy shoots or closes in comes from its weapon (an item's skill, else a word list), with no new line in the enemy files; arrivals come in at six seconds a round; enemies who shoot pick spots by line of fire, odds and danger, those who close in go for the nearest, each re-thinking every 1.3 to 2.3 s.
- **Kept from `src/combat.js`:** the d20 against defense, abilities and energy, the belt, the sidearm, talking them down, giving up, Run for it, followers, arrivals, difficulties, the ending and what the story is told. `combatAction` refuses rounds in a live fight.
- **On screen** (`public/live-fight.js`, drawn through hooks in `public/iso.js`): fighters with a health bar and a readiness bar, the notes at the top left, the room's panel (What's here) listing the two sides, the orders along the bottom as a bar (numbered, details in tooltips, a click on the room to pick who), and at the end **Continue the story** and **Look around first**. The camera keeps the player above the bar, and the typing line is hidden in a live fight.

Checked: 14 tests in `test/live-fight.test.js`, a server trial of 12 fights, and a scratch browser check (`livefight-check.mjs`) passing 48 checks at 1384 and 814 wide. Not checked: the owner playing it, a real AI starting a fight, and balance beyond a few trial fights (three mutineers beat a player who never moves in about the same time as in rounds).

**How to apply:** expect them to ask for changes once they've played it. Changing it shouldn't change `src/combat.js`'s numbers, so their files keep working; the timings are constants at the top of `src/live-fight.js`.
