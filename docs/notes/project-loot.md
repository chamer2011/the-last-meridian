# project-loot

**Loot across the world is planned (2026-11-13), and nothing is built.** At their "Could you generate around 300 objects that can be looted in the world, like in a dresser having clothes, armory having weapons, etc. What would be the plan?" (offered a session, they chose "Do it here"), the plan went on a page they tick off: https://claude.ai/artifact/BnbzMme7xJVHtuyMcGAGxi.

**What was found first:** 69 items (`world/items/*.txt`, types weapon, armor, consumable, gadget, story); 366 things in 117 rooms across the 24 places, of which 8 open (the Meridian's logistics stores and the stashes `src/uses.js` makes of things called locker, footlocker, chest, trunk, cabinet or cupboard, empty) and about 60 more are named like containers but don't open, many of them story pieces that hand out one item; 84 storage pieces of furniture on the Meridian's decks (28 lockers, 28 shelves, 25 barrels, 3 desks, from `public/furniture.js`); the people beaten already drop loot (`world/inventory.json` `loot`); a thing with a `holds` line opens as a 6 by 5 grid (`src/bag.js`); any market buys anything at `sellPrice`; the inventory draws an item from `ART` in `public/item-art.js` by id, or its kind's drawing.

**The plan:** about 300 new items in ten families (clothes 40, weapons 30, armor 15, food and drink 35, medicine 25, tools and gadgets 35, parts and salvage 35, papers and data 30, personal things 45, contraband 10), each a file like the others with three new lines (where it's found, how rare, its drawing), turning up in containers by kind (dresser and crew locker: clothes and personal things; armory and racks: weapons and armor; medicine cabinet: medicine; desk and filing cabinet: papers, data, small tools; pantry and sack: food; tool roll and engineering shelves: tools and parts; crate and barrel: salvage; safe: valuables and contraband; bar and cooler: drink), richer in officers' quarters and rich ports. Suggested rules: filled once when first opened, the same in the same game, nothing refilled aboard (their "nothing from nothing"); the pack and the load still count; theft only when seen, as with the stores, and at a port the holders' standing; a Take everything button. Seven steps, each after their test: (1) container kinds and the new item lines, the checker, the Story board; (2) the 300 items as my drafts, with two new effects (stress off, words to read); (3) containers aboard the Meridian; (4) containers at the 23 other places, from a file; (5) whose it is; (6) using what you find (reading, giving back as a request, salvage into parts crates at the Repair Bay); (7) later, ports restocking from their supplies and a dealer for junk. Seven choices with my picks: clothes worn in the armor place; filled once; what opens aboard (container-named things plus lockers, shelves, desks and barrels); theft only when seen; 0 to 4 things a container; no ammunition; my drafts for them to change.

**How to apply:** build only the step they name, with the choices they give ("your picks" means the picks above). Ask before downloading anything.

**Step 1, the kinds of container and the new item lines, went in on 2026-11-13, at their "Yes, your picks" (which took the seven picks), untried by them, and changes nothing in play.** `world/loot/containers.txt` (my draft, read and written by `public/loot-text.js`: `readKindLine`, `readAdjustLine`, `readFurnitureLine`, `parseLootText`, `writeLootText`; how-to in `world/loot/_how-to-write-loot.txt`) names ten kinds (dresser, crew locker, armory, medicine cabinet, desk, pantry, tool shelf, crate, safe, cooler), each `called` its words, its squares inside (`6x4`) and its things (`1 to 4 things`); `Richer and poorer` lines (`rooms called cabin, quarters: one more`, `places graveyard, frontier: one fewer`); and `Furniture` lines (`shelf: tool shelf in Engineering, The Repair Bay, Stores; pantry in The Galley; medicine cabinet in Medbay; crate anywhere else`). `src/loot.js` reads it once (`lootModel`, `reloadLoot`, `LOOT_DIR` from `GAME_LOOT_DIR`), says what a thing's name makes it (`containerKindOf`: whole words, the longest words winning, so "weapons locker" is an armory), what a piece of furniture opens as in a room (`furnitureKindOf`), how many more or fewer things a room and a place hold (`lootAdjustment`), and which items a kind holds (`itemsFoundIn`), and tells `npm run check-story` what's wrong through `addChecker` (`lootProblems`: the file's lines, two kinds of one name, a word two kinds share, sizes over 8x6, ranges, unknown places, furniture pieces, kinds and Meridian rooms, and items found in a kind that doesn't exist, with a rarity that isn't common, uncommon or rare, or that are story items); the checker's summary counts the kinds. Items gained `found in:` and `rarity:` lines and a `Drawing` part (`ITEM` in `public/world-text.js`, a new `drawing` part type, each row between backticks so its spaces are kept), and the 61 drawings that were in `public/item-art.js`'s table moved into their item files exactly (`artFor` reads `item.art`, else the kind's; `itemFacts` in `src/bag.js` sends `art`); no item says where it's found yet (step 2). The Story board's item page has a tick for each kind of container under About this item, a rarity picker and a Drawing box with its own editor (`inspectDrawing`, `.board-drawing`), checked at 1400 by 800 and 810 by 700 on a test copy whose board saved into copies; keeping a tick writes `found in:` into the file. `test/loot.test.js` (8 tests), 1,184 tests.

**Step 2, the things to find, went in on 2026-11-13, at their "Yes, step 2", untried by them, and changes almost nothing in play** (nothing opens yet, so none of it can be found). 300 new files in `world/items`, all my drafts with a `draft: Claude's, not yet edited by the owner` line (a new header line in `ITEM`, `public/world-text.js`; emptied on the Story board's About box, it goes): clothes 40, weapons 30, armor 15, food and drink 35, medicine 25, tools and gadgets 35, parts and salvage 35, papers and data 30, personal things 45, contraband 10; 170 common, 104 uncommon, 26 rare. They were written by a scratch generator (`make-loot-items.mjs` with `loot-data.mjs` and `loot-art.mjs`, not in the repository) that checked every one before writing anything: an id and a name nobody has (the names compared as `itemByWords` in `src/logistics.js` reads them, a final s dropped), no name of an enemy's weapon (`src/bag.js` and `src/live-fight.js` match an enemy's weapon to an item by name, so a new one would drop in fights), a size that fits every kind of container it's found in and the pack, a drawing that fits its squares at the inventory's 9px type (about 4 characters and 2 rows a square), damage 2 to 14 on the stat and skill rules, defense 0 to 4, supplies that only heal, give energy, ease stress or dress a wound, words with no line break and no colon at the end, papers with words, contraband restricted. Prices sit on the game's own curves (a weapon about 85 × 1.6 to the power of its average damage less 4.5; clothes 2 to 150, defense 0, seven of them 1; armor 70 to 1,500; supplies 3 to 160; goods mostly 1 to 60, jewellery to 180, contraband to 250). Clothes are `type: armor` worn in the armor's place (choice A), and the inventory's tip calls armor that stops nothing **Clothes** (`kindWord` in `public/bag.js`). **A new kind, `goods`** (`world/inventory.json` kinds, `KINDS` and `KIND` drawings, the board's `itemTypes`, the content test): something to keep, sell, read or give back that does nothing by being carried, for parts and salvage, papers, keepsakes and contraband goods. **Two new effects:** `effect: stress N` takes stress off through `changeStress` (`useItem` in `src/rules.js`, "-4 stress", or "no stress to ease"; not for the belt unless it also heals or gives energy, by `fightable`'s rule), and `dress` was there already; the tip now words them ("-4 stress", "dresses a wound", `effectWords`). **Words to read:** an item's `When read` part (`read`, a text part) shows **Read** on its right-click menu, carried or in something open, and a double-click reads it in the bag; `readFrom` in `src/bag.js` (the `read` order) keeps the words in What you know once (`game.lore`, id `item:<id>`, kind concept), the page shows them under the grids until **Done reading** (`readingPanel`, `.bag-reading`), and the tip says "Something's written on it: right-click, then Read". 37 things have words: the 30 papers, six keepsakes (the crayon drawing, the diary, the book of poems, the dog tags, the postcard, the photo album) and the navy codebook. **What you know showed nothing the player had read** (from a board through `src/uses.js`, on a computer, or now in the bag): `codexMenu` in `public/menus.js` listed only `custom:` entries and lore the story had mentioned; it now lists every entry of `game.lore`. Eight new gadgets give +1 to a skill no gadget helped before (athletics, void ops, gunnery, tactics, lore, survival, composure, intimidation: eight, since the sample kit's science went, the field scanner giving it already) and three clothes and a vacuum suit +1 in a setting (confined, facing authority, in the dark, vacuum); everything carried counts its effects once (`fxSources`), so a pack of tools could add several of these. 20 of the game's own items were tagged with where they turn up (ration packs, gels, calm tabs, medkits, stims, trauma kits, energy cells, hull patches, repair kits, scrap, the sidearm, the shock baton, the service rifle, the powder pistol, the boarding axe, the flak vest, the work suit, the refugee coat, the smuggler's coat, the tool roll), and scrap got a drawing; the rig, keycards, lens units, air bottles and fuel cells were left alone, since the hacking rework and the logistics count on them. **The character creator's shop is as it was:** `gearAllowed` in `src/creator.js` leaves out anything found in containers unless a market sells it, so the drafts aren't offered and only scrap left the shop. Names I made up for papers were checked against the world's files (Bray, Haddad, Lindqvist, Okafor, Pia and Ruth are people elsewhere, so the drafts use others), and the visitor's map of Haven names its districts as `world/places/haven.txt` does. The Story board's item page has a **When read** box (bound to `read`, left out of the file when emptied: `bind.optional`), says a draft is a draft on its About box, and offers stress and dress among a supply's effects. The checker's summary counts the things found in containers (`lootItemCount`). `test/loot-items.test.js` (9 tests: the drafts, what's found and where, the numbers, names, rarities in every kind, the words, Read, stress, the creator's shop); `test/loot.test.js`, `test/content.test.js` and `test/world-text.test.js` changed for 369 items and the new kind; 1,193 tests. Checked at 1400 by 800 and 810 by 700 on a test copy (port 3285, its own data folder): the letter's tip, its menu, the words once read, the tea's "-4 stress", the jumpsuit as Clothes, the board's page for the letter, and What you know with the letter in it; no console errors, nothing past the window's edges.
