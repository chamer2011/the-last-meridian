# project-loot

**Loot across the world is planned (2026-11-13); steps 1 to 6 are built (see below).** At their "Could you generate around 300 objects that can be looted in the world, like in a dresser having clothes, armory having weapons, etc. What would be the plan?" (offered a session, they chose "Do it here"), the plan went on a page they tick off: https://claude.ai/artifact/BnbzMme7xJVHtuyMcGAGxi.

**What was found first:** 69 items (`world/items/*.txt`, types weapon, armor, consumable, gadget, story); 366 things in 117 rooms across the 24 places, of which 8 open (the Meridian's logistics stores and the stashes `src/uses.js` makes of things called locker, footlocker, chest, trunk, cabinet or cupboard, empty) and about 60 more are named like containers but don't open, many of them story pieces that hand out one item; 84 storage pieces of furniture on the Meridian's decks (28 lockers, 28 shelves, 25 barrels, 3 desks, from `public/furniture.js`); the people beaten already drop loot (`world/inventory.json` `loot`); a thing with a `holds` line opens as a 6 by 5 grid (`src/bag.js`); any market buys anything at `sellPrice`; the inventory draws an item from `ART` in `public/item-art.js` by id, or its kind's drawing.

**The plan:** about 300 new items in ten families (clothes 40, weapons 30, armor 15, food and drink 35, medicine 25, tools and gadgets 35, parts and salvage 35, papers and data 30, personal things 45, contraband 10), each a file like the others with three new lines (where it's found, how rare, its drawing), turning up in containers by kind (dresser and crew locker: clothes and personal things; armory and racks: weapons and armor; medicine cabinet: medicine; desk and filing cabinet: papers, data, small tools; pantry and sack: food; tool roll and engineering shelves: tools and parts; crate and barrel: salvage; safe: valuables and contraband; bar and cooler: drink), richer in officers' quarters and rich ports. Suggested rules: filled once when first opened, the same in the same game, nothing refilled aboard (their "nothing from nothing"); the pack and the load still count; theft only when seen, as with the stores, and at a port the holders' standing; a Take everything button. Seven steps, each after their test: (1) container kinds and the new item lines, the checker, the Story board; (2) the 300 items as my drafts, with two new effects (stress off, words to read); (3) containers aboard the Meridian; (4) containers at the 23 other places, from a file; (5) whose it is; (6) using what you find (reading, giving back as a request, salvage into parts crates at the Repair Bay); (7) later, ports restocking from their supplies and a dealer for junk. Seven choices with my picks: clothes worn in the armor place; filled once; what opens aboard (container-named things plus lockers, shelves, desks and barrels); theft only when seen; 0 to 4 things a container; no ammunition; my drafts for them to change.

**How to apply:** build only the step they name, with the choices they give ("your picks" means the picks above). Ask before downloading anything.
