# project-quests

*The quest system asked for on 2026-11-06: tracker C at the top left that J grows into the quest log, no story along the bottom, scenes that go on only with the person they need, on hold when you walk away, failed if that person dies; built the same day and untried by them*

On 2026-11-06 they asked, verbatim: "Ok, now for the story. I don't like that the story never goes away at the bottom. I want more like a quest system where you can pick the active quest and it shows at the top left. The story can only be continue by talking to the person needed or doing the thing that it whants you to do. You shouldn't be able to continue the quest if it requires you to talk to a certain person and your no longer in the room, the quest is on hold".

I described the plan first (CLAUDE.md: story changes are told before they're made). They answered: "Yes, show me exemple first. Two thing tough, first, I want the quest menu to be connected to the tracker at the top left, when you press J (the quest button) a menu slides seemlessly with the tracker, in a way thye tracker expand to become the quest menu. Two, if the person is gone for good or dead the quest is declared failed, and cannot be continued, this will break balance but we will fix it later." Three examples went on a private page, https://claude.ai/artifact/HZehW7YyPowfqsd5x1zGtg (A console window, B plain lines, C checklist; source in the scratchpad of session e04cce0a). They picked: "C but the window should slide back when you close the quest window, plus everytime you select a new quest should not reopen the quest window and just switch the quest your viewing". Both faults were my example redrawing the box on every change.

Built the same day, untried by them:
- **The engine** (`src/scenes.js`): a part waits for whoever speaks last in it (`partNeed`; a `WITH: name` or `WITH: nobody` line overrides, read in `src/scene-text.js`, checked by check-story). `goOn` plays a part only when that person is in the player's room (`personWhere` in `src/walk.js`), else leaves `game.scene` with `told: false` and `with`; the event `meet` (`/api/chat {event: "meet", person}`) plays it, or after walking away (`heldUp`, in `src/quests.js`) repeats their last line and the choices. A story's first part brings its speakers over (`bringOver`), and so does a part for someone at another place or behind a door the story hasn't opened (`reachable: false`), so nothing soft-locks. Choices are refused while held. Other scenes wait in `game.parked` (taking up a lead parks the current one instead of dropping it; `takeUp`). A needed person who's dead fails the quest (`failForDeath`; `note_contact` with status dead does it too, in `src/tools.js`); missing or captured holds it up. `game.scene.quest` names the scene's quest (`sceneQuest`); what scenes say is tagged `scene`/`quest` in the transcript. `update_quest` keeps old objectives in `quest.done`. Company the story brought stays while a scene waits on them (`companyMovesOn` in `src/life.js`). With an AI, the player's own words while a part is held go to the AI; no AI instructions or tools changed.
- **The tracker and log** (`public/quests.js`, view from `questsView` in `src/quests.js`, `game.following` set by `/api/quests`): one persistent box, appended to the room, drawn in place; J, the Quests button (`window: "quests"` in `MENUS`) or a click opens it; it slides open (width/height transitions) and back; picking a quest only redraws its pages; Follow; Journal and Facts on record folded on the right page; narrow rooms stack the pages; it drops under the clock when they'd meet, and sits under the status line in Classic. The old Quests drawer is gone.
- **The story over the room** (`public/popups.js`): no bottom window; what happens is a caption at the top that goes once read (`CAPTION_STAYS`); the choices hang off the pane of whoever the scene waits on (`answerNode`, `#pop-answer`, `#story-choices`), or off the player's own head; panes keep clear of the small tracker; "The story is waiting for you" brings it back after Esc; the typing line only on Enter (`ui.typing`). Clicking someone a quest waits on calls `meet` (`talkTo` in `public/app.js`).
- **Tested:** `test/quests.test.js` (8), the story tests updated to walk to each person (all of act 1 plays through), 825 in all; a browser check at 1384x861 and 814x601 (66 checks). Not tried by them, not in the installed app, no real AI, and AI mode's choices box not checked in a browser.

**Why:** they choose looks from examples before building, and asked for story changes to be told first.

**How to apply:** ask them how it plays before changing the rules (on hold, failing on death, people coming over). Writing `WITH:` lines into their scene files is story content, so ask first.
