# project-command

*The ship's command and jobs, asked for on 2026-11-09: posts that change hands when their holders do badly (their reference: Shadow of Mordor's nemesis system), mutiny against the captain, and a watch bill of jobs kept by the bridge officer who manages crew; a seven-step plan page they tick off; steps 1 (who holds what, the Command menu), 2 (the watch bill) and 3 (duty bars and skill in the work) built 2026-11-09, untried by them*

On 2026-11-09, after the logistics steps and release 0.7.0, they said, verbatim: "Now I would like to add a big update about jobs and positions in the command of the ship. For the command, It should be like the nemesis system in Shadow of Mordor like the position can all switch poeple if they don't do a good job/ possible mutinerie for the captain. For the job like cook, engineer, etc. they all are assigne by the position that manage crew on thebridge and can change. How would you implement it in a comprehensive way"

**The plan** is a page they tick off: https://claude.ai/artifact/7C98yAKWLrGfFRb2TfXVGB (Command and Crew Plan). Its shape:
- Posts held by named people already in the game: captain (the player, or Reddick, Marchetti or Lund), first officer Commander Voss (her file calls her the executive officer; she keeps the watch bill), quartermaster Brandt (odessa), chief engineer Marrow, chief of security Tarrow, chief medical officer Aduro, deck chief Hask (tobin), comms Sorel (iven), helm Pell, sensors Vey (lira); the civil council (Speaker Halm) has its own confidence in the captain.
- Jobs are the crowd's groups in world/life/meridian.json: cooks Imre and Dessa, mess hands Bao and Lotte, gardeners Odile and Benno, techs Sami, Oona and Goran, nurses Haru and Anton, deckhands Kofi, Ines, Rafe and Zuri, ensigns Mirela, Tadeo and Nadia; guards are a gap (Tarrow alone); Yusuf, Salome, Emeka, Ayla, Teo and Petra are spare hands to draft.
- A plain-text world/command/meridian.txt (posts with who chooses them, what they head, skills, duty measures; jobs with stations and how many wanted; who holds what; review and mutiny numbers) with the checker and a board page; three new lines in officers' files (ambition, temper, loyalty), skills from the numbers already there.
- Duty counted from the game's own numbers, memories and grudges that fade, loyalty, a staged mutiny, no game over, one AI state line and no new tools.

Steps, each after their test: (1) who holds what: Command menu cards, everything that names a person for a post asks the holder (council decisions, systems' engineers and guards, errands, the chair), save version 18; (2) the watch bill: jobs drive each named person's day, machines/errands/systems count by job, click to reassign, Voss fills gaps, the player is on it if crew; (3) duty records, with skill factors in the machines and repairs; (4) rising and falling: weekly reviews, warning then relief and replacement, candidates by skill, loyalty, ambition and relations, memories, rivalries, the captain's recommendations, overrides and a hold switch; (5) loyalty and mutiny: grumbling, orders refused, a delegation scene (give ground, arrest the ringleader, step down, fight with fights as they are), the mutiny; a player-captain is relieved, confined a few days, then crew with a way back, an NPC captain is replaced; (6) the story and the AI know: `? holds/job/loyalty/mutiny`, `* appoint/relieve/assign/loyalty/remember`, facts, the AI line, the board page; (7) later: the player's career up to the chair, the other 850 as numbers, other places' chains, elections, courts martial.

My picks, offered as choices: Voss keeps the watch bill; the named crew first; a winning mutiny relieves the player and keeps them aboard; the officers act on their own with a hold switch; character from three lines in each officer's file.

Flagged to them on the page: their own rule that nothing from another game goes in (the plan takes the feeling and builds it from the Meridian's own pieces), and that Warner Bros. holds a US patent on its Nemesis System (granted 2021), so keep the design the game's own and get a lawyer's look before any sale; and that the written scenes assume who's who (Marrow as chief engineer, Brandt as quartermaster) until step 6 lets a scene ask.

**Step 1 is in (2026-11-09, on their "Yes, start step 1", which took my five picks), untried by them:** world/command/meridian.txt (posts with room, chooser, watch bill, heads, skills, duty; jobs; who holds what, with "the chair" for the captain) read by public/command-text.js and compiled by src/command.js; save version 18 keeps the holders (game.command.meridian.holders); a Command menu under The ship shows each post as a card (holder, station, chooser, skills, heads, since when), grouped by who chooses, and a story person's card opens their registry printout. The council's decisions name posts ("post" in world/ship.json), the systems' guards and engineers can be "the chief of security"/"the chief engineer", the errand is "the quartermaster", the chair reads the captain's post, and changeCommand records the new captain. Nobody changes posts by themselves yet. The ship's own lines in world/ship.json still name Marrow and Aduro by name: step 4 must make them follow the posts. No AI change.

**Step 2 is in (2026-11-09, on their "Yes, step 2"), untried by them:** the watch bill. world/command/meridian.txt has "Who does which job" (the crowd by job, guards nobody, six spare hands); each job's day is its first worker's world/life day (guards get a plain day in the aft holds); moving someone gives them that job's day at their own shift (a per-game roster in src/life.js; plan keys carry a dayTag so they actually walk); the galley needs someone whose job is cook, errands say "a tech"/"a cook" and go to whoever's free, the techs are the engineers and the guards come with the chief of security; the captain moves people with two clicks on the Command menu (POST /api/command); Voss covers anyone lost (beaten, dead) with a spare hand and sends them back; crew lives (engine hand, orderly, marine) are on the watch bill and their shift follows a move. No AI change.

**Step 3 is in (2026-11-09, on their "Yes, step 3"), untried by them:** each post's duty words are measures (morale, sittings missed, power short, alarms, thefts, deaths aboard, stations staffed and the rest); at midnight the holder is marked out of 100 on each, a week kept, and the card shows a DUTY bar and the last day's marks (written as 0/100, so a 0 can't read as "none missed"). Skill shows in the work: "each point of skill: 8% quicker or slower" in the command file; Marrow's engineering 8 makes repairs about 1h40 instead of 2h, spare hands cook about a quarter slower (the crowd are 5 at their own job, 2 elsewhere). Also fixed: step 2's galley wasn't really counting cooks only (a backslash lost through the shell). Lesson: the Bash tool turns \\ into \ before bash, so write code with backslashes through the Write or Edit tools, never through a heredoc or node -e. No AI change.

**Why:** they want a living chain of command on the ship, where posts change hands by how well people do, the captain can lose the ship, and jobs are assigned from the bridge.

**How to apply:** build nothing until they name a step, then only that step, tested with a stand-in AI; tell them before any change to the AI's instructions or tools. It leans on [project-logistics](project-logistics.md) (the machines' workers and the errands' carriers), [project-fleet-layer](project-fleet-layer.md) (the council and the chair now in src/ship.js) and [project-world-engine](project-world-engine.md) (people with days).
