# project-inventory

*The grid inventory on I: their pick (B with C's locker, smaller) built on 2026-11-06 and untried by them, with new rules they haven't confirmed (belt-only supplies in fights, a sidearm to draw, a load limit, loot piles)*

On 2026-11-06 they asked, verbatim: "Next I would like to add an interactive inventory that open with the I key, It should open like the rest of the windows in the main map. The inventory should work on a grid like the game Colony Ship. Show me different exemples before implementing". Four interactive examples went up on a private page, https://claude.ai/artifact/33XM6LNgtrwkMVW8kPnLda (A one grid, B body and bags, C yours and the room's, D along the bottom; their source is in the scratchpad of session cabc31c1). I recommended A with C's second grid. They answered, verbatim: "B with C's locker for when you loot something. Could the menu made a little smaller". I took "the menu" to mean the inventory window, so its squares are 26px instead of the examples' 30px.

Built the same day, and untried by them:
- **The window** (`public/bag.js`, drawings in `public/item-art.js`): I, the Inventory button under Gear, or `/inv` opens it; I, Close or Esc shuts it. It floats like the check and computer windows (`.check-float`, `pullable`, `leaderNode("person:you")`, the clock running on), and while it's open the room's keys and clicks are its own (`bagOpen` counts in `anotherWindow` in `public/walk.js`), so a number key picks from its right-click menu and not the story's choices. A fight, a check, a talk, a computer or a raid closes it. Opening it puts away a thing's window and the room's right-click menu (`putAwayRoomWindows`). The room's notes are hidden under it (`body:has(#bag) .pop-notes`), and on a short screen it still starts below the status line and clock.
- **The layout**, as B: gear round an ASCII figure (weapon in hand, armor worn, a sidearm on the hip of 2 squares or fewer, a belt of 4 supply places), 4x2 pockets for one-square things, an 8x5 pack, a load bar; C's grid beside the pack for whatever's open, with Take everything. Drag to move (green or red where it would land), double-click to put on, use or take, right-click for the rest, hover for what it is.
- **The rules** (`src/bag.js`): `game.inventory` stays the truth for every rule, and `game.bag` (pockets, pack, belt) only says where things are; `settledBag` matches it up on a copy whenever it's read, so showing the game changes nothing, and `settleBag` keeps it. A first bag puts fight supplies on the belt. What has no room is carried loose. `game.equipped` gained `sidearm`. Piles are `game.stores["pile:<n>"]` (`game.pileCount`), things with `holds` in a map file are `game.stores["thing:<place>:<id>"]`, filled the first time they're opened; `game.looting` is what's open, shut on leaving the room. Rearranging (moves, splits, the belt) is `quiet` and stays out of the story's log; putting on, taking off, using, taking and putting down log an `[ITEM]` line as before.
- **New rules, which they haven't confirmed:** in a fight only the belt's supplies are on offer, each use taking one off it; a sidearm can be drawn and drawn back, each taking the turn; carrying more than 12 kg + 3 per Might tires twice as fast; buying needs room; someone beaten leaves a pile, "What they left", from their file's `loot` line or the chances in `world/inventory.json` (a machine only from its own line), plus now and then their weapon. The numbers are all in `world/inventory.json`.
- **The files:** items have `size`, `weight` and `stack` lines (every one of the 60 has them now); people's `In a fight` part and enemies' top lines can have `loot`; a map thing can have `holds`, though none does yet, so the only things to open are piles. `bagProblems` in `src/world.js` puts a wrong size or a loot line that isn't an item into `npm run check-story`. The Story board shows an item's size and weight, edits them in its About, and edits loot from the list of items.
- **Tested:** `test/bag.test.js` (12 tests) and the whole suite; a browser check of the window at 1384x861 and 814x601 (74 checks: opening, the line, fitting with nothing overlapping, dragging into hand, hip and pocket, a refused drop, hover, the menu and its numbers, a double-click, putting down, a loot pile opened from its window, Take everything, pulling aside), and a board check. Not tried by them, not in the installed app, and no real AI. No AI instructions or tools changed.

**Why:** they choose looks from examples before anything is built (see [project-story-popups](project-story-popups.md)), and only the idea of a grid came from Colony Ship (CLAUDE.md: nothing from another game goes in). Fights stay the way they are with more options, so the belt and the sidearm are options, not a new system.

**How to apply:** ask them how it plays before changing the rules above, which were my choices. Filling lockers and crates with `holds` is world content, so it's theirs to decide. See also [project-walk-around](project-walk-around.md), [user-keyboard-shy](user-keyboard-shy.md) and [project-menus](project-menus.md).
