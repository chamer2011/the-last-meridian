# project-story-popups

*The story as popups over the isometric room, the player's pick (example D, speech over people's heads) with their three tweaks, plus interface size and full screen. Built 2026-11-02, backup before-story-popups-2026-11-02. Not yet played by them or run with real Claude*

On 2026-11-02 the player asked to "revamp the text story window and integrate it seemlessly in the walking window like a popup that informs the player of the story and whats happening", and to see examples first. I showed four pictures: A a story card, B a log beside the room, C a band along the bottom (which I recommended), D speech in windows over people's heads. They chose D over my pick: "D is a good start", with three tweaks, in their words: "first the style show feel like the rest of the game, two i would like the text that represent the people speak to write and not appear all at once, three maybe the resolution could be adaptable or at least the UI could be chosen by the player".

How each tweak was read, which they haven't confirmed:
- **Style:** my mock used warm steel, orange corners and a book font, and that was what felt wrong. The popups are now the same `check-window` console windows as the rest of the game, in green with the Terminal font, and a speech window takes the speaker's color. Popup font in Settings can switch to Book.
- **Writing out:** speech writes a letter at a time, at 45 letters a second by default, with Slow, Fast and All at once in Settings. Narration appears whole, because they only asked for people speaking. It also applies to replies in the talk window. A click finishes the line, and the next click goes on.
- **Adaptable:** the windows rearrange themselves by the room's size (a container query at 760px). Interface size in Settings has Automatic, Small, Normal, Large and Largest, using CSS zoom on the page: Automatic only grows past 1400x900, so the default window looks unchanged. Full screen is a Settings button using the browser's fullscreen, since the app's F11 is in a hidden menu I didn't want to rebuild for. "UI chosen by the player" became Story over the room: Popups, One box or Under the room (the old panel). A and B weren't built.

Decisions that aren't obvious from the code:
- `public/scene.js` has no screen, so the tests can use it. `public/popups.js` draws, and `public/writer.js` holds words back. A scene runs from `beginScene` (when the player acts) or from `latestSceneStart` after a load.
- Pieces are cut at sentence ends, and a limit from the window's size is taken once per scene, so resizing mid-scene doesn't re-cut it.
- Game messages come up as notes with the piece they follow, so a check's result doesn't spoil the text before it. Fight blow-by-blow isn't shown.
- Until a scene's been read, a click on the room goes on with the story and doesn't walk. That was my call, because a player clicking "anywhere to go on" would otherwise walk away. They may not like it.
- Balloons only go over someone found in `game.walk.people` by name, and a wrong match is worse than none, so ambiguous names go in the bottom window. Comms voices and machines never get a balloon.

**Why:** they want the walking window to be the game, with the story happening in it and looking like everything else.

Their screen is 2560x1440, so at full screen Automatic draws everything at 1.6. Testing full screen in the real app covers their whole screen for a few seconds, and a stray click during one run changed that test window's size setting, so keep those runs rare and read a surprise like that as them, not the game. Headless Edge with a test server (`popup-check.mjs` in the scratchpad, which won't last) showed everything else without a window popping up.

**2026-11-04, the pane by the head.** They asked to "change the talking window for the people. It should slide open not far from the head like a bubble in a bande-dessinée", and to see examples. A clickable page (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/6mPLZpQEfXEKAHoqat55h9, the Long Mess with Halm's real lines) showed A a comic bubble, B a small console tag beside the head on a line, C a cut-corner bubble; they said "I like B but with a bit more style, still computer style", so B1 readout, B2 terminal prompt, B3 comms meter, B4 brackets followed, all with a `<` sign trailing the writing because they'd asked for "this sign <". They chose: "implement the B4 but forget the < and put back the with rectangle writing" (the block cursor), plus, verbatim: "I want the talking window to be able to move if the player moves it with it's mouse and should slide back when he stop clicking on it. For the talking new window, I don't want the choice to be at the bottom but beside the saying window, when the png stop talking, the choices slide at the right or left depending on the position of the window." Built the same day: `public/pane.js` makes the pane (four corners, a tag with the name, the words on a dark translucent pane, a thin line from the nearest corner to a dot over the head, sliding out from the head the first time it's that person's, pulled aside by pointer events with a 4px dead zone and springing back on release with a transform transition, the line redrawn each frame meanwhile); `popups.js` places every pane (`placePane`: up and to the right of the head, else the left, out from under the clock and notes, shrinking to scroll when the room's short; a talk's choices on the side with room, else below or above, lined up with the pane's top, scrolling when taller than the room) and uses it for the story's speech, with the width cut to `clamp(26ch, 36cqw, 34ch)` so `limitNow` cuts speeches smaller; `talk.js` has `talkPanesNode` for when the story's over the room (`app.popupsShown`): the person's latest line on their pane (the same `#talk-log`/`#talk-live` ids, so the writing code is shared), "feels friendly" on the tag, `#talk-choices` with the options, the typing line, the cost and "What was said" folded, which slides in when the pen finishes (`written()`), and the player's own line on a pane over their own head (`#pop-you`, kept clear of the person's pane by going to the other side or under the feet) that fades when the answer's done; the old talk window stays for Walk-around, Classic and Under the room. `talkView` gained `key`. Checked by `pane-check.mjs` (new scratchpad, 66 checks at both sizes, with a stand-in Claude), and the test suite's popups test asserts the pane. The dotted underline of a lore word shows ahead of the writing, which was already so. They haven't tried it.

**How to apply:** ask how it feels once they've played. Real Claude hasn't run this: its real text has only been read back from their save, not streamed live, so real pacing is untested. The AI's instructions and tools didn't change for it, so there was no reload. Fight and check windows still have their own big windows. Backup is `backups/before-story-popups-2026-11-02`. Related: [project-walk-around](project-walk-around.md), [project-world-engine](project-world-engine.md), [user-keyboard-shy](user-keyboard-shy.md).

**The thing's pane (2026-11-05).** After point 3 they asked: "Could you make the window of interaction with object more like the talking buble, beside the concerned object, movable and spring back and no opaque filter over the rest, plus now the action/skill action don't stop time anymore". So a thing's, a part's or a door's window is a pane like the speech panes (`thingPane`/`thingLayer`/`placeThingPane` in `public/walk.js`, the `walk-things` layer over the room with `#walk-window` as a `.pop-balloon`, built by `paneNode` with a new `place` option so it springs back by its own placer): up and to the right of the thing (or left), with the leader line to where the isometric view drew it (`isoMark` in `public/iso.js`, the chip's hit) or to its mark on the classic map (`data-mark` on the marks), its look and the result of what was done in the pane, the actions hanging off its far side like a talk's choices (`placeThingChoices`), nothing dimmed (no `check-overlay`; the deck plan keeps its window as `#walk-plan-window`), the notes at the top made `covered` while the pane would cover them, Escape and the numbers on the keys, and walking anywhere putting it away (`go` clears `walk.thing`; it no longer counts as another window). I read "the action/skill action don't stop time anymore" as a request: the clock runs while the pane is open and through the room's own checks, `ROOM_CHECKS` = part, thing, door, computer (`walkTick` in `src/walk.js` and `clockWaits` in `public/walk.js`; `checkView` carries `source`). Talks, fights, computers one is logged into, raids, the deck plan and the story's own checks (choice, tool, practice) still stop it, which I told them, along with the air or the alarm being able to move on during a slow check. `thingpane-check.mjs` (new scratchpad) 46 browser checks at both sizes, and the systems, computer, hacking and pane checks were run again.
