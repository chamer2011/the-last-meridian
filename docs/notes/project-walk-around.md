# project-walk-around

*The Last Meridian's walk-around view: every part of all 24 places is its own level, with closed doors that only the story opens, and invented places get drawn rooms. Built 2026-11-01, not yet run with real Claude*

Went in on 2026-11-01 (save version 10): `src/walk.js`, `public/walk.js`, `public/talk.js`, `world/maps/<place>.json`, `world/rooms.json`, `test/walk.test.js`. Backups are `backups/before-walkaround-2026-11-01`, `backups/before-levels-2026-11-01` and `backups/before-all-places-2026-11-01`. Classic was the default until 2026-11-02, when the player chose the isometric look, which draws these same levels: Isometric is now the default, and Walk-around and Classic are still in Settings (see [project-world-engine](project-world-engine.md)). Inspired by the Steam game Anywhere RPG.

How it grew: the player tried one deck plan with bare rectangles for off-plan places, then said: "You can implement this version but the level should be all different and personalized with the room it's supposed to be." So each Meridian room became its own hand-drawn level. Asked whether to extend it, they said "Yes, build the rest", so now 121 levels cover all 24 places.

Since 2026-11-02 the story in Isometric is popups over the room, not a panel under it: see [project-story-popups](project-story-popups.md). Walk-around itself still has the panel.

Decisions that aren't obvious from the code:
- A talk never moves the clock. Tools are refused at run time rather than removed from the tool list, because changing the list makes Claude reload the whole story on every line. Walking doesn't move it either, but since 2026-11-01 the clock runs by itself while exploring anywhere in Walk-around (see [project-world-engine](project-world-engine.md)). In Classic it only moves with the story.
- A talk line never brings on the periodic full state resend, and fleet news waits until the talk is over.
- Off-map places never show the nearest map. They get a drawn room by kind (15 in `world/rooms.json`), chosen by the AI through `set_scene`, or worked out from the name and codex text. Matching room names is exact on purpose.
- Invented rooms are saved in `data/campaigns/<id>/rooms/`, like people, and a file there can have its own drawing. The player's Section Fourteen was hand-drawn from their story and lives there, not in `world/`, because another campaign's Section Fourteen would be a different place. Two places can each have a room of the same name.
- `gm.districts` in the place files are game-master notes with spoilers in them, so every new level has its own `desc` for the player to read. Only the Meridian still shows its district text.
- 15 exits are `closed`: a guard, a lock, a picket line, a sponsor, an invitation. Free walking would otherwise let the player stroll past things only the story should decide, such as Oriel's salon, the Regent's palace or crossing the Cinder picket. Asking at one is a normal AI turn, and `set_scene` opens it for good (`game.opened`). I chose which ones, and the player hasn't weighed in on that.
- The state line is `Map: captain at X`, in Classic too, so `set_scene` can now cost its extra AI round at every place, not only aboard the Meridian.

**The right-click menu (2026-11-05, 05d12ef).** They asked, verbatim: "Now I want to add a right click that act as a secondary actions. It should be a sliding menu that has different chioces depending on the target. A object would have use, put on and off if possible, etc. A person talk, pickpocket, attack, etc." I built it straight away, since it was a plain request, and told them how I'd decided the new rules, which they haven't seen or tried. A right-click in the isometric room (`oncontextmenu` on the canvas in `public/iso.js`, the `menu` hook), on the classic plan (`[data-mark]`, people's marks gained one) or on the list beside the room opens `walk.menu` (`openMenu`/`menuFor`/`menuNode`/`placeMenu` in `public/walk.js`, layer `#walk-menus` over the room): the console's window at the click, sliding open down (or up near the bottom) with a clip-path animation, themed by kind like the thing windows plus a "person" theme from `--speaker`. Someone: Talk, Look, Pickpocket (its check and difficulty from `personView.pocket`, disabled while `wary`), Attack (asks first, Never mind goes back). A thing: Look, Use. A part: Look and its actions. A door: Go through; closed, Look and Ask; locked, Look, Force, Hack. The floor: Walk here. Choices that need the player close go through `go(body, after)`, which walks over and then runs `after` instead of the talk or window; Look doesn't walk; Look on someone opens a person's window beside them (`walk.thing.person`, `personChoices`). A pointerdown outside closes it and `menuJustShut` keeps that click from walking for 400 ms. The server side is `src/deeds.js` and `world/deeds.json`: `pickPocket` starts a Stealth check with `source: "person"` (added to `ROOM_CHECKS` on both sides, so the clock runs through it) and `game.personAsked`, settled by `settleDeed` from `/api/check`: credits from the file's ranges, or caught (attitude a step down for someone with a file, 2 standing with the holder or 2 morale on the captain's own ship, `caught_stealing_at_<place>` on the record, the alarm if a camera watches, via `soundAlarm` without `seen` so its text doesn't call it tampering), and the person `wary` for 24 hours either way. `attackPerson` starts the fight: someone with a file and a fight block from their file, anyone else as `dock_thug` or `customs_marine` ("watchful" title words) under their own name (`startCombat` gained `asName`, so the prose drops "the"), costing 5 standing or morale, hostile for someone with a file, `attacked_<id>` or `attacked_someone_at_<place>`, the alarm under a camera; `afterDeedFight` from `/api/combat` hides someone beaten for 12 hours (`isHurt`, filtered in `peopleOnLevel`). Both push a line into `pendingEvents` for the AI; the AI's instructions and tools didn't change. `test/deeds.test.js` has 6 tests (780 in all), and `menu-check.mjs` (new scratchpad) passed 54 browser checks at both sizes; the thing-window, speech-pane, systems, computer and hacking checks passed again. Not modelled: what a beaten named person does next in a written game beyond being out of sight, items in pockets, and a pickpocket seen by a bystander rather than a camera.

**Walking freely, and a clock that runs until Space (2026-11-05).** They asked, verbatim: "Next, for the movement, I want it to be free, not by tiles. The time should always pass until the player press space to pause. The movement for the player should be WASD". `public/move.js` walks the player smoothly. A position is a fraction of a square, counted from a square's corner with the player at its middle. W, A, S, D and the arrow keys are read by `event.code` and by the letter, so they work on any keyboard. In Isometric W walks up the screen (`ON_SCREEN`), and in Walk-around up the plan (`ON_PLAN`), at `SPEED` 6 world units a second, with a body `BODY` 0.13 wide each way. It slides along walls and is eased up to half a square into a doorway or corridor it walks straight at (`stepAlong`). People and closed doors are in the way; open ways out aren't. A click still asks the server for its way (`go`, with `from` the current square). `follow` walks that way smoothly, straightened wherever nothing's in the way (`smoothed`), and ends on the very spot clicked (`point`, from `squareAt` and the iso floor hit). Keys pressed on the way take over, unless the way leads out of the room (`firm`, and `leavingRoom` stops clicks meanwhile). The server still keeps squares. `walkView` has `floor` (rows of "." and " " from `canStand`). The new `move` order puts the player on the square they've walked to if it's floor and can be reached from where the server had them (`route`), takes an open way out walked into, and stops at a closed door (`goToMark`). It replies with only the square unless the room changed. `adopt` keeps the page's position in step with the server's view: a new room, or the server moving the player itself, resets it. Each agreed square is written into the page's view, the bug the browser check caught first. The iso view draws the player where they are (`placeYou`), every frame while they walk. **The clock** runs from the start. There's no "Walking starts the clock", no two-minute idle stop, and no stop for menus, the deck plan or reading a scene. It still stops for talks, fights, raids, the story being written, text in the typing line, the Story board, and a minimized window. Space (`toggleClock`, `pauseKey`) pauses and goes on, except in fights, checks, raids and talks, which use Space themselves, and Enter now does what Space did for the story over the room. In a room the typing line no longer takes the keys after every redraw (`focusComposer` in `public/app.js`): it's clicked, or Enter brings it up. Esc gives the keys back, Space with nothing typed pauses, and number keys pick the story's choices (`pickOption`). News from the clock while a menu is open waits behind it (`newsBehindMenu`), except a raid, which closes the menu. The page no longer uses the server's `step` order, which stays for the tests. 803 tests (`test/move.test.js` has 5, and `test/walk.test.js` 3 more); `move-check.mjs` (new scratchpad) 54 browser checks at both sizes, with every key, walls, the server following, a click on a thing and on the floor, the typing line, a number key, Space, a menu, and walking out through a door. Untried by them. Not done: a key to use what's in front of you (things are still clicked), and other people walking freely (they still go square to square, gliding).

**Why:** the player wants rooms to look like what they're supposed to be, with mechanical things staying local and free.

**How to apply:** Real Claude has never run this, so how well it keeps to one to three sentences, how often and how well it calls `set_scene` with `kind` and `things`, and whether it follows the Map line away from the Meridian are unknown. The per-line costs are estimates (about 1.7 cents on Opus, 0.07 on Haiku, at a 50k story). Check `data/usage.jsonl` after the player tries it. Timers run slowly when the browser pane is in the background, so walking looks slow in testing there. The drawing tools (`maps-src/`, `build-maps.mjs`) are in a session scratchpad and won't last; the JSON in `world/maps/` is the thing to edit. Related: [project-last-meridian](project-last-meridian.md), [project-fleet-layer](project-fleet-layer.md).

**What's here floats over the room (2026-11-09).** At their "You can integrate C but without the things subcategory" (idea C on the design canvas, see [project-interface-canvas](project-interface-canvas.md)), `hereNode` in `public/walk.js` is a pane like the speech panes, floating over the room's right side: four corners, and a tag with the room's name and deck that folds it to one line of counts, such as `4 people · 7 systems · 1 way out` (`keepHere`, kept in `localStorage` as `meridian-here`, with whether About this room is open). Open, it has About this room, the place's plan button, People (a badge in their colour, the name, and what they're doing), Systems as tags by state (`partChip`: teal, amber when off or broken, red when ringing), the room note, Ways out and You can. Things aren't listed, since they're the ? marks in the room. It's an absolutely placed grid child of `.walk-view` over the plan area (the view is `position: relative` now), at z-index 21, under the speech panes, the thing windows, the right-click menu and the tracker. `placeHere` puts it under the clock and clear of the typing window, and sets `--here-room`, what it covers on the right while it's open: the caption and the waiting chip keep clear of it, the classic plan pads by it, and `isoCover` in `public/iso.js` keeps the player in the rest of the room (`aim` centres in `across`, the width less the cover, so the whole level's picture isn't drawn again). `placePane` in `public/popups.js` keeps the speech panes to its left (`hereCover`), and a talk's list of what to say now keeps out from under the tracker wherever it hangs (`roofFor` in `placeChoices`), which mattered once the list started going on the left of the person's words. The isometric room's help line is gone, as on their canvas (the classic plan keeps its key), and the clock over the room wraps onto a second line rather than run under the tracker when it says why it's stopped. At 640 wide or less the pane goes under the room, as the old panel did. `strip-check.mjs` (scratchpad) passed 141 checks at 1384, 814, 620 and 540 wide: the strip and its menus, Esc and clicks elsewhere, the pane's place under the clock and clear of the tracker and the typing window, folding and its memory, About this room, a talk opened from Pell's row keeping left of the pane, and nothing overlapping. 908 tests. Untried by them.
