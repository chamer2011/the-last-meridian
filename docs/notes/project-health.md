# project-health

*The health system, asked for on 2026-11-10: death hard to reach because medics carry anyone who can't get to the medbay on their own and treat them there, and a health state for everyone, made up when a game starts; offered three ways, they picked Way 3, a body for everyone; a ten-step plan page they tick off; nothing built yet*

On 2026-11-10, right after the AI game master went and the old menu came back, they said, verbatim: "Now I would like to attack the health system in the game. First, death should be, for now and in most case hard because the medic tanks in charge poeple that cannot go in the medbay by themselve (unconsious, no legs, etc). Plus the health of everyone should exist in the game and be generated. What are the possible way to achieve it?"

**What the game did with health then** (check against the code before relying on it):
- Nobody died one by one. Losing a fight (`endCombat` in `src/combat.js`) was a setback: a random wound from `CONDITIONS` in `src/character-data.js`, health set to a quarter, a quarter of the credits gone, where the player fell; Survivor kept 1 health once.
- Someone beaten from the right-click menu was marked hurt for 12 hours (`afterDeedFight` in `src/deeds.js`, `hurt_hours` in `world/deeds.json`) and filtered out of the room (`src/walk.js`); `lostReason` in `src/command.js` called it "being patched up" and the watch bill covered them. In a live fight someone at 0 stops standing; after the fight the fight's bodies are gone.
- Story people died only when their contact's status was set to dead (`update_contact` in `src/tools.js`); `stillAlive` in `src/command.js` says nothing kills the crowd. A quest fails when its person is dead.
- The 900 were `game.ship.aboard` with one `health` 0 to 100 (`world/ship.json`, start 70); under 30 a few die a day down to 300 (`countTheDead` in `src/ship.js`), counted for the chief medical officer's "deaths aboard". Medicine crates drain by head count, faster the sicker the ship.
- The medbay (deck 2, the room's desc "Four cots, a scan bed, and a queue that never quite ends"): Dr. Aduro (chief medical officer, heads nurses, duty "medicine out, deaths aboard"), Nurse Haru and Nurse Anton on rounds (`ward-*` spots), six crowd people queueing each morning, Vik and Hana "laid up on a cot" forever (`patient-1/2`), a cot with its curtain drawn as a thing, the medicine cabinet with 5 crates.
- People's files had attributes, age and species but no health; enemies have `hp`. Marrow's file says he's missing two fingers, as appearance only. The crowd's printout is made up from the name, seeded (`src/person-sheet.js`). Sleeping people and downed fighters are already drawn lying down (`drawPerson` in `public/iso.js`).
- Counts: 62 story people, about 280 crowd people with days across 24 places (26 on the Meridian), 35 enemy kinds.

**The three ways put to them:** 1, health on paper with a quick trip to the medbay (about 3 steps, nobody seen carried); 2, wounds that matter and medics who come (about 6 steps, my pick then); 3, a body for everyone, with parts, hits landing on a part at random, artificial limbs, the 900 on paper (about 10 steps, changes where fight damage lands). They answered "Way 3".

**The plan** is a page they tick off: https://claude.ai/artifact/3nvKGghmgyLtW9DN4RefcC (Bodies and Medbay Plan). Its rules: everyone has a body made up at the start from species, age, job and past, a person's file winning; hits land at random by part size, never chosen (no aimed shots, which they turned down); nobody dies in a fight, death comes afterwards from bleeding that isn't reached in time, wounds left to rot, or sickness with no medicine; nothing from nothing (treatments use medicine doses from the cabinet, artificial limbs parts crates); the medics are people on the watch bill who walk, and can be busy, locked out, in the dark or dead; skill shows in the work; plain-text files with the checker and board pages; everything by clicking. Fights keep their health, hit chances and damage; what changes is where a hit lands, the wound it leaves, and a broken leg or arm in the room fight. They were told this changes fights before they chose it.

Files proposed (drafts): a `Body` part in each species file (parts with hit weights, plus `bones: heavy` for Korrun, `heals: slowly` for Thessan, `bleeds: no` and `mended by: the techs, with parts crates` for Synthetics, all from the species' one line); an optional `Body` part in a person's file (Marrow's fingers); `world/health/wounds.txt` (bruise, cut, deep cut, broken bone on an arm or a leg, burn, knock, concussion, lost, infected, with bleeding per hour and healing days); `world/health/meridian.txt` (the medbay room, its four cots, who answers a call, blood thresholds, 20 doses to a crate, treatments with doses and minutes).

The ten steps, each after their test: 1 a body for everyone (seen only: Look, the printout's chart, the inventory figure; save version 20); 2 fights land on the body; 3 living with wounds (healing, bleeding, infection, limps, arms, the morning queue); 4 down where they fell, and the medics come (carried on screen, cots, the player carried and waking in the medbay instead of losing a quarter of their money, Carry on the right-click menu, the 12 hours out of sight gone); 5 treatment in the medbay (by skill, doses, four cots and a queue, laid up, watch bill, quests wait, visits, the Ship menu, two duty measures); 6 when help doesn't come (death, the Names Bulkhead on deck 1, record facts, roster, morale); 7 lost limbs, artificial ones made in Repair from parts crates, synthetic bodies mended by techs; 8 the 900 and sickness (a register, illness spreading, the medbay computer's list); 9 the story knows (`? hurt`, `? can walk`, `? laid up`, `* wound`, `* heal`, `* dies`, record, board pages); 10 later, other places' doctors and clinics.

Six choices on the page, with my picks: can the player die (not yet); the 900 (a register, changed from counts once they chose Way 3, since it makes the daily deaths named); who carries (the nurses, then spare hands the doctor sends); how many parts (six, hands and fingers as marks on an arm); synthetics (mended by the techs with parts); losing a limb (rare, in fights and from neglect).

Build only the step they name, in order, each after their test.

**Step 1, a body for everyone, went in on 2026-11-10, on "Yes, step 1" (which took my six picks), untried by them.** Nothing works differently: bodies are only shown.
- Files: `world/health/wounds.txt` (`old marks: one every 20 years after 20, at most 3`, `rough work:` words, 12 wounds and 21 old marks, each `name, where: words; rules`), `world/health/meridian.txt` (`At the start:` Vik's broken right leg, Hana's deep burn and burn), `world/health/_how-to-write-health.txt`, all read by `public/health-text.js` (`readKindLine`, `readRule`, `readWhere`, `readBodyLine`, `readStartLine`, `readOldMarks`, parse and write for both files) and loaded by `reloadHealth` in `src/world.js` (`WOUNDS`, `WORLD_HEALTH`, `GAME_HEALTH_DIR`). A species file's `Body` part is fields (`hit` with a new `parts` value type in `public/world-text.js`, `bones break`, `wounds heal`, `bleeds`, `own marks only`, `mended by`); a person's `Body` part is items, body lines, with `nothing else`. Ten people got one from their own appearance words (Marrow, Voss, Rennick, Rusk, Orsolya, Marchetti, Talia, Hesk, Brenna, Dorn); 16 outcomes in `src/life-data.js` got `marks` from their own text.
- `src/body.js`: no body is in the save. Each is made from `game.bodySeed` (save version 20, `fromVersion19`, `newBodySeed` in `assembleGame`) and the person's key (`id:<id>`, or `crowd:<place>:<name>`), cached per seed. Old marks: (age − after) / every, twice for rough work, capped at the most less what's written; a kind likelier in the job ×4; synthetics only their own; a kind is named only by its exact name or its name and a comma (so "a burn scar along one jaw" is an old mark, not a burn). The player's body: biography lines matched to outcomes by their bio, traits (`TRAIT_MARKS`, skipped when an outcome that gave the trait has marks), careers and background as the job, and conditions as wounds on seeded parts (`CONDITION_WOUNDS`; an infection on another wound; blood loss puts blood at 55). `bodyView` gives part states, marks, `lines` (the same mark on several parts joined: "old burn scars on the chest, both arms and left leg"), status, blood (null for a species that doesn't bleed) and species notes; `lookLine` leaves out the old marks a person's own file gives, since Look shows their appearance.
- `crowdWho` gives the crowd's species, pronouns and age from a stream of its own, shared with `src/person-sheet.js`, so the crowd's printouts were remade once (some people's species changed; an "Old" name is 60 or more). The printout's `split(/s+/)` post bug is fixed (`crowdJob`): "Mess Hand Bao" prints Mess Hand.
- Shown: `personView` carries `marks` (Look, `lookAtPerson` in `public/walk.js`); `personSheet` carries `body`; `publicState` carries the player's `body`. The printouts have a BODY block (`bodyBlock`/`bodyFigure` in `public/sheet.js`: an SVG figure beside the label, its right arm on the left, with each part's marks and the species' notes as hover text; the status and blood on a line; the lines worst first). The inventory's figure colours its characters by part (`FIGURE_PARTS`, `.bag-bp-*`), with the status and blood in its box's corner.
- The Story board: a person's Body box edits the lines; a species' Body box shows its lines (changed in the file for now); a save reads bodies again (`clearBodies`) and shows `healthProblems` for that file. `npm run check-story` includes `healthProblems`.
- `test/body.test.js` has 9 tests (851 in all). Screens checked against a test copy on port 3299 at 1400×900 and 810×800: nothing overlaps; the player's printout at 1400 went from 11px type to 10px, Vik's at 810×800 from 9 to 8, the rest unchanged. Look on Vik was checked through the data, not by clicking him in the picture, and the board's Body boxes weren't opened in a browser.

**Step 2, fights land on the body, went in on 2026-11-10, on "Go on to step 2", untried by them.**
- `world/health/wounds.txt` gained `a wound from a hit: 20% of their health` and `a hard wound: 40% of their health, or a hard hit` (`readShare`), a `Weapons` part (a kind of hit and words in a weapon's name, first match wins, `anything else` the rest: burn, blade, shot, blunt; `readWeaponLine`), a `Hits` part (`kind[ for species]: light; hard: wound on where, ...; down: wound on where`, `anything` any kind; `readHitLine`), synthetic wounds (dent, cracked casing, ruined joint on an arm or a leg), and the rule `in a fight: attack -N` (broken arm -2, concussion -1, ruined arm joint -2). All 88 weapons in the game were listed by kind and the words fixed where they fell wrong (longrifle, burning, saber, shears, sharpened, hook, mandible, drill, needle).
- `src/body.js`: wounds are kept now, save version 21 (`fromVersion20`): the player's in `game.wounds`, anyone else's in `game.bodies[key].wounds` by their body's key, each `{ part, kind, words, at, until }`. `addWounds`, `woundsNow`, `healWounds` (called in `hoursPass`, with a `[HEALTH] Healed:` line for the player's): a wound is simply gone after its kind's days, untreated, or treated where it only heals once treated, or its hours out cold; `never heals` never goes. `bodyOf` and the player's body show them (`withWounds`, `markOfWound`, also on a cached body, the bug the screen check found). `landHit(body, { weapon, damage, maxHp, critical, down })` picks the part by the species' hit weights, never chosen, and the wound by the share of health and the kind of hit; the hit that puts someone down always leaves at least the light wound. `fighterBody` binds a fighter to a body (a person's file by id, a crowd person by place and name, anyone else a body for the fight alone, machines none); `fightAttackOf`, `legWound`, `downState`, `fightWords`, `woundEffects` (the player's attack, through `fxSources` in `src/rules.js`).
- `src/combat.js`: every fighter carries `body` and `wounds` (`newFighter`, bound in `startCombat`, arrivals too); `hurtEnemy`, `attackPlayer` and `attackFollower` land each hit, put the part in its numbers ("6 damage to the left arm", `where` on the event), push "That leaves X with ..." / "It leaves you with ...", and give a down fighter's state as the down event's numbers; enemies and followers attack worse by their wounds; the player's wounds go straight into the save, others' fresh wounds at `endCombat` for bodies with a key; the simulator's snapshot keeps `wounds`. `inflictWound` is gone: neither a hard hit nor losing picks a random condition any more (`COMBAT_TEXT.player.wound` is now unused). Run for it is greyed with "not with a broken left leg", and refused. `fighterView` and the player's view carry `wounds` for the cards (`public/fight.js`) and the room's panel (`public/live-fight.js`, which also adds "(left arm)" after a hit's line and how someone down is).
- `src/live-fight.js`: someone with a wound they can't stand on doesn't walk or plan a move (`legOf`), the player's move order is refused, and going for someone out of reach says once "You can't get to them with a broken right leg."
- `src/deeds.js`: someone with only a name attacked from the right-click menu fights with their own body, so their wounds stay; they still go out of sight for 12 hours afterwards (step 4 changes that).
- `test/wounds.test.js` has 7 tests (858 in all, all passing alone; two timing tests, the year and the long gap, failed once in a full run while another session was working here, and take the same time on the commit before this step). Screens checked against a test copy on port 3299: the room fight at 1400 (hits with "(right leg)", wounds in the panel, Brandt's printout with her three deep cuts afterwards), the fight window at 1400 and 810 (numbers with the part, wound lines); the room fight at 810 (a deep cut under Mess Hand Lotte in the panel, the notes with the part, nothing overlapping); a card's wound line in the window never came up (no serious wound in those fights).

**Step 3, living with wounds, went in on 2026-11-10, on "Step 3", untried by them.**
- `world/health/wounds.txt` gained eight lines at the top (`CARE` in `public/health-text.js`, read by `careOf`): blood comes back 2 an hour once nothing bleeds; under 60 blood, checks one step (2) harder; bleeding stops at 35 for now (the floor step 6 lowers); people dress their own wounds that aren't serious after an hour; left alone, a wound that only heals once treated takes twice as long; a limp walks at 60% of the pace and a leg they can't stand on at 30%; pain is 2 stress a day per wound, 5 per serious one. The rule `can go bad` (cut, deep cut, burn, deep burn, lost), the infected kind made checks harder and healing in 7 days once dressed, and an `Accidents` part (burn, cut, bruise, each `likelier in:` work words, `readAccidentLine`). `world/health/meridian.txt` gained `the morning queue: 07:45 to 10:30, at queue` (`readQueue`, a spot or group in the life file) and `accidents at work: 1 in 20 days in rough work, 1 in 100 for everyone else` (`readAccidents`). The medkit's effect is `hp 15, dress 1`.
- `src/body.js`: a wound kept now has `dressed`, `dressedAt` and `infected`, and a body `blood` (the player's `game.blood`); `stamped` divides a wound's hours by the species' `wounds heal`; `passWounds(game, hours, { speed })` (from `hoursPass`, replacing step 2's `healWounds`) dresses minor wounds after the hour, bleeds blood down to the floor, adds an infected wound on the same part when one that can go bad is still undressed after the infected line's days, brings blood back once nothing bleeds, shortens or lengthens the player's by resting and the ship being out of medicine (as for conditions), drops healed ones and tidies empty bodies, and returns what to say of the player's (`[HEALTH] You dress ...`, `... has gone bad`, `Healed: ...`). `dressWounds` is the medkit (bleeding first, then serious); `markOfWound` adds ", bleeding" or ", dressed"/", splinted"; `paceOf(body)` and `woundsPace(wounds)` give the walking pace; `handsFree(game, place, who)` (kept wounds and start lines); `woundEffects` now also makes checks harder (concussion, infection, low blood); `painADay`; `hurtAt` and `queueOf` for the queue; `accidentsDay(game, day)` (once a day, `game.accidentDay`, seeded by the game's seed, backdated into the day just ended). The status for a leg they can't stand on is "Can barely walk" now, since they hobble until step 4 has them lie where they fell.
- Wired in: `src/rules.js` (`passWounds`, pain into stress, `accidentsDay` in the day loop, the medkit's `dress`, no work shift with a hand that's no use); `src/life.js` (`pacesFor` and a per-person `carry` in `moveOn`, `queueDetour` in `detoursFor`, a detour may name a group); `src/logistics.js` (`freeHand`, the named carrier and the machines' workers all need `handsFree`); `src/walk.js` (`pace` on the room view) and `public/move.js` (the player's walking speed times it); `src/live-fight.js` (fighters walk at their pace); `public/sheet.js` (a wound's time left in days past two). `world/life/meridian.json`: the civilians' 07:45 step is "passing the morning" now, without the queue, so only the hurt queue.
- Tests: `test/living.test.js` has 9 (867 in all, all passing in the full run); `test/life.test.js` gives the two "same" games the same body seed and allows a limping walker longer; `test/watch-bill.test.js` knows "passing the morning". The year test still takes about 1.2 s.
