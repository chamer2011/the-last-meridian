# project-logistics

*The ship's logistics, asked for on 2026-11-08: nothing from nothing (garden → pantry → kettle → people; fuel cells → generator → fixed power for the systems; people carry from the shuttle stores, which refill from nothing until trade is simulated); a seven-step plan on a page they tick off, built one step at a time after their test; steps 1 (the stores as things you can open) and 2 (machines on the clock, everyone eating from the kettle) built 2026-11-08 and steps 3 (people carrying between stores), 4 (the generator, fuel cells and power against loads) and 5 (refills from nothing with a manifest, and the Ship menu's counts being the stores) on 2026-11-09, all untried by them; step 7's spare parts went in on 2026-11-09 at their 'do the spare part only for now' (repairs use up parts crates from the stores); step 6 (the story and the AI know the stocks) and the rest of step 7 (other places, real trade) are left*

On 2026-11-08, after the decks and the ship's look, they said, verbatim: "Ok, so now let's attack the logistic system. We'll start by just the ship. My goal would be that nothing gets created out of nothing. Exemple, the mess hall serving food would get the food from the mess hall storage, the storage would get it from the garden, . Plus, the generator would need fuel and generate a fixed amout of energy for all the electrical systems in the ship's. Etc for the logistic. People working certain station would need to go get the ressource from the shuttle storage to keep the system's working and for now let's keep stop that loop there, so until I rework the system, the shuttle storage creates what the ship need from nothing(commerce off ship not yet simulated). How would the engine of the game handle it? Since it's a big feature, could you present me a comprehensive plan for the integration of each step? If a different adaptation of the feature fit's better, express it"

**The plan** is a page they tick off: https://claude.ai/artifact/RmQxh1FeT31C64g86sZYj3 (Ship Logistics Plan). Its shape:
- Rules: nothing from nothing but the shuttle stores; every stock is a thing in a room with a `holds` line (the inventory's open grid, `game.stores`, already there); people carry as errands in their days (the life engine walks them); the clock drives it at the minute beat; a plain-text file `world/logistics/meridian.txt` (stores with capacities, machines that take from one store and give to another so much an hour with power and a worker, loads per system and a drop order, errands "from X to Y when under N, so many at a time", refills) with the checker and a Story board page; shortages show through what already exists (power cut → dark, air stops, computers off; no food → empty kettle, the fleet's shortage morale rule); the AI only gets one line, no new tools.
- Steps, each after their test: (1) stores you can see and open + Ship menu Stores line; (2) machines on the clock and everyone eating (the whole 900 aboard, a crate = 200 sittings); (3) errands: people carry, stand waiting when a source is empty; (4) the generator: supply 100 vs loads (air 20, engines 30 under way, doors 6, comms 6, nav 6, cameras 4, lights 2 a room, computers 2), drop order cameras, comms, lights, doors, nav, air, the reactor gauges program; (5) the boundary: shuttle stores refilled every 6 hours from nothing, ration crates and fuel cells both, as they first said (the fleet went on 2026-11-08, so there are no fleet holds to draw on; the Ship menu's stores and the pantry then read the same count), a manifest program in the lighter bay; (6) story conditions/effects (`? stock`, `* stock`, `* refill`, facts) and the AI's line; (7) later: other places, real trade replacing the refills, parts for repairs.
- My picks, offered as choices: everyone aboard eats, not only the visible people; the generator burns carried fuel cells, not the jump tank. The third, food from the fleet's holds, was settled by the fleet's removal: from nothing, as they first said.

**Step 1 is in (2026-11-08, on their "Do it" after I reminded them of the steps), untried by them:** `world/logistics/meridian.txt` names seven stores (shuttle stores, fuel rack, pantry, garden bins, medicine cabinet, parts lockers, and the soup kettle as 400 sittings), each with its room and what it holds; `src/logistics.js` makes each a thing in its room, full to start with, that opens beside the pack like a pile of loot; the Ship menu gained **In the rooms** with each store's count; the Story board gained **Logistics**; `check-story` reads the file; four new items (ration crate, produce, parts crate, medicine crate). The Machines, Loads, Errands and Refills parts are kept as words until their steps. Nothing moves yet, and the old daily drain still runs on `game.ship`; step 2 joins them. No AI change, so no reload.

**Step 2 is in (2026-11-08, on their "Yes, step 2" right after step 1, before trying it), untried by them:** the machines in the file run on the clock (the galley cooks the pantry into the kettle while a cook's there with power, the garden grows produce into its bins while a gardener's there; the generator's line waits for step 4), stepped in minutes from `passTime`/`clockRuns`; the whole 900 eat at four sittings (06:00, 07:30, 18:00, 19:30, 90 minutes, 450 each, a `sittings at` line in the file) from the kettle, misses are counted, the visible eaters "find the kettle empty", and the ship's morale target drops at midnight by the missed share × the food shortage's morale, with a log line; the Ship menu's In the rooms shows machines and the sittings; the cooks moved into the galley room and two gardeners joined the crowd; save version 17. Without errands (step 3) the pantry lasts about three days, so the owner's test should stay within that, or carry crates by hand. No AI change.

**Step 3 is in (2026-11-09, on their "Yes, step 3", straight after step 2 and before trying either), untried by them:** the Errands lines run: when a store runs short its person leaves their day, walks to the source store (the life engine's detour, like the alarm's), stands there a chunk, carries the load back with their line saying so, and waits at an empty source; Brandt (odessa) keeps the pantry in crates from the shuttle stores, Imre in produce from the bins, Oona the fuel rack in cells; the Ship menu lists the errands. Nobody carries asleep. The shuttle stores still aren't refilled (step 5). No AI change.

**Step 4 is in (2026-11-09, on their "Yes, step 4"; a usage limit cut the session mid-way and it was finished on "Continue"), untried by them:** the generator burns a fuel cell from the rack every eight hours (starting with one in it) and gives 100 power; the Loads lines say what each system draws (lights 1 a room, 80 at rest) and the drop order (cameras, comms, lights, doors, navigation, air); when short, systems are let go in that order (under way the engines add 30 and cameras and comms drop); when the rack's empty at the next cell the generator stops and nothing aboard has power (dark, airless, no cooking, no jump) until a cell is put in the rack; the Ship menu's Power fold, the Machines lines, the reactor gauges' readouts and computers' Power screens say the balance. 18 cells aboard = about six days until step 5 refills the shuttle stores.

**Step 5 is in (2026-11-09, on their "Yes, step 5", before trying any of it), untried by them:** the shuttle stores refill from nothing every 6 hours on the hour (ration crates and fuel cells, up to full), each refill in a manifest (on the shuttle stores' look, the reactor gauges' logs, the Ship menu's Refills); and the Ship menu's food, medicine and parts ARE the rooms' stores now (food = crates + produce, 56 to start; medicine = the cabinet; parts = lockers + shuttle), the kettle eats the food, the medbay and engineers take whole crates as they use them up, buying puts crates into the stores (needs room), a story's food unit = 10 crates, rations scale the sittings. While the refills keep up the food reads "holding" (no days counted, so the council doesn't cut rations for it and the chair doesn't shop for it; it settles at about 24 crates: shuttle stores full, pantry at Brandt's four), and while a store is out nothing's owed, so crates that come after a shortage last their whole time. Lesson from the screen check: in play the clock runs by the second, so the logistics' five-minute pieces don't start on the minute; anything that waits for an exact mark (`t % every === 0`) never fires in a continued game. Test with a fractional `game.logistics.meridian.at`, not whole hours only. Generous rations outrun the kettle a little (morale settles under 74). Two steps remain, neither built: 6 (the story and the AI know: `? stock`, `* stock`, `* refill`, facts, one line in the AI's state) and 7 (other places, real trade in place of the refills). I wrongly told them only step 7 was left on 2026-11-09 and corrected it when they asked what the last step does. No AI change.

**Step 7's spare parts are in (2026-11-09, on their "Ok, do the spare part only for now"), untried by them:** a line at the top of world/logistics/meridian.txt, "a repair takes: 1 parts crate, from the parts lockers or the shuttle stores, for what's broken, cut, crippled or forced", makes the engineers' repairs use up a parts crate: in the last hour an engineer fetches it from the first store with one (their line says so), the fix takes it, and with none aboard the repair waits (said once, the part's look and the Ship menu's new Repairs lines say so) until a crate comes, then it's done within the hour. A sealed vent, covered lens, jammed intake or relay and scrambled alignment take nothing; so do the player's own fix and a story's * fix. Tech Sami now carries parts crates from the shuttle stores to the parts lockers when under two. Parts still aren't refilled from nothing. The board's numbers box edits the line. No AI change. My assumption, stated to them: "the engineers' repairs" means the systems' repairs (the hull's daily upkeep already took crates since step 5); the player fixing by hand stays free.

**Why:** their vision is a living ship with nothing conjured; the game already has the ship's stores (`src/ship.js`: food t/day per person, fuel tank, medicine, parts, rations, shortage morale), systems with power per junction box (`src/systems.js`), days with blocks (`src/life.js`), and containers (`src/bag.js`), so the plan reuses them.

**How to apply:** build only the step they say, test it with a stand-in AI, show what they'll see, and don't change the AI's tools. Keep the numbers in the file, theirs to edit.
