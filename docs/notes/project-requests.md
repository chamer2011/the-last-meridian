# project-requests

*Organic quests, asked about on 2026-11-10: three ways laid out, they chose "Way 2, people ask", and step 1 (the first asks, about the ship's stores) was built the same day at their "Yes, step 1", step 2 (every kind of goal, handing a request off, the reminder, parts that wear out) at their "Yes, step 2", and step 3 (they remember) at their "Yes, step 3", all untried by them*

On 2026-11-10 the owner asked, word for word: "Is there a way to implement a organic quest system and how would that look like?" A side session read the quests, the scenes, the story clock, the record and the systems that already make trouble by themselves, and put three ways on a private page they tick off, https://claude.ai/artifact/Fg8bnmSQ81G2NS5gLMHLXj: a job board (about 4 steps), people ask (my pick, about 7), and troubles that grow into stories (about 11, touching their stories). They answered "Way 2, people ask", then "Yes, step 1", which took my six picks on the page: a mark on the asker's tag, coming over only when it can't wait; Not now and No both; generous limits in game days; no money between shipmates; parts that wear out and break now and then, from step 2; and my drafts of the words, marked. Build only the step they name.

The plan, in order: (1) the first asks, the ship's stores; (2) every kind of goal (fix it yourself, Give, a check, a place, a talk), the captain handing a request to whoever's post it is, a reminder the day before, and parts that break by themselves; (3) they remember (helped, let down, turned down, with lines in their first words, and facts on the record); (4) people's own troubles (a rival, a bad stretch before a review, someone relieved, a friend in the brig, grumbling, someone hurt, someone down that a nurse needs a hand with, which needs health step 4); (5) the life decides who asks and how (orders for crew, the full pacer); (6) off the ship (the ports' shortages and events, paid in credits and standing); (7) the written story knows (`? request`, `* request`, a Requests page on the Story board). Later, only if they ask: chains, gossip, requests from the unwritten stories' notes, faction contracts.

## Step 1, built 2026-11-10, untried by them

- **The files:** `world/scenes/request_<kind>.txt`, a scene in the conversation's way with five lines on top, read by `parseSceneText` into `scene.request` (`when:`, `asks:`, `needs:`, `by:`, `gone when:`), and parts `ask`, `yes`, `later`, `no`, `done`, `failed`, `gone` with `{asker}` for whoever asks. Four drafts, marked as mine: `request_medicine` (the chief medical officer, captain only, when `stock medicine_crate < 2`, needs 2 crates aboard in 5 days), `request_fuel` (the chief engineer, captain only, `fuel < 3`, needs 3 in the tank in 5 days), `request_parts` (the chief engineer, captain only, `stock parts_crate < 4`, needs 2 aboard in 7 days) and `request_pantry` (a cook, anyone aboard, the pantry bare while the shuttle stores have crates, needs 2 ration crates carried into the pantry in a day). How-to: `world/scenes/_how-to-write-a-scene.txt`, REQUESTS.
- **The engine:** `src/requests.js`. `compileRequest` reads the lines on top (a post by `postByWords`, a job by `jobByWords`, or someone by `personByWords`; the room is the post's or job's unless `, in a Room` says; `needs:` is `<item> aboard +N`, `<item> in <store> +N` or `fuel in the tank +N`; `by:` days or hours) and `requestProblems` feeds `check-story` (a request file is checked as a talk, and its endings count as reached). `advanceRequests` runs at the end of `passTime` and `clockRuns` through a hook (`tellRequests` in `src/rules.js`, since rules can't import a module that reads the scenes; importing it from rules or walk broke the load order), and after the Ship menu's orders and the inventory's moves in `server.js`, so a buy counts at once with the clock stopped. Once a game hour it looks for new asks: one a day, three waiting at most, one each per asker, never a kind resting after it ended (two days after done or gone, four after failed, a week after no, half a day after it was dropped), the one asked longest ago first, and only for someone `aboardShip`. An ask whose `when:` stops holding, or whose asker can't ask (dead, in the brig, laid up), is dropped.
- **What's kept:** `game.requests` (`open` by kind: asker, state offered or accepted, `later`, `base`, `due`, quest id; `rest`; `asked`; `day`; `n`), made only when someone first asks, so a new game's save is unchanged; no save version. Accepted requests are quests in `game.quests` with `request`, `askedBy` and `due`; `stepOf` in `src/quests.js` shows "Dr. Aduro asked. By day 6.", and ended ones beyond eight leave the log.
- **Counting:** `src/puts.js` keeps running totals in `game.puts` of what the player put in: `buySupply` in `src/ship.js` notes each store a lot went into and `aboard` (through an `onPut` that `putAboard` in `src/logistics.js` now takes), fuel goes to `tank`, and the inventory's moves into and out of a store (`src/bag.js`) add and take away. Progress is the total now less the total when they said yes. Done when it reaches the number; if `gone when:` holds first, it's gone with nothing brought, or done with something brought; failed at the due hour.
- **The talk:** `openWrittenTalk` in `src/talks.js` plays the request's `ask` first when the person has one (`requestFor`); entering `yes`, `later` or `no` answers (`answerRequest`); a part with no choices hands the talk back to their own conversation (`usualStart`), offering its first choices without its first words. In a request, what a part changes is emitted before what's said, so the pane by the head shows their words and the "Taken on" line goes to the notes. Talks now also play `* remember` and `* loyalty` lines (`storyCommand`), which the checker always allowed but a talk used to fail on.
- **The ending:** done, failed and gone are said as one `[REQUEST]` line in the story ("Done: Medicine for the medbay. Dr. Sen Aduro: ..."), and their `*` lines run (`playSteps`: facts, the chain of command, the game's actions; a line that fails is logged and skipped, so the clock never stops for it).
- **Conditions:** `? stock <item> <op> <n> [in <store>]` (the Meridian's stores, `stockOf` and `storeByWords` in `src/logistics.js`), the missing piece of the logistics plan's step 6, and `? fuel <op> <n>`. `knownPerson` in `src/command.js` accepts a stand-in in curly brackets, and `who` and `other` are filled with ids (`ID_FIELDS` in `src/scenes.js`); `withStandIns` takes extra stand-ins.
- **On screen:** a person who asks has `asks` in their view (`personView`, inlined `asksNow` in `src/walk.js`). The isometric room draws a filled amber box over them from across the room, `!`, with their name up close (`ASKS_COLOR` in `public/iso.js`, filled so it can't be taken for the gold `?` over things); What's here lists them by name, crowd or not, with an amber `!` (`.walk-here-asks`); the classic plan colours their letter (`.walk-person.asks`); the quest log has a Requests heading and a `request` tag on ended ones (`public/quests.js`); `[REQUEST]` notices are green when done, red when failed (`public/story.js`).
- **Tested:** `test/requests.test.js` (12), all 894 tests then, `check-story` clean, and the screens on a test copy (port 3296, a scratch data folder) at 1400 and 810 wide: Aduro's mark across the room and up close, What's here, the ask on their pane with the answers beside it, their reply on the pane after yes with their usual questions, the Taken on note, the quest log's Requests and the tracker following one. Not checked by eye: the classic plan's mark, and a done or failed line on screen (the tests cover them). A simulated captain who only presses Wait 3 days is asked about fuel on day 13 and medicine on day 19 (the medbay's treatment uses it up); parts take two months or more, and the pantry only empties when the errands fail.

## Step 2, built 2026-11-10, untried by them

- **Parts wear out:** `wearDay` in `src/systems.js`, called once a day from `hoursPass` in `src/rules.js` (`game.wearDay`): at a place whose systems file has `a part wears out every: N` (days; `numbers.wear`, `SYSTEMS_HEADER` in `public/systems-text.js`; the Meridian's is 10, and none anywhere else, since `_defaults.json` has no such number), one day in N a working part breaks the way its kind breaks, with `by: "wear"`. Only kinds with a `worn` line in `world/systems/_defaults.json` wear (junction box, air plant, speaker, relay, engines), a kind first and then a part of it, so the many speakers don't crowd out the rest; `lookAt` shows the worn line instead of the damaged one. The numbers are seeded by the game's name, place and day (`unitOf`, stirred so near days don't clump; the first version wore a part every four days). The engineers mend it as anything broken, with a parts crate where a repair takes one.
- **Every kind of goal:** `needs:` in a request is now one of bring (as before), `fix a part waiting for repairs` (one of `partsWaitingIds`, the parts with `noParts`, that no other request is about, kept as `open.part`) or `fix <a part>` (`partByWords`), `give N <item> to <who>`, `talk to <who>`, `go to <place>`, `pass <skill> [at <part> | with <who>]`. Who is `{asker}`, a post, a job or a name (`readWho`, `whoNow`), fixed at the yes as `open.to`. What counts is kept in `game.puts` (`src/puts.js`): the player's own fix (`doWork` in `src/systems.js`, `fixed:<place>`), a give (`give:<key>`), opening a talk (`openWrittenTalk`, `talk`), a check passed (`src/checks.js`: `check:<skill>` with `any`, `at:<target>` and `with:<audience>`); going somewhere is just being there. `gone when:` is optional now, except for bring and for a handed request that isn't a fix; a part fixed by anyone counts as put right. The objective reads "Fix the speaker in the Galley", "Give Nurse Haru the medkit", "Talk to Quartermaster Brandt", "Go to Haven Station", "Pass a check of engineering". `{part}` and `{room}` are stand-ins in a fix request (`extrasFor`; the checker uses the speaker in the Galley), and the new condition `? repairs waiting` is true when something waits for a parts crate.
- **Give:** `personView` in `src/walk.js` has `wants` (`wantedFrom`: an accepted give request to them, and the player carrying the item); the right-click menu and the person's window offer **Give the medkit** (`giveLabel` in `public/walk.js`), which walks over and sends `give` (`walkCommand`: as many as are still wanted and carried, `removeItem`, `notePut`); What's here lists anyone who wants something, crowd or not. The answer part `given` takes the request on and gives there and then (`answerRequest`).
- **Handing off:** `handed to: <post>` on top and an answer part `handed` (the drafts put `? captain` on its choice): the request is `state: "handed"` with `holder`, a quest "Chief Engineer Marrow is seeing to it" with `handed: true`, and it ends when it's put right (done by them) or at the due hour (failed), with the game's own line. `tallyDuty` puts `handed:<post>` (+5 or -15, `HANDED_DUTY`) on that day's tally, and `dutyDay` in `src/command.js` adds it to the post's score for the day, kept as `handed` on the day, which `dutyOf` says on the card ("5 more for a request you handed them, done").
- **Reminder:** a request taken on with more than a day to it says "Due tomorrow: ..." once, 24 hours before (`open.reminded`).
- **Drafts:** `request_broken.txt` (the chief engineer, anyone aboard, `repairs waiting`, fix it in 2 days, or hand it to the chief engineer), `request_medkit.txt` (a nurse, anyone aboard carrying a medkit, the cabinet empty, give 1 medkit to them in a day: "Here, take mine" or "I'll bring you one"), and the pantry can be handed to the quartermaster. Nothing uses talk, go or pass yet; the tests do, with files of their own.
- **Server:** the walk route's deeds include `give`, and the walk, check, ship and bag routes all run `advanceRequests` afterwards, so a fix, a give, a check or a buy counts at once.
- **Tested:** `test/requests-goals.test.js` (9: the checker's new rules, wear, fixing by hand, handed done and failed with the duty, the medkit given there and then and by Give, talk, go and pass, the reminder) and `test/requests.test.js` (12, updated for the medkit request that now comes up with an empty cabinet). Screens on a test copy at 1400 and 810: Marrow's ask about the speaker in the Galley with Leave it to your engineers, the hand-off's note and his usual questions after, the quest log with both requests ("Chief Engineer Marrow is seeing to it", "Fix the speaker in the Galley"), Nurse Haru in What's here and Give the medkit on her menu, the give and the done line. Not checked by eye: a worn part's look and the duty line on the Command card (both in the tests), and the classic plan.

## Step 3, built 2026-11-11, untried by them

- **Memories:** four new kinds in `MEMORY` in `src/command.js`: `helped` (+10), `seen_to` (+4, `about` whoever the captain left it to), `let_down` (-10, or -6 for a request handed on that wasn't done) and `turned_down` (-4), each with `{words}`, the request's title ("medicine for the medbay"), and `recent: 3`. `rememberRequest` (exported) leaves one, `by: "you"`, from `endRequest`, `endHanded` and a no in `answerRequest` in `src/requests.js`. They count in `regardFor` like any memory, so an officer's loyalty to the captain moves (`loyaltyOf`), and `memoryLines` puts them on the Command card ("Day 1: asked you for help (medicine for the medbay), and got it"). The drafts' `* remember {asker} you` lines came out, so nothing counts twice.
- **First words:** `grudgeLine` says a memory with `recent` only within that many days of it, so the thanks or the reproach come with the first words of a talk for three days and then stop, while the memory stays. The lines are `helped_by_you`, `seen_to_by_you`, `let_down_by_you` and `turned_down_by_you` in `world/rooms.json`'s `talk.stock.grudge` (written with no "the other day", which read oddly on the same day); `{words}` can be used in them.
- **The record:** `leaveFact` puts `request_<kind>_done`, `_failed`, `_refused`, `_seen_to` and `_left_undone` on the record with a line ("You brought 2 medicine crates aboard, when Dr. Aduro asked (day 1)."), clearing it first so the latest time is the one kept; `REQUEST_FACTS` and `requestFacts`, read by `factsLeft` in `src/scenes.js`, so a scene can wait on them. They show under Facts on record in the quest log.
- **Tested:** `test/requests-memory.test.js` (5: helped with the thanks for three days, the loyalty, the card and the fact; let down; turned down; seen to by the quartermaster for a cook; the checker knows the facts), 26 request tests in all, 917 tests then (915 passing; the two "a year" speed limits failed under the load of the full run and pass alone, and a year takes as long on the last commit). Screens on a test copy: Aduro's thanks as their first words at 1400 and 810, their card in the Command menu with the loyalty reason and the memory, and the quest log's finished request and Facts on record at both widths. In the hidden app window the page's walk-over to someone moving between cots stalled at 810, so that talk was opened through the game's own route; that's the test window, not the game.

## Step 4, built 2026-11-11, untried by them

- **About someone:** a new line on top, `about:`, names a trouble (`ABOUTS` in `src/requests.js`): `a rival`, `someone relieved`, `someone in the brig`, `the least loyal officer` (`troublesOf` in `src/command.js`, from the chain of command's state: `rivalOf` for each post; the latest `relieved` memory within 14 days of someone who holds no post now; `state.brig`; `officersOf` by `loyaltyOf`) and `someone down with nobody coming` (`lyingDown` in `src/requests.js`: in `game.down` aboard, not on a cot, not carried, not on their way, alive, a quarter of an hour with nobody called for them). The request comes up only while such a trouble exists, about the first one with someone free to ask, and `putRight` counts it gone (if nothing's been done yet) once the trouble's no longer there (`troubleStands`). `asks: them` is the trouble's own person (the holder for a rival, the relieved, whoever's in the brig); the checker refuses it for the least loyal officer and someone down, and refuses `asks: them` with no `about:`.
- **Stand-ins:** `{about}` (who it's about), `{post}` (the post: its title in words, its id in a `*` line, through `ID_FIELDS` in `src/scenes.js`), `{holder}` (whoever has that post now) and `{room}` (where someone down lies), filled by `extrasFor`; the checker uses Kara Tarrow, the chief medical officer, Dr. Sen Aduro and the Galley.
- **Two new goals:** `needs: your word` (the yes is all: its `*` lines do what was asked, and it's done at once, `progressOf` 1) and `needs: carry {about} to the medbay` (done once the player has carried them, `open.carrying` set when `carriedBy` is `you`, and they're on a cot; gone if someone else picks them up first; failed if they die there). Both checked against the about: line.
- **New conditions and lines for any scene:** `? rival any|<post>`, `? brig any|<person>` (`rivalHolds`, `brigHolds` in `src/command.js`), `? down any|<person>`; `* rivalry ends <post>: reason` (`rivalry_ends` in `storyCommand`: the rival's entry goes from `state.rivals`, with a `[COMMAND]` line) and `* release <person>: reason` (`release`: out of the brig, a spare hand); `* appoint` takes `{post}`.
- **The fix it needed:** `newAsks` skipped any trouble whose asker was the one it's about, which is every `asks: them` for the relieved and the brig; it lets them ask now.
- **Due by the clock:** a request given less than a day (`open.short`, `dueShort` on the quest) says the time it's due, "by 14:30 on day 1", in its notes, the quest log and its facts (`dueWords` in `src/quests.js`, used by `src/requests.js`), since "by day 1" meant nothing for the carrying's three hours.
- **Drafts (five, mine):** `request_rival.txt` (Bad blood: the holder asks you to talk to the rival in 3 days; done ends the rivalry and the rival remembers it, -3; a no is taking the rival's side, which the rival remembers, +4), `request_post_back.txt` (The post they lost: the captain, your word, a yes appoints them again), `request_brig.txt` (Days in the brig: the captain, your word, a yes releases them), `request_grumbling.txt` (Who's stirring it: while there's grumbling, the first officer asks the captain to talk to the least loyal officer in 2 days, gone when the mutiny's calm, done +4 loyalty for them) and `request_carry.txt` (A hand with the carrying: a nurse asks anyone aboard to carry someone down in 3 hours). Not made into kinds of their own: "an officer with a bad week before the review" (the rival and post-back requests cover the review's people, and a warning already comes from the chooser) and "someone hurt waiting on medicine" (the medicine and medkit requests already ask for that), and "they come to you when it can't wait" from the plan isn't built.
- **Tested:** `test/requests-people.test.js` (8: the conditions and the checker's rules, the rival done and refused, the post given back, the brig, the grumbling, the carrying done, gone and failed, and the due time), 34 request tests in all. Screens on a test copy at 1400 and 810: Aduro's amber mark and What's here, the ask about Nurse Anton on their pane with the answers beside it, the Taken on note, the quest log's Bad blood with "Talk to Nurse Anton", and the no with Anton's memory note. Seen there: the carrying request ended as no longer needed when Bao got up by themselves, and its line said someone had brought them in, so it says "{about}'s been seen to" now. Not checked by eye: the post back, the brig, the grumbling and the carrying's done (the tests cover them).

**Why:** they asked how organic quests would look before anything was built, and big updates are planned, chosen, then built a step at a time after their test.

**How to apply:** build only the step they name (step 5 next, as the plan page has it). The words of the eleven requests are my drafts; theirs to rewrite. The fuel can run out in open space before the captain reaches a depot, and then a request can't be done; the dead end CLAUDE.md mentions is still there.
