# project-fleet-layer

*The fleet layer, built 2026-11-01 and removed 2026-11-08 at the owner's word: the game is about the Meridian alone now (src/ship.js, world/ship.json, save version 16), with the Sundial and Anselm's Mercy kept as places of their own and the raids dropped*

The fleet layer (41 ships, supplies, rations, work crews, raids, the Choir's pursuit, the fleet's council and "whoever has the chair") went in on 2026-11-01 (save version 7). On 2026-11-08 the owner said, verbatim, "the fleet is now a thing of the past, could you erase that feature from the game, only the Meridian counts now", and to the cut as laid out, "Yes to the cuts, keep the two ships for now and drop the raid for now".

**What's gone:** the Fleet menu and the FLEET/PURSUIT numbers in the status bar, the other 40 ships and their day, the raids (not turned into attacks on the Meridian: "for now", so attacks on the ship may come back later, written properly), the work crews, the blind jump, the AI's `change_fleet`, `change_fleet_ship` and `read_fleet` and THE FLEET in its instructions (one story reload for an AI game under way), `world/fleet.json`, `world/fleet-events.json`, `src/fleet.js`, `public/fleet.js`.

**What's the Meridian's now** (`src/ship.js`, `world/ship.json`, save version 16, state on `game.ship`): 900 aboard, food/fuel/medicine/parts that drain daily, shortages, rations, the hull mending with parts and wearing without, health that kills people aboard when low, the officers' council for rations only (Brandt, Halm), the chair (Reddick, Marchetti, Lund) moving her and buying with her own funds, the star map and jumps (`game.ship.at`), passage for other lives, one `Aboard:` line and a `<ship_report>` for the AI. The Ship menu (`public/ship.js`) is open to everyone, the star map is `public/map.js`, the route is `/api/ship`.

**Kept at their word:** the Sundial and Anselm's Mercy as places of their own on the star map, a jump away, with the field hand and porter lives; and every story's, scene's, place's and person's words. The engine reads `fleet:` lines in story clocks as the ship's and lets pursuit/ships/named go; `* stores food 10: reason` is a scene effect; eleven stories that stand on a lost ship are set aside in `world/stories/_set-aside.txt`; `npm run check-story` ends with the list of files that still speak of the fleet (126 stories, 18 scenes, 22 places, 35 people), theirs to rework.

**Why:** the owner's universe is theirs to write, and they decided the fleet is over; the logistics plan (see [project-logistics](project-logistics.md)) then has no fleet holds to draw on, so the shuttle stores refill from nothing.

**How to apply:** never bring the fleet's ships back into code or data; words about the fleet in `world/` are the owner's to change, not mine; if they want attacks on the Meridian, that's a new design to tell them first. The player's campaign once named a creche ship, the Ember Day, deliberately not one of the 41; it's just lore now. Related: [project-last-meridian](project-last-meridian.md), [project-logistics](project-logistics.md).
