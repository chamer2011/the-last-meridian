# project-menus

*The menus tidied on 2026-11-02 at the owner's request: seventeen entries grouped under five headings on the rail, long sections folded behind [ + ] lines, three entries renamed, and no explaining text under the names, which they asked for*

On 2026-11-02 the owner said the menus felt cluttered and asked for a couple of options to make them easier to read and to know what's in them. I showed three (five grouped doors with overview pages; the same seventeen entries grouped and labelled with folding sections; a front page of cards) first as chat mock-ups, then, when they asked to keep the console command style, as pictures drawn with the game's own stylesheet. They chose the second, "Option B but without the explaining under the name of the window".

What went in: `MENU_GROUPS` in `public/menus.js` (You: character, skills, traits, perks, beliefs, your past; The story: quests, leads, people, what you know, enemies met; The fleet: fleet, ship, star map; Gear: inventory, market; Game: settings), drawn by `menuButtons` in `public/app.js` as `-- YOU --` headings on the rail and inline on the narrow window's tabs; `fold`/`folded` in `public/dom.js` (a `[ + Title ]` / `[ - Title ]` line, remembered while the game's open, redrawing through `onFoldChange`); folded by default: Quests' Finished and Journal, Leads' Finished and Moved on, Traits' Everything affecting you, Your past's Life history and Loose ends, Settings' Display, Skill checks, Fights, AI model, What the AI has cost, API key and Your files, Fleet's Rations and Work crews (with the current choice on the line), Officers and council and Fleet log, the Star map's News. Renamed: Dossier to Your past, Codex to What you know, Bestiary to Enemies met (the commands `/dossier`, `/codex`, `/bestiary` and the ids stay). The Leads page's intro paragraph and most of the Star map's went; what each menu holds is a tooltip on its button (`about` in `MENUS`), not on the screen. Checked in the browser at 1384 and 814 wide with a throwaway script; not played by them yet.

**Why:** they judge by playing and found seventeen flat entries and long pages hard to read; they wanted the console style kept and no explanatory lines.

**How to apply:** keep the groups and the folding when adding a menu or a section; put what a menu holds in its `about`, never as a line under its name; don't add intro paragraphs to pages.
