# Almagest Explorer's

It was called The Last Meridian until 2026-11-08, when the owner said "Change the name of the game to Almagest Explorer's", and the name is used as they spelt it, apostrophe included. The ship is still the CFS Meridian, the saves, settings and ids kept their names, the app's id stayed `com.lastmeridian.game` so a new installer replaces the old app instead of sitting beside it, the app takes over the files an older install kept under `%APPDATA%\The Last Meridian` the first time it runs, and both GitHub repositories keep their old names until the owner says otherwise.

A sci-fi RPG where local code keeps the rules, and a story written in advance plays through them. The written main story was taken out on 2026-11-12, at the owner's word, and a mutiny on deck 1 opens the game instead, a battle in the room; what follows it is still to be built (see "A mutiny to open the game" under "Where things stand"). There's no AI in it since 2026-11-10 (see "The direction now"). It's plain Node with no build step: `server.js` and `src/` are the rules, `public/` is the page, `world/` is the content, and `app/` is the Windows app around it. `README.md` explains all of it for the player. `docs/notes/` has the history of what was decided and why, so read the note on whatever you're about to change.

## Who you're working for

The owner has no coding experience and judges the game by playing it. Describe changes by what they'll see on screen, and handle installs and commands for them. They're bad with a keyboard, so everything has to work by clicking, with no time limits on typing. Their pronouns aren't known, so use they/them.

## Rules

- **Their old Anthropic API key is still in `data/settings.json`,** which the game no longer reads or writes. Never read it, print it, commit it or delete it: it's theirs to delete. All of `data/` stays out of git, and that includes their saved games and `data/usage.jsonl`, the record of what the AI cost them.
- **Test with `GAME_DATA_DIR` pointing at a temporary folder and `PORT` set to something other than 3210,** and never run `server.js` without both: it reads the autosave in whatever data folder it's given.
- **They may be playing while you work.** Never overwrite or restore `data/saves/`, and treat a changed autosave as their own progress. Port 3210 is the lock: only one copy of the game can run on it.
- **They also edit on GitHub's website.** Run `git fetch` before starting work, and never force a push.
- **Don't use subagents.** Eight running at once used a whole 5-hour allowance.
- **Everything runs on this computer.** The written story changes the game only through the validated actions in `src/tools.js`. Don't bring an AI back unless the owner asks.
- **Fights play in the room, in real time with a pause, since 2026-11-10, at their "Implement C".** That was the owner reopening the old rule (fights stay the way they are, with more options); keep `src/combat.js`'s rules and numbers under it. They explicitly turned down Fallout's action points, grids and aimed shots, and that still stands.
- **Nothing from another game goes in.** UnderRail and the rest are references for mood only.

## When something's done

The tests pass (`npm test`, with `GAME_DATA_DIR` set as above), every new screen has been checked at about 810 and 1400 wide with nothing overlapping, the README explains it, and the report says in plain language what was tested and what wasn't. Before a big change, make sure everything's committed, so there's a way back.

## The direction now: no AI game master

On 2026-11-10 the owner said, word for word: "Completly get rid of the AI gm. The game from now on will rely on systems already there localy". The plan was laid out to them first (what goes, what replaces it, what would be missing) and they said "Yes, go ahead". What went: `src/ai.js` (the instructions, the Claude and Ollama clients, costs), the `@anthropic-ai/sdk` dependency, `game.mode` and the Settings switch (`/api/story`, `switchStory`), the settings routes (`/api/settings`, `/api/settings/test`; the server no longer reads or writes `data/settings.json`, and nothing writes `data/usage.jsonl`), fit turns and `follow_scene` (`storyTurnKind`, `talkTurnKind`, `fitTurn`, `sceneBrief`, `talkSceneBrief`, `keepForAi`), everything assembled only for the AI (`aiSnapshot`, `aiDossier`, `walkLine`, `placeLine`, `shipLine`, `systemsLine`, `commandLine`, `recordLines`, `roleBrief`, `openingFor`, `talkBrief`, `shipReport`, `worldReport`, `placeFile`, `threadNotes`, `arcBrief`, `mysteryNotes`, `whatHappened`, `characterBrief`, `WORLD_BIBLE`, the world's and ship's `unheard` lists), the AI-only actions (read_character, read_thread, read_mystery, read_world, create_character, create_enemy, follow_scene, note_fact, end_story), and `game.pendingEvents`, the notes the AI read after fights, checks, computers, pockets, jumps and the officers. What stays: every game action a written scene uses (`src/tools.js`, still called `TOOL_DEFS` and `runTool`, now the rule book the scenes and the checker go through), the record (facts and endings from `* flag` and `* ending`), the story clock, written talks with stock lines for everyone else, things and doors from their files, and the typing line for /commands and typing part of a choice. **Continue the story** is offered only when the written story has something to pick up: `storyWaits` in `src/scenes.js` (told to the rules through `tellStoryWaits`, since the rules can't import the scenes) is true for a check or fight a scene part is waiting on, a door's check, a written arrival, a part whose rule holds once the ship has arrived, or the officers' scene; `publicState` sends it as `storyWaiting`. So a fight from the right-click menu ends with **Back to the room**, a check nobody waits on with **Close**, and a jump without a written arrival needs nothing more. Leads' rumors lost **Ask around about this**, which only the AI answered. Save version 19 (`fromVersion18`): `ai`, `mode` and `pendingEvents` go, an AI game gets one `[STORY]` notice with `turnover: true` (so its last choices aren't offered), and a talk the AI was playing closes. Old campaigns' AI-made people and enemies are still read from their files. `AI:` lines in scene files and the stories' "For the game master" notes and reactions stay as notes for whoever writes; the game doesn't read them out. What's missing now, as told to the owner: free actions; 112 of the 117 stories in play aren't written and only move on by their clock; 19 of 22 lives get the shared opening; most people have only stock lines (the map files' special questions are gone with the AI); most things do nothing yet; only Haven has a written arrival; nothing new is invented as you play. 842 tests (the AI tests went), `check-story` clean, and the changed screens (title, Settings, the talk by the head and the talk window, a fight's and a check's end, Leads) checked at 1400 and 810 wide against a test copy on port 3299. The owner hasn't played it. Their own introduction at the top of `README.md` still says the AI is everywhere; that's theirs to rewrite.
