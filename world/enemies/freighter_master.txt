In a line:
Whoever answers for a freighter and everything in her hold, with a pistol they've carried for years and never wanted to draw.

When the fight starts:
- The master stands in the hold with the manifest in one hand and a pistol in the other, and puts the manifest down.

When they hit:
- The pistol cracks in the closeness of the hold, and the round finds you.
- They fire from behind a crate, steady, the way someone fires who has done it before.

When they miss:
- The shot rings off a deck plate and goes whining down the hold.
- They fire high, and a strip light goes out over your head.

When they hit hard:
- They wait until you're close enough to see their eyes, and then they don't miss.

When they're beaten:
- The master goes down on one knee, and then the rest of the way, and the pistol slides under the crates.
- They drop the pistol and sit back against the hull, breathing hard, and tell you to take it and go.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} comes out of the cabin aft, already aiming.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} shakes their head hard, the pistol pointing at the deck.

When persuading works:
- You tell them it's cargo, and cargo's insured, and they aren't. They lower the pistol an inch at a time.

When persuading fails:
- You tell them to think of the crew. They tell you that's exactly who they're thinking of.

When a threat works:
- You tell them what you'll leave of the ship if they make you do it the hard way. The pistol goes down on a crate.
