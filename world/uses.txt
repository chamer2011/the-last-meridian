# What the things in the rooms do
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner

# A thing in a room does something by what it's called. Each line under Uses names one use and the words that mean
# it: a thing whose name has one of those words in it gets that use, beside whatever its own file says. The first
# use that fits is the one it gets. Under Things, a line names one thing at one place and says what it does instead,
# or "nothing". Under Put in the rooms, a line stands a thing of its own on a free square of a room.
#
# The uses the game knows: sleep, sit, eat, drink, wash, read, stash and quiet. On a use's line, after its words:
#   30 minutes / 1 hour      how long it takes
#   stress -4, tiredness -2  what it takes off
#   once a sitting           aboard the ship, only while a sitting's on, and once for each
#   once every 4 hours       not again before then
#   once a day               not again the same day
#   2 credits off the ship   what it costs anywhere but aboard the Meridian
# Sleeping is the game's own eight hours, wherever you'd sleep anyway. A stash is a thing you can open and leave your
# own things in; a thing that already gives something or has words of its own when used is never made one.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.
