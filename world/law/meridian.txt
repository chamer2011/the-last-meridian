# Whose things are whose, aboard the Meridian
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner

# Under Stores, each of the ship's stores (as world/logistics/meridian.txt names them), and who
# may take from it: posts and jobs from world/command/meridian.txt, with commas between. The captain may always, and
# so may anyone for "everyone". A store that isn't here is anyone's.
#
# Under Rooms, the rooms that are closed to all but the posts and jobs named. The containers in them are theirs too.
#
# Under Containers, whose the lockers, shelves, desks and barrels in each room are (world/loot/containers.txt says which
# open): posts and jobs, "everyone", "nobody" (the captain's alone), or "whoever sleeps there", and "anywhere else" for
# every room without a line of its own. The captain may take from any of them.
#
# A line for a maker the crew runs for itself (a machine in world/logistics/meridian.txt, by its name) says whether the
# law looks away from it: "the still: the law looks away", or "the still: against the law", which shuts it: nobody
# runs it, and the Ship menu's Machines list says why.
#
# None of it matters unless it's seen: by someone awake in the same room, or by a camera that works, with the room's
# lights on. Whoever's walking with you looks the other way. The two lines at the end say what being seen costs.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.
