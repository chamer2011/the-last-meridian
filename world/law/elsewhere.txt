# there where it names none; "everyone" would make them anyone's, and "whoever sleeps there" the people who sleep in the
# room. A line for one room, by its name, comes before "anywhere"; a room no place has is left alone. A place that
# should have its own law, a Stores or Rooms part or a container that's someone's in particular, gets a file of its
# own, named by its id like meridian.txt.
#
# None of it matters unless it's seen: by someone awake in the same room, or by a camera that works, with the room's
# lights on. Whoever's walking with you looks the other way. The lines at the end say what being seen costs: what was
# taken goes back, standing with whoever holds the place drops, and a second time within the week costs a fine.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.
