# Emergencies in the rooms
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner

# Three things that go wrong in a room by themselves, and can be put right by hand. Each has the rooms it can happen
# in (the place's id, a comma, the room's name; several with semicolons between) and its numbers. Every number here
# is a first guess.
#
# A fire grows by one every so often, up to its most. From a size, each time it grows it breaks something in the room;
# at a size it spreads once through a door that isn't locked. It spoils the room's air, so people leave. It shrinks
# while the room's air is shut off (the vent sealed, or the air plant stopped), while the engineers are at it, and
# each time you fight it by hand, which practises a skill and can burn you. The engineers line is how long after it
# starts they have the better of it. Aboard a ship with a work board (world/logistics) one of them is sent the
# moment it starts and has to get there first, so with nobody able to come nobody's at it; anywhere else they're
# counted as there from that time.
#
# A breach lets a room's air out until it's patched: by you, with an emergency hull patch or a parts crate, or by the
# engineers after a while, with a parts crate out of the ship's stores (aboard a ship with a work board, by the one
# of them it sent, once they've got there and the time is up).
#
# A brawl is two people coming to blows at a sitting, on short rations with morale low. Step between them (a skill
# against a number) and it's over; leave it, and after a while they've both been hurt.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.

Fire:
- rooms: meridian, The Galley; meridian, Engineering
- starts: 1 in 40 days a room, 1 in 10 with something broken in it
- grows: every 15 minutes, to 5 at most
- burns: 1 in 3
- breaks parts: from 3
- spreads: at 5
- by hand: 10 minutes, engineering
- engineers: after 45 minutes

Breach:
- rooms: meridian, The Port Airlock; meridian, The Starboard Lock; meridian, The Boat Bay; meridian, The Lighter Bay
- starts: 1 in 80 days a room, 1 in 20 with the hull under 40
- by hand: 20 minutes
- engineers: after 2 hours

Brawl:
- rooms: meridian, The Long Mess
- starts: 1 in 3 sittings, with morale under 40
- lasts: 20 minutes
- stepping in: persuasion against 6
