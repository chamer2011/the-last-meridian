HOW TO WRITE WOUNDS, MARKS AND BODIES
=====================================

Everyone in the game has a body: the people with files, the people with only a name who have days at a place, and
the player. A body has six parts, the head, the chest, the right arm, the left arm, the right leg and the left leg,
and each part can carry old marks from a life (a scar, an old break, two fingers gone) and wounds of now (a cut, a
broken bone, a burn). Bodies are made up when a game begins, from a person's species, age, job and past, and from
whatever their own file says, which always wins. The same person has the same body all game, and a different one in
the next game.

Bodies show on Look (right-click someone), on the registry printouts, and on the figure in the inventory, and since
step 2 of the health plan every hit in a fight lands on a part and can leave a wound there, which stays after the
fight. Since step 3 wounds bleed, get dressed, go bad, heal at their own pace, slow a limping walker and keep a useless
hand off errands and work. Since step 8 illnesses go round the ship, among the nine hundred on the register too. The medbay, carrying the fallen and treatment come in the later steps, one at a time; what
works now is said where it's explained. npm run check-story reads all of it and
says what's wrong, with the file and the line.

Four kinds of file have a say:

1. world/health/wounds.txt: the kinds of wound and old mark.
2. world/health/<place>.txt: who starts the game hurt or marked at a place (meridian.txt is the Meridian).
3. Each species file in world/species: its Body part.
4. Each person's file in world/characters: their Body part, if they have one.


1. WOUNDS.TXT
-------------

    # Wounds and old marks
    draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
    old marks: one every 20 years after 20, at most 3
    a wound from a hit: 20% of their health
    a hard wound: 40% of their health, or a hard hit
    rough work: engine, tech, dock, guard, marine, miner

    Wounds:
    - deep cut, any part: a deep cut in the {part}; serious; bleeds 12 an hour; heals in 8 days once dressed

    Weapons:
    - blade: knife, sword, claw
    - blunt: anything else

    Hits:
    - blade: cut; hard: deep cut
    - blunt: bruise; hard: concussion on the head, broken bone on a limb; down: knock on the head
    - anything for synthetic: dent; hard: cracked casing

    Old marks:
    - bad knee, a leg: a bad {side} knee; limps; likelier in: dock, hauler, porter

old marks: how often someone gathers an old mark: one for every so many years of their life after an age, and the
most anyone carries, counting what their own file says.

Living with wounds (step 3 of the health plan), the lines at the top that say:
    blood comes back: 2 an hour, once nothing bleeds
    low on blood: under 60, checks one step harder
    bleeding to death: at 0 blood          (where there's a medbay, someone whose blood runs this low dies)
    bleeding stops at: 35, for you and away from a medbay, for now   (the player, and anyone at a place without one)
    they dress their own: wounds that aren't serious, after an hour
    left alone: twice as long, for a wound that heals once treated
    with a limp: 60% of the pace           (how fast someone walks with a limp: a wound on a leg, or a mark that limps)
    on a leg they can't stand on: 30% of the pace
    too weak to stand: under 40 blood      (they go down where they are, and stay down while they're bleeding)
    beaten in a fight: down for 1 to 3 hours                 (how long someone beaten lies where they fell, at least)
    pain: 2 stress a day for a wound, 5 for a serious one      (the player's stress)
    losing a limb to a hit: 1 time in 4, a hard hit on a limb that's already broken
    losing a limb to rot: the doctor takes it after 3 days gone bad
    artificial limbs from before: 1 in 60 have one, 1 in 25 in rough work
    illness when hungry: twice as likely on half rations, half again as likely on reduced
Losing a limb (step 7 of the health plan): a hard hit on an arm or a leg that already has a broken bone on it takes it
that often (the lost line for the arm or the leg), and a wound gone bad on a limb that's still bad after those days is
treated by taking the limb. Artificial limbs from before: how many people the game makes up with an artificial arm or
leg (the artificial limb lines under Old marks), more in rough work; a roll of its own, so nobody's other marks change.
A synthetic's wounds say "heals in N days, M once mended": mended in the workshop (below), they heal in M days.
Illness when hungry (step 8): how much likelier an illness that's worse when hungry is caught on half and reduced
rations; a kettle that ran dry for some of yesterday's sittings counts as that share of the way to half rations, and
a ship out of food as half rations.

A wound that bleeds lowers its owner's blood every hour until it's dressed. Someone dresses a wound that isn't serious
themselves after the time it says; a serious one waits for someone else: for the player, a medkit, which dresses or
splints their worst wound (its effect line says dress 1), and later the medbay. A wound that can go bad goes bad if
it's still undressed when the infected line's days are up, and the infected line's "kills after 5 days undressed" is
how long a wound gone bad can be left undressed before it kills, where there's a medbay. A wound heals in its days, faster once dressed, and a
species' Body part can make it slower; one that only heals once treated takes the left alone times as long untreated.
One step harder on a check is 2 off the roll.

a wound from a hit: how much of someone's health one hit has to take to leave a wound. a hard wound: how much it has
to take to leave a hard one, and ", or a hard hit" if a hard hit (a critical) always does. A hit that takes less
leaves nothing, though it still lands somewhere, and the fight says where.

Weapons: which kind of hit a weapon makes, by the words in its name, the first line that has one: a Plasma Cutter
burns, a Concord Sidearm shoots. The line that says anything else is for everything the other lines don't name, bare
hands included. The kinds are yours to name.

Hits: the wound each kind of hit leaves. After the colon, the wound a hit leaves (or nothing); after hard:, the hard
wound, as a list of wounds each with "on" and where it can be, the first that fits the part the hit landed on; after
down:, the wound the hit that puts them down leaves, if it fits, instead. "anything" is any kind of hit, and "for"
and a species is a line for that species only, which comes before the others. A wound has to be able to be on the part
the hit landed on, or the next one is tried, and a hit that puts someone down always leaves at least the light one.
Where each hit lands is at random, by the hit line in the species' Body part: nobody aims.

rough work: words in a job that make old marks come twice as often. A word counts when a job has a word starting
with it, so engine counts for Engineer and Engine Hand.

Each wound or mark is one line: its name, a comma, where it can be, a colon, what it looks like, and its rules after
semicolons.

Where it can be: any part, the head, the chest, an arm, a leg, a limb (an arm or a leg), or one of right arm, left
arm, right leg and left leg, joined with "or" if it can be more than one: the chest or a limb. Two lines can have
the same name for different parts, like broken bone on an arm and broken bone on a leg.

What it looks like is the words the game shows, as a sentence. {part} is the part it's on (the right arm) and {side}
is right or left: a bad {side} knee is "a bad left knee".

The rules:
    serious                         a badly hurt wound, shown in red
    bleeds 4 an hour                (until treated, if you like)
    heals in 4 days                 and heals in 4 days, 2 once dressed, or heals in 8 days once dressed (only once
                                    treated); splinted instead of dressed for a broken bone. For now a wound is gone
                                    when its days are up: the untreated days, or the treated ones where it only heals
                                    once treated, since nothing treats anyone yet
    never heals
    out cold for 1 to 4 hours       and gone after that many hours
    checks one step harder
    in a fight: attack -2           this much off their attack in a fight, the player's and anyone's
    can go bad                      left undressed long enough, it goes bad (see the infected line)
    the hand's no use
    can't stand on it               someone with it can't walk; for now, in a fight in the room they stay where they
                                    are, and they can't run from a fight
    limps                           someone with it walks with a limp
    comes after 2 days undressed    (for an infection)
    worse every day
    hidden                          it can't be seen by looking, only read on a printout
    artificial                      an artificial part, shown pale
    never at random                 only someone's own file, their past or a trait gives it
    likelier in: dock, engine       four times as likely for someone whose job has one of these words
    only for: synthetic             only that species has it
    not for: synthetic              that species never has it
    catches 1 in 3000 a day         an illness (step 8): this often, someone aboard a place with quarters catches it,
                                    on the register and in the crew alike; synthetics never do
    spreads 1 in 4000 a day in the same quarters
                                    and this much likelier again for each who has it where they sleep
    worse when hungry               likelier on short rations, by the illness when hungry line
    heals in 8 days, 3 once dosed   an illness the doctor's rounds can dose
    kills 1 in 3 left untreated 5 days
                                    a serious illness left undosed that long kills one time in that many; the rounds
                                    settle it at midnight, and the player never dies of it


Accidents (in wounds.txt, before Old marks): the wounds people get at their work, each likelier in the work it names:
    - burn: likelier in: engine, tech, cook, galley
How often is in a place's file, below.

2. A PLACE'S HEALTH, LIKE MERIDIAN.TXT
--------------------------------------

    # CFS Meridian
    draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
    the medbay: Medbay
    cots: patient-1, patient-2, patient-3, patient-4
    who answers a call: a nurse, then a spare hand
    who treats: the chief medical officer, a nurse
    medicine: the medicine cabinet, 20 doses to a crate
    bed rest: 3 days after a serious wound is treated
    the dead are laid out in: The Quiet Room
    the names go up on: The chalked names
    a death aboard costs: 3 morale, 1 for someone on the register
    fitting a limb: 1 dose, 2 hours
    the workshop: The Repair Bay
    who mends: the chief engineer, a tech
    parts from: the parts lockers, the shuttle stores
    quarters: The Overflow 5, The Hot Bunks 4, The Aft Holds 1
    the doctor's rounds: 1 dose for each serious illness, once a day
    the morning queue: 07:45 to 10:30, at queue
    accidents at work: 1 in 20 days in rough work, 1 in 100 for everyone else

    Treatments:
    - cut: 1 dose, 10 minutes
    - deep cut: 2 doses, 30 minutes
    - broken bone: 2 doses, an hour

    Mending:
    - cracked casing: 1 parts crate, an hour
    - artificial arm: 2 parts crates, 6 hours

    At the start:
    - Vik, right leg: broken bone
    - Hana, chest: deep burn

the medbay, cots and who answers a call (step 4 of the health plan): the medbay's room, its cots as spots in the
place's file in world/life (a cot that someone's day already has them on, like Vik's, stays theirs), and who goes out
to someone down, in order, jobs from the place's watch bill in world/command (a spare hand is anyone the watch bill has
spare). Whoever's down there is carried to a free cot, or the medbay's floor when they're all taken, by the first who's
awake, free and has a hand to carry with, and they stay until they can get up and aren't bleeding. A place without
these lines has no medbay: someone down there comes round where they fell once they can get up.

who treats, medicine, bed rest and Treatments (step 5 of the health plan): who treats the patients, posts and jobs from
the place's command file (the chief medical officer, a nurse), and only while they're in the medbay by their day and
not out on a call; the store in world/logistics whose crates hold the medicine, and how many doses a crate holds; how
many days someone with a serious wound stays on their cot after it's treated; and under Treatments, one line for each
wound the medbay treats, by its name in the wounds file, with the doses and the time it takes someone with a Medicine
of 5 (a better medic is quicker, a worse one slower, as the command file's skill line says). A wound with no line here
isn't treated in the medbay. The medics treat the laid up first, the worst off first, then the morning queue, then the
player if they're standing in the medbay. Without who treats, the medicine or any Treatments, nobody is treated there.

the dead are laid out in, the names go up on and a death aboard costs (step 6 of the health plan): the room whoever
answers a call carries the dead to (they're gone once they're there; without this line they're gone at once), the thing
in one of the place's rooms whose Look and Use list the dead (by its name on the map), and how much morale a death
there costs the ship, and after a comma how much a death on the register costs (the same, if it doesn't say). Someone dies at a place with a medbay when their blood runs out (bleeding to death, in the wounds
file) or a wound gone bad is left undressed too long (kills after, on the infected line). The player never dies, for now.

the morning queue: when anyone there who's hurt and on their feet goes to wait at the medbay, and where: a spot or a
group of spots in the place's file in world/life (queue is the medbay's). Anyone asleep or laid up stays put.

fitting a limb, the workshop, who mends, parts from and Mending (step 7 of the health plan): how many doses and how
long the medbay's medics take to fit an artificial limb that's ready; the room the techs mend and build in; who mends,
posts and jobs from the command file, who walk there when there's work; the stores in world/logistics whose parts crates
it uses; and under Mending, one line for each of a synthetic's wounds the workshop mends, by its name in the wounds file,
and for an artificial arm and an artificial leg, with the parts crates and the time each takes someone with an
Engineering of 5. A synthetic who can't get up is carried to the workshop's bench, and one on their feet goes there in
the morning queue's hours. A limb is built for anyone who's lost one and had the stump dressed, then fitted in the
medbay. A place without these lines builds no limbs, and its synthetics heal by themselves.

accidents at work: how often someone there hurts themselves at their work, rough work being the wounds file's. The
wound is one of the wounds file's Accidents, likelier the ones for their work, on a part at random.

quarters and the doctor's rounds (step 8 of the health plan): a place with a quarters line has a register: everyone
aboard who has no day of their own in the place's file in world/life, made up when a game begins (a name, an age, a
species and the quarters they sleep in, the rooms here with how many of every ten). Only who's sick and who's dead is
kept in the save. The wounds file's illnesses go round them, more where they sleep together, and the people with days
and the player catch them too, as wounds. Once a day, at midnight, the doctor's rounds give each serious illness this
many doses, on the register and off it, from a crate the rounds take from the medicine's store for themselves; a dosed
illness heals in its dosed days, and one left undosed long enough may kill (its kills line). An illness isn't one of the
Treatments: the rounds see to it, not the medbay's table. The ship's health settles a point lower for every hundredth
of the register sick, three for every hundredth seriously sick, and when it's low enough that people die of it, the
dead are people on the register, the worst off and the oldest first. Everyone is listed on any computer with a
register log (world/computers), like the records terminal in the Meridian's medbay. A place without a quarters line
has no register.

Each line under At the start is someone at that place, a comma, and a body line (below). It's for the people with only a name, who have
no file of their own; someone with a file can be named here too, by their id or their name. A line that names one of
the wounds file's wounds is a wound they start the game with, which heals like any other; someone whose wound is
serious starts the game laid up on a cot in the medbay, if the place has one. Any other line is an old mark that's
always part of their body.


3. A SPECIES' BODY PART
-----------------------

    Body:
    hit: head 10, chest 34, right arm 14, left arm 14, right leg 14, left leg 14
    bones break: 0.5            (fractions of the usual: 0.5 is half as often)
    wounds heal: 0.75           (three quarters the pace)
    bleeds: no
    own marks only: yes         (they only get the old marks that say only for: them)
    mended by: the techs        (or the nurses, which is the usual)

hit is how often a hit lands on each part, out of the total. Leave out any line that's as usual.


4. A PERSON'S BODY PART
-----------------------

    Body:
    - left arm: two fingers gone from the left hand
    - a leg: a bad limp, and a stick he won't lean on in front of people; limps
    - nothing else

Each line is a body line: the part, a colon and what's there, with rules after semicolons if you like. The part can
be any of the where words above, and then the game picks one for each game (a leg is the right leg in one game and
the left in another). If what's there is the name of a wound or mark in wounds.txt, like broken bone, it's that, with
its words and rules; anything else is shown as you wrote it, as an old mark. "nothing else" stops the game making up
more marks for them. Leave the part out and the game makes up their whole body.

The player's own body comes from their life story: the injuries in it (src/life-data.js), their traits (One-Eyed,
Old Wound, the cybernetic arm and eye) and the wounds they have now.


5. THE STORY BOARD AND THE WRITTEN STORY
----------------------------------------

The Story board's Health page shows this file and each place's file as boxes: a box for each line at the top,
one for each wound, old mark, weapon, hit, accident, treatment, kind of mending and person hurt at the start,
with arrows from a weapon to the hits it makes and from a hit or an accident to the wounds it leaves. Click a
box to change its line; the board won't keep a line the game can't read, and says how it's written. A
species' board has a Their bodies box for its Body part, and a person's board a Body box for theirs.

A written scene can ask how someone is (? hurt tarrow, ? badly hurt, ? ill, ? can walk, ? can't walk,
? laid up, ? dead) and can wound, heal or kill them (* wound tarrow deep cut on the left arm: reason,
* heal tarrow: reason, * dies tarrow: of her wounds). The wound is one of this file's, by its name, on a part
it can be on. world/scenes/_how-to-write-a-scene.txt has the rest.
