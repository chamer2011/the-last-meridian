draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
old marks: one every 20 years after 20, at most 3
a wound from a hit: 20% of their health
a hard wound: 40% of their health, or a hard hit
blood comes back: 2 an hour, once nothing bleeds
low on blood: under 60, checks one step harder
bleeding to death: at 0 blood
bleeding stops at: 35, for you and away from a medbay, for now
they dress their own: wounds that aren't serious, after an hour
left alone: twice as long, for a wound that heals once treated
with a limp: 60% of the pace
on a leg they can't stand on: 30% of the pace
too weak to stand: under 40 blood
beaten in a fight: down for 1 to 3 hours
pain: 2 stress a day for a wound, 5 for a serious one
losing a limb to a hit: 1 time in 4, a hard hit on a limb that's already broken
losing a limb to rot: the doctor takes it after 3 days gone bad
artificial limbs from before: 1 in 60 have one, 1 in 25 in rough work
illness when hungry: twice as likely on half rations, half again as likely on reduced
without air: out cold after 2 to 3 hours of bad air, dead 1 to 2 hours after that
back in good air: comes round after 10 minutes, and gets their breath back twice as fast as they lost it
