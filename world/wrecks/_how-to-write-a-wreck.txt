How to write a wreck
====================

A wreck is a dead ship the player can take the shuttle out to, for the parts a rig is built from. Each file here is
one wreck, named by its file. The player finds it under You can, anywhere at the place the file names: a run out takes
an air bottle from their pack, and the bottle's air runs down with the clock whatever they're doing (pausing with
Space stops it, like everything). The Harrow, the ship's own salvage run in world/salvage.txt, is something else and
works as it did.

The first line is the wreck's name, after a #:

    # The Merrow

Then these lines:

    at: graveyard                 the place it's reached from, by its id in world/places
    order: 1                      where it comes in the list under You can
    it was: an ore freighter      a few words, shown under the button
    in a line: ...                a few words about it
    the shuttle: 20 minutes each way
                                  the trip out and back, which doesn't count against the air
    a bottle of air: 60 minutes   how long one bottle lasts out there
    out of air: stress 15         what being hauled out with an empty bottle costs

Then a line that says Jobs:, and under it one line for each job, in the order they're listed:

    lockers: Empty the crew lockers, 15 minutes, Engineering 1: standard_cell, memory_2, 2 scrap

    - first its id, then the button's words, the minutes it takes, and the Engineering it needs;
    - ", behind the hatch" before the colon means the job called hatch has to be done first;
    - after the colon, what comes out: rig parts by their file names in world/rig-parts, and items by theirs in
      world/items, with a number in front for more than one (2 scrap), or "nothing" for a job that only opens the way.

Rig parts come out worn if the player's Engineering is at least the job's, and broken if it isn't. Scrap and anything
else come out as they are. A job done stays done, and what's taken stays taken: once every job that gives something is
done, the wreck is stripped bare.

What's carried goes home when the player goes back to the shuttle. If the air runs out first, the shuttle's crew haul
them out, and what they were carrying is left by the airlock, to be picked up on their next run there.

After changing a file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.
