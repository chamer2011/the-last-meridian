HOW TO WRITE AN ITEM
====================

Everything the player can carry, buy or be given is one file in this folder, named by its id: service_rifle.txt is
the Service Rifle, and places name what they sell by that id. The Story board in the game (The story, then Story
board, then Items) shows and changes the same files, and npm run check-story reads them all and says what's wrong,
with the file and the line.

    # Service Rifle
    draft: Claude's, not yet edited by the owner   (on the things Claude wrote for you; take it out once it's yours)
    order: 23                           (where it comes in lists; leave it out to come after the rest, by name)
    type: weapon                        (weapon, armor, consumable, gadget, goods or story)
    price: 250
    size: 4x1                           (squares it takes in the inventory, across by down; leave it out for its kind's)
    weight: 4.5                         (in kg; leave it out for its kind's)
    stack: 10                           (how many go on one square; leave it out for its kind's, 1 for most)
    damage: 5 to 9                      (weapons)
    stat: perception                    (weapons: the attribute that adds to the attack)
    skill: firearms                     (weapons: the skill that adds to the attack)
    defense: 2                          (armor)
    restricted: yes                     (not for sale to just anyone; leave it out if not)
    effect: hp 10, energy 5             (consumables: what using it does; hp, energy, stress, dress, hull or fuel)
    found in: armory, crew locker       (the kinds of container it turns up in, from world/loot/containers.txt)
    rarity: uncommon                    (common, uncommon or rare: how often it turns up there)
    keepsake: yes                       (someone's own: whoever it was taken from asks after it; leave it out if not)
    salvage: 3                          (what it counts for toward a parts crate at the bench; leave it out if nothing)
    feeds: 1                            (food: the sittings it makes at the kettle, once it's in the pantry)
    doses: 4                            (medicine: the doses it gives the medbay, once it's in the medicine cabinet)
    spares: 1                           (parts: the repairs it's good for, in the parts lockers or the shuttle stores)

    In a line:
    The old navy's standard long gun, easy to find now that so few are left to carry one.

    When read:
    A name is scratched into the stock, and under it a date.   (words on it, which Read in the inventory shows)

    Effects:
    in a setting: vacuum 1              (while it's worn or carried: easier or harder in a setting)
    skills: hacking 1                   (a bonus or penalty to a skill)

    Drawing:
    `==[##]======-`                     (its little picture in the inventory, one row to a line, between backticks)

In a line is what the player reads in the inventory and the market. A part that has nothing in it can be left out.

The kinds: a weapon goes in the hand or on the hip, armor is worn (clothes too, with defense 0), a consumable is used up
(and can go on the belt to use in a fight if it heals or gives energy), a gadget helps by being carried, goods are
things to keep, sell, read or give back that do nothing by being carried (a letter, a keepsake, a burnt-out relay), and
a story item is one the story hands out, which can't be put down if it has no price.

What a consumable's effect does: hp gives health back (plus the player's Medicine rank), energy gives energy, stress
takes that much stress off, dress dresses or splints that many of the player's wounds, hull mends the ship's hull (plus
twice the Engineering rank) and fuel puts fuel in her tanks.

When read is what's written on it: a letter, a page, a log, a label. The inventory offers Read for anything that has
it, carried or in something the player has opened, shows the words, and keeps them in What you know under the item's
name. Keep it to a few sentences on one line, and don't end it with a colon, or it reads as the start of a new part.

Found in and rarity are for the containers in the rooms (a dresser, the armory's rack, a crate): world/loot explains
them. An item without a found in line is never found in a container, and one with a found in line isn't for sale in the
character creator unless a market sells it too. The Drawing is how the inventory draws it, in plain characters, one row
to a line, each row between backticks so its spaces are kept; without one it's drawn as its kind. Each square holds
about 4 characters across and 2 rows down (a 2x1 thing about 8 across, a 2x3 thing about 8 across and 7 down).

Keepsake and salvage are for using what's found. A keepsake (a photograph, a ring, a harmonica) taken aboard the
Meridian from a container that's someone's in particular is missed: some hours later its owner asks after it, in
world/scenes/request_keepsake.txt (the scene how-to says how, under PEOPLE'S TROUBLES). Salvage is turned into parts
crates at the bench: each piece counts for its salvage number, and the ship's logistics file says how much a crate
takes, where and how long (its salvage makes line). The drafts give salvage a quarter of the price, at least 1.

Feeds, doses and spares are what a thing is worth to the ship, and an item has one of them at most. Whatever uses
that kind of goods takes anything worth some of it: the galley cooks any food in the pantry (a ration crate or a
basket of produce feeds 200, a tin feeds 1), the medbay doses from any medicine in its cabinet (a medicine crate is
20 doses, a medkit 4), and a repair takes any spare (a parts crate is 1). The Ship menu counts it all for its share
of a crate, and so do the asks for food, medicine or parts. Leave the line out for anything nobody would cook, dose
with or fit: drink, sweets and tobacco feed nobody. The numbers on the loot's foods and medicines are drafts.

Size, weight and stack are for the inventory. A thing one square big goes in a pocket; anything bigger goes in the
pack, which is 8 squares by 5, so nothing can be bigger than that, and a weapon of 2 squares or fewer can go on the hip.
Each kind's usual size, weight and stack, and how much the player can carry, are in world/inventory.json.
