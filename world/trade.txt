# What the Meridian's captain can do with a dock, beside buying by the lot from the Ship menu. Every number here is a
# first guess.
#
# The purse is the ship's own money, apart from yours. It begins the first time you use any of this, and a levy comes
# in every day after that while you're the captain.
#
# Restocking is a switch you throw in the Lighter Bay (under You can). While it's on, nothing comes into the shuttle
# stores from nowhere any more: at a dock, every so many hours, the missing ration crates and fuel cells are bought
# from the port out of the purse, at the port's own price that day, and the port has that much less. In open space,
# or at a port that doesn't sell it, or with an empty purse, nothing comes. While it's off, the stores fill as before.
#
# Cargo and passengers are offered at a port while the ship is docked there and you're ashore: a few crates, or a few
# people, for another port on your chart. The fee goes into the purse when the ship gets there; cargo delivered late
# pays half. Passengers eat at the sittings on the way.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.

the purse: 600 to begin with, 60 a day
restocking: every 6 hours at a dock
a ration crate: 2 times the port's food price
a fuel cell: 0.5 times the port's fuel price
cargo: 2 offers a port, 4 to 10 crates, 15 credits a crate, 10 days to deliver, 20 crates in the hold
passengers: 4 to 10 people, 10 credits each
