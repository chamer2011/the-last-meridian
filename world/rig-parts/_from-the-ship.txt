# Taking the ship's own things apart, for your rig
draft: mine, not yet edited by the owner
#
# Now and then a part the rig wants is already aboard, in one of the ship's own systems (world/systems). As her captain,
# it's all yours to take: a part of the kinds below shows "Take it apart for your rig" in its window and its
# right-click menu. What comes out is worn. The part is broken from then on, by the systems' own rule, until the
# engineers mend it with a parts crate from the stores: a junction box's rooms go dark, a camera sees nothing, the relay
# takes the comms down, and the nav core leaves the jumps to be plotted by hand. If someone sees you do it (someone
# awake in the room, or a camera that works), whoever holds the post it belongs to hears of it, and remembers.
#
# A line: a kind of part (as world/systems/_defaults.json names them), what comes out of one (rig parts by their file
# names, items by theirs, a number in front for more than one), the minutes it takes, the Engineering it needs, and
# the post it belongs to (as world/command/meridian.txt names it).
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.

junction box: fin_sink, 2 scrap; 25 minutes, Engineering 2; the chief engineer
relay: short_radio; 15 minutes, Engineering 1; the comms officer
camera: lens_unit; 20 minutes, Engineering 2; the chief of security
nav core: overclock_board; 30 minutes, Engineering 3; the helm officer
