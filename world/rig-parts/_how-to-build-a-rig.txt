HOW TO WRITE A RIG PART
=======================

The rig in the player's pack is built from parts: a casing round a board, and the board's sockets each holding a
part of their kind. Each board, casing and part is one plain text file in this folder, world/rig-parts/<id>.txt. The
game reads them again a moment after one changes.

    npm run check-story

checks these files too.


THE FILE
--------

    # Yard board
    kind: board
    order: 1
    grade: 1
    weight: 0.3
    sockets: core at 1 1, sink at 2 1, memory at 3 1, link at 1 2, cell at 2 2, memory at 3 2
    where from: Comes with the rig.

    What it is:
    The shipyard's own. Six sockets on a grid.

The first line is its name. The lines under it can come in any order, and any number that isn't there is 0:

    kind          board, casing, core, memory, link, cell, sink or extra.
    order         Where it comes in the lists, smallest first.
    grade         How good it is, 1 and up. Mending it at the bench takes that many hours and that much
                  Engineering, and one of the ship's parts crates.
    weight        In kilograms. The rig weighs its board, casing and every part with it.
    worth         What it's worth in credits, which the ports' dealers (world/dealers) go by. Left out, it's
                  35 for each grade.
    heat          How much heat it makes. A session adds a point every ten minutes on top.
    power         Added to a program's power against a computer's class (1 a door panel to 5 the Beacon).
    quiet         Takes noise off what the rig does: 1 takes one off anything making 2 or more, 2 takes one off
                  everything, 3 makes it silent. Below 0 it adds one.
    speed         Added to the rig's speed, which starts at 1: minutes are divided by it.
    memory        Blocks of memory for programs.
    cools         A heat sink's: the heat it takes off each socket beside it (left, right, above, below).
    cooling       A casing's: the heat it takes off the whole rig.
    echo          An extra's: noise CLEANER takes off on top of its own.
    sockets       A board's: each socket's kind and where it is on the grid, column then row, from 1. A socket
                  can open only from a level of Hacking: extra at 5 1 from Hacking 7. A board needs a core, a
                  cell and a link socket.
    reaches       A link's: what it reaches a computer by, one of cable, clip (a service port), optic (an optical
                  port), radio or navy (a Navy coupling). Which a computer takes comes with the computers that
                  need one; until then any link that works starts the rig.
    made from     What making it at the bench takes, if it can be made: what goes into it first (scrap, anything
                  in world/items by its name, or a parts crate from the ship's stores, joined by "and"), then
                  the Engineering, the hours, and a tool for the bench if it needs one, from _tools.txt:
                      made from: 2 scrap, Engineering 1, 2 hours
                      made from: a fuel cell and a parts crate, Engineering 4, 3 hours, with the cell press
    where from    Where it comes from, in a few words, for the Rig page.

    What it is: a few words for the Rig page.


HOW A BUILD ADDS UP
-------------------

The rig won't start without a core, a cell and a link that work. A worn part gives half and runs a point warmer; a
broken one gives nothing until it's mended. A session warms the rig a point every ten of its minutes: at 3 its fan
is heard (anything noisy is one louder), and at 5 it's overheating (everything takes twice as long, and the hottest
part wears a step when the player logs off). The bench's rules (mending, overclocking and undervolting a core, the
lathe, taking apart) are in the game's code, src/rig-parts.js, with my first numbers. The lathe needs the micro lathe
on the bench.


THE OTHER FILES HERE
--------------------

    _tools.txt          The tools that can be bought for the bench, at Tessen Colony's workshop.
    _from-the-ship.txt  The kinds of the ship's own parts the captain can take apart for the rig, and what comes out.
    _finds.txt          Things that turn up aboard for the rig, and where.

Each explains itself in its own first lines. The wrecks the parts come from are in world/wrecks, and the dealers who
buy and sell them in world/dealers.
