# CFS Meridian
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
everyone aboard eats: 2 sittings a day
a crate feeds: 200 sittings

Stores:
- shuttle stores in The Lighter Bay: holds 20 ration crates, 12 fuel cells, 6 parts crates: Crates strapped down where the lighter unloads them, with the manifest on a clip.
- fuel rack in Engineering: holds 6 fuel cells: A rack of cells by the reactor, each one tagged with the day it came aboard.
- pantry in The Galley: holds 12 ration crates, 12 produce: Shelves behind a wire door, and the smell of whatever's nearest to going off.
- garden bins in The Garden: holds 12 produce: Bins at the end of the beds, with today's picking in them.
- medicine cabinet in Medbay: holds 5 medicine crates: A locked cabinet with a list on its door of what's left.
- parts lockers in Stores: holds 6 parts crates: Lockers of spares, sorted by someone who's since given up.
- soup kettle in The Long Mess: holds 400 sittings

Machines:
- generator in Engineering: burns 1 fuel cell from the fuel rack every 8 hours, gives 100 power
- garden: grows 1 produce into the garden bins every 2 hours, with power and someone tending it
- galley: cooks 1 produce or 1 ration crate from the pantry into 200 sittings in the soup kettle every hour, with power and a cook there

Loads:
- air plant 20, engines 30 while under way, doors 6, comms 6, navigation 6, cameras 4, lights 2 a room, computers 2 each
- when short, drop: cameras, comms, lights, doors, navigation, air

Errands:
- Tech Oona: fuel cells from the shuttle stores to the fuel rack when under 3, two at a time
- Quartermaster Brandt: ration crates from the shuttle stores to the pantry when under 4, two at a time
- Cook Imre: produce from the garden bins to the pantry when under 4, four at a time

Refills:
- the shuttle stores: ration crates and fuel cells from nothing for now, every 6 hours, up to full
