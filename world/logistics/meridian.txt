# CFS Meridian
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
everyone aboard eats: 2 sittings a day
a crate feeds: 200 sittings
sittings at: 06:00, 07:30, 18:00, 19:30, each 90 minutes
a repair takes: 1 parts crate, from the parts lockers or the shuttle stores, for what's broken, cut, crippled or forced
salvage makes: 1 parts crate from 10 salvage, in The Repair Bay, in 2 hours, into the parts lockers
carried by: the deckhands, the spare hands
a trip carries: 2 ration crates, 2 fuel cells, 4 produce, 1 parts crate, 1 medicine crate
what comes first: the first officer, every hour
what stores want: the quartermaster, every 4 hours
left waiting: 30 minutes if it's urgent, 3 hours if it isn't
who asks: the chief medical officer for the medbay, the chief engineer for the engines and repairs, the quartermaster for the galley, the deck chief for the rest
everyone aboard drinks: 9 measures of water a day
a tank of water fills: 100 measures
with the pumps stopped: 4 times as much water goes
water is rationed under: 200 measures
a reserve tank gives: 24 hours of air
a day of reserve air takes: 4 measures of water
a breached room takes: 6 hours of reserve air
a breached room fills in: 3 hours with no reserve air
the crew uses: 20 drink, 20 tea and tobacco, 10 clothes a day, from the comforts locker
each comfort steadies morale: 2 points when things go wrong
going without one sours it: 2 points
a good table lifts morale: 6 points after a hard day

Stores:
- shuttle stores in The Lighter Bay: holds 24 ration crates, 36 fuel cells, 6 parts crates: Crates strapped down where the lighter unloads them, with the manifest on a clip.
- fuel rack in Engineering: holds 6 fuel cells; wants 3; urgent under 2; carried by the deckhands, the techs, the spare hands: A rack of cells by the reactor, each one tagged with the day it came aboard.
- pantry in The Galley: holds 12 ration crates, 12 produce; wants 4 of each; carried by the deckhands, the cooks, the quartermaster, the spare hands: Shelves behind a wire door, and the smell of whatever's nearest to going off.
- garden bins in The Garden: holds 12 produce: Bins at the end of the beds, with today's picking in them.
- medicine cabinet in Medbay: holds 5 medicine crates; wants 2; carried by the deckhands, the nurses, the spare hands: A locked cabinet with a list on its door of what's left.
- parts lockers in Stores: holds 6 parts crates; wants 2; carried by the deckhands, the techs, the spare hands: Lockers of spares, sorted by someone who's since given up.
- soup kettle in The Long Mess: holds 400 sittings
- water tank in The Pump Room: holds 800 measures of water: The ship's water, behind the pumps, with a sight glass somebody has to wipe before they can read it.
- reserve tanks in Life Support: holds the reserve air: A rack of grey bottles along the hull, each as tall as a person, with a gauge at the neck and a date stencilled on the shoulder.
- comforts locker in The Long Mess: holds 4 kegs of beer and spirits, 4 chests of tea and tobacco, 2 bales of clothes, 3 hampers of provisions, 48 ship-brewed beer, 24 crawlway moonshine: A steel locker by the serving line with the quartermaster's seal on its hasp, and a tally chalked inside the door.
- freight hold in The Aft Holds: holds freight: Tie-down rings and netting, and a slate by the hatch with what's whose chalked on it.

Machines:
- generator in Engineering: burns 1 fuel cell from the fuel rack every 8 hours, gives 100 power
- garden: grows 1 produce into the garden bins every 2 hours, with power, someone tending it and 1 water
- galley: cooks 1 produce or 1 ration crate or any food from the pantry into 200 sittings in the soup kettle every hour, with power, a cook there and 1 water
- air plant in Engineering: takes 1 water from the water tank every 3 hours, with power
- beer bucket in The Galley: brews 1 produce from the garden bins into 48 ship-brewed beer in the comforts locker every 480 hours, with a cook there and 1 water
- still in The Crawlways: distils 1 produce from the garden bins into 24 crawlway moonshine in the comforts locker every 240 hours, with someone there and 1 water

Loads:
- air plant 20, engines 30 while under way, doors 6, comms 6, navigation 6, cameras 4, lights 1 a room, computers 2 each
- when short, drop: cameras, comms, lights, doors, navigation, air

Refills:
- the shuttle stores: ration crates and fuel cells from the dock, every 6 hours, up to full
- the water tank: water from the dock, every 6 hours, up to full

Urgency:
- carrying someone who's bleeding
- a fire
- a breach
- carrying someone who's down
- the alarm
- urgent carrying
- a repair the ship can't do without
- treating someone
- carrying the dead
- carrying
- a repair
- mending in the workshop
