How to write a dealer in rig parts
==================================

A dealer buys and sells the parts a rig is built from, at a port. Each file here is one dealer, named by its file
(haven.txt is Haven's market). The player finds them in the Market menu while they're at that port.

The first line is the dealer's name, after a #:

    # Kessara's fence

Then these lines:

    at: kessara                   the place it's at, by its id in world/places (Tessen Colony is frontier)
    in a line: ...                a few words about it, shown in the Market menu
    buys: anything                anything, broken too: a broken part fetches 5 credits
    buys: what works              only parts that still work
    buys: scrap, 4 credits each   only scrap, at that price
    markup: 1.5                   everything it sells costs this much more than it's worth (1.5 is half again)
    friends pay less: yes         a friend of whoever holds the place pays less and gets more, and every day the
                                  player sells there counts a little with them
    wants: cores, boards          the kinds it wants, one a day in turn; it pays half again for one of them
                                  (cells, memory, cores, boards, casings, links, heat sinks, extras, scrap)

Then a line that says Sells:, and under it one line for each thing it sells, with how many it has:

    memory_4: 2                   a rig part (world/rig-parts), a tool for the bench (world/rig-parts/_tools.txt),
                                  or an item (world/items), and how many it has
    scrap: 3, 15 credits, 3 at a time
                                  its own price instead of what the thing is worth, and how many come with each one
                                  bought

What things cost: a rig part its worth line, a tool its price, an item its price; times the markup. Each one bought
makes the next a fifth dearer until the port restocks: it takes in one more of everything it sells each day. The
player's way with a price (Commerce and Presence, as at the market) takes a little off what they pay and adds a little
to what they get.

What it pays for a spare part: half its worth where it buys anything, two fifths where it buys only what works; half
that for a worn one; half again for the kind it wants today.

Tools for the bench are the ship's, so a captain pays for them out of the ship's purse.

A program whose file says "sold at: kessara" is sold by the dealer at kessara, at its price line (or 30 credits for
each point of Hacking it needs), times the markup.

After changing a file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.
