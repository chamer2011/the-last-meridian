HOW TO WRITE A PLACE'S CHAIN OF COMMAND
=======================================

Who holds which post, who chooses them, and what each post answers for. It's one plain text file in this folder,
world/command/<place>.txt, named after the place (the file name of its map in world/maps). Only the Meridian has
one so far.

    npm run check-story

checks these files too.

It's built in steps, each tried by the owner before the next. The game plays the posts and who holds them now: the
Command menu shows each post as a card, and everything in the other files that names someone for a post (the
council's rations decisions in world/ship.json, the guards and engineers in world/systems, the carriers of the
errands in world/logistics, and the captain's chair) asks this file who holds it. The jobs and the numbers at the
top are read, kept and checked, and come to life in the later steps.


THE FILE
--------

    # CFS Meridian
    draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner      (the game doesn't read this: it's for whoever edits)
    reviews every: 7 days                            (kept for a later step: how often the officers review)
    a bad stretch: duty under 40 for 5 days          (kept for a later step: when someone's replaced)
    mutiny brews: morale under 30, loyalty under 35  (kept for a later step: when trouble starts)

    Posts:
    - captain on The Bridge: chosen by the council; skills leadership, tactics
    - first officer on The Bridge: chosen by the captain; keeps the watch bill; skills leadership, composure
    - quartermaster in The Long Mess: chosen by the captain; heads cooks, mess hands, gardeners; skills commerce

    Jobs:
    - cook in The Galley: 2 wanted; skills survival

    Who holds what:
    - captain: the chair
    - first officer: voss
    - quartermaster: odessa

The first line is the place's name. The lines after it, before the first part, are the numbers at the top. Then
come the parts, each a heading with a colon and lines that start with - and a space.


POSTS
-----

Each line is the post's name, in or on, the room it's kept in, a colon, and then what's said about it, each part
after a semicolon:

    chosen by the captain          who chooses whoever holds it: another post, or the council, or the
                                   civilians. Every post has one. Nobody chooses themselves, and following who
                                   chooses whom has to end at the council or the civilians.
    keeps the watch bill           the post that gives everyone their job (played from a later step)
    heads cooks, mess hands        the jobs it answers for, by their names in the Jobs part
    skills commerce, persuasion    the skills that count for it: the Command menu shows the holder's numbers
                                   in them, from their file in world/characters
    duty: sittings missed          what its duty will be measured by (played from a later step)

The room is one of the place's rooms, by its name in the maps. The Command menu groups the posts by who chooses
them, in the file's order.


JOBS
----

Each line is the job, in or on, the room it's done in, a colon, how many it wants, and its skills:

    - cook in The Galley: 2 wanted; skills survival

They're read and checked now, and the watch bill gives people these jobs from the next step.


WHO HOLDS WHAT
--------------

Each line is a post, a colon, and who holds it when a game begins: someone with a day at the place in
world/life, by their id (odessa), their name (Odessa Brandt, or Brandt) or their crowd name (Tech Sami). One post
each. The captain's post can be the chair: the player, if they started as the captain, and otherwise whoever the
game gave the chair, from the chair's list in world/ship.json. A post that nobody holds is a problem the checker
names.

A game keeps who holds what in its save from the moment it begins, so a change here reaches new games, and the
games under way keep theirs.


NAMING A POST ELSEWHERE
-----------------------

The other files can name a post instead of a person, as the and the post's name, and whoever holds it is the one:

    world/ship.json, a council decision:     "post": "quartermaster"
    world/systems/<place>.txt:               guards: the chief of security
                                             engineers: the chief engineer, Tech Sami, Tech Oona
    world/logistics/<place>.txt, an errand:  - the quartermaster: ration crates from the shuttle stores to ...

A council decision says {who} as the post and the holder's name (Quartermaster Brandt), and the council does it
itself if nobody holds the post, or whoever does has been lost.
