# what the game knows right now. The questions are under Questions, and what they say is under Answers: several lines
# with the same name are picked between, so nobody sounds like the next person. The words in {braces} are filled in
# by the game; a line can leave them out, but can't use one its name doesn't have.
#
#   job          {job} {a job} {room} {keeper}     someone on the ship's watch bill ("job spare": a spare hand)
#   going ...    {n} {sittings} {keeper}           how their own work stands, by job: short (people missing), cook,
#                                                  cook empty, tech, tech broken, tech waiting, nurse, nurse waiting,
#                                                  guard, guard alarm, and plain "going" for everyone else
#   where        {who} {where}                     where one of the officers is, or whoever a quest of yours waits on
#   where gone   {who}                             when they can't say ("where who" is what they ask back first)
#   news ...     {what} {who} {n} {illness}        the talk: mutiny, hungry, short (of a supply), dead, sick, low
#                                                  (morale), trouble (at a port), and plain "news" on a quiet day
#   self ...     {wounds} {illness}                how they are: hurt, ill, hungry, and plain "self"
#
# Under Come with me are the things said when you ask someone to walk with you, or to go to the medbay: yes, done
# (when you say that's all, or the two hours are up), and the reasons for no. {name} is their name.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.
