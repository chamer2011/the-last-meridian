# A bare pantry
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-10, not yet edited by the owner. A cook asks anyone who lives aboard when the pantry has
// nothing left to cook and the shuttle stores in the lighter bay still have ration crates. The quartermaster usually
// carries them, so this is for when nobody has. It's done when the player has carried two crates into the pantry
// themselves (open the shuttle stores, take them, open the pantry, put them in), and gone if someone else does first.
// The captain can leave it to the quartermaster instead, whose duty shows whether the pantry was filled in time.
when: stock ration_crate < 1 in the pantry and stock produce < 1 in the pantry and stock ration_crate > 1 in the shuttle stores
asks: a cook
needs: ration crates in the pantry +2
by: 1 day
gone when: stock ration_crate >= 2 in the pantry
handed to: the quartermaster

== ask
@{asker}: "There's nothing in the pantry. Not a crate. The shuttle stores down in the lighter bay are full of them, and nobody's brought any up. Could you carry a couple? They're heavy."
> I'll bring two up -> yes
> Have the quartermaster see to it -> handed ? captain
> Not now -> later
> Not my job -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Two will do the next sittings. Thank you."

== handed
@{asker}: "I'll tell {quartermaster} you said so."

== later
@{asker}: "Soon, or the next sitting gets water."

== no
@{asker}: "Then they'll eat what's left in the kettle, and there's not much."

== done
@{asker}: "That's the next sittings fed. Put them by the wall, there."
* xp 10: A bare pantry

== failed
@{asker}: "The kettle's been empty since this morning. People went back to work hungry."

== gone
@{asker}: "Someone brought crates up. Never mind."
