# Hurt in the trouble
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. While there's trouble at a port (a strike turned
// into a fight, a raid, a feud: the events marked trouble in world/daily.json), someone there with a name asks anyone
// who comes through for a medkit for someone hurt in it. Given, not sold: what they get back is standing with whoever
// holds the place. {place} is the port, {port} whoever holds it.
for: anyone
when: trouble here
asks: someone here
needs: give 1 medkit to {asker}
by: 1 day
gone when: not trouble at {place}

== ask
@{asker}: "You'll have heard what's going on on {place}. Someone I know got caught in it, and there's nothing to patch them up with. A medkit, if you've one to spare?"
> Here, take mine -> given ? has medkit
> I'll find one -> yes
> I can't -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Quick as you can."

== given
@{asker}: "Thank you. I'll take it to them now."

== no
@{asker}: "No. Nobody has, these days."

== done
@{asker}: "They're patched up. People here will hear who did that."
* faction {port} 5: You gave a medkit for someone hurt in the trouble on {place}
* xp 10: Hurt in the trouble

== failed
@{asker}: "It was too late by then."

== gone
@{asker}: "It's quieter now, and they've been seen to. Thank you for meaning to."
