# A medkit for the ward
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-10, not yet edited by the owner. A nurse asks anyone who lives aboard and carries a
// medkit, when the medicine cabinet is empty: it would tide a patient over until crates come. The player can hand it
// over there and then, or say they'll bring one and give it later (Give, on the nurse's right-click menu).
when: stock medicine_crate < 1 and has medkit
asks: a nurse
needs: give 1 medkit to {asker}
by: 1 day
gone when: stock medicine_crate >= 1

== ask
@{asker}: "The cabinet's empty, and there's someone on a cot I've nothing to give. You carry a medkit, don't you? It would hold them till the crates come."
> Here, take mine -> given
> I'll bring you one -> yes
> Not now -> later
> I need it myself -> no

== given
@{asker}: "Thank you. I'll put it to use straight away."

== yes
@{asker}: "Quickly, if you can."

== later
@{asker}: "Not too much later."

== no
@{asker}: "Fair enough. It's yours."

== done
@{asker}: "That'll hold them. Thank you."
* xp 10: A medkit for the ward
* remember {asker} you 6: You gave up a medkit for a patient when the cabinet was empty

== failed
@{asker}: "They had a bad night. I asked you for that medkit."
* remember {asker} you -4: You said you'd bring a medkit, and didn't

== gone
@{asker}: "Crates came in. Keep your medkit."
