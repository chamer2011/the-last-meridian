# The prisoners
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-12, not yet edited by the owner. After a battle aboard, the chief of security asks
// the captain what's to be done with the prisoners in the brig, a number without names. Each answer settles it its own
// way: kept locked up; put to work under guard by day (a little morale and the council's confidence at once); or put
// ashore at the next port with a market the Meridian docks at, when they go off the ship and the brig is empty of them.
for: the captain
when: aftermath prisoners
asks: the chief of security
needs: your word
by: 2 days
gone when: aftermath prisoners done

== ask
@{asker}: "Captain. The brig's full of them, and the medbay sends me more as they're fit to stand. Two cells and a chair weren't built for this. What do you want done with them?"
> Keep them locked up -> yes_locked
> Put them to work under guard -> yes_work
> Put them ashore at the next port -> yes_ashore
> Let me think about it -> later

== yes_locked
@{asker}: "Locked up it is. They'll eat and they'll sit, and my people will stand over them."
* prisoners locked: The captain had the mutineers kept locked up

== yes_work
@{asker}: "Under guard, and the first one who tries anything goes back in a cell and stays there."
* prisoners work: The captain had the mutineers put to work under guard

== yes_ashore
@{asker}: "The next port with a market, then. They'll be somebody else's trouble."
* prisoners ashore: The captain had the mutineers put ashore at the next port

== later
@{asker}: "They're not going anywhere. Neither are my guards, while they're in there."

== done
@{asker}: "That's settled, then."
* xp 10: The prisoners

== failed

== gone
