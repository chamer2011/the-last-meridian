# Bodge it till the crates come
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. For crew aboard the Meridian who work as a tech
// (the engine hand begins as one): when something has broken and there's no parts crate to mend it with, the chief
// engineer orders them to bodge it by hand, which takes no crate: the part's own fix, in its window or on its right-click
// menu. It's an order, so refusing it costs more than a plain no. {part} is what's broken and {room} where it is.
for: crew
as: an order
when: repairs waiting and job you tech
asks: your officer
needs: fix a part waiting for repairs
by: 1 day

== ask
@{asker}: "The {part} in {room} is down, and there isn't a crate aboard to mend it properly. Get in there and bodge it. Tape, wire, whatever holds."
> On my way -> yes
> That's not my job -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Don't make it pretty. Make it work."

== no
@{asker}: "It's exactly your job. I'll remember you said that."

== done
@{asker}: "It's holding. Ugly, but holding. Good."
* xp 15: Bodge it till the crates come

== failed
@{asker}: "The {part}'s still down. I gave you a day."

== gone
@{asker}: "Someone's seen to the {part}. Never mind."
