# A job left waiting
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-16, not yet edited by the owner. When a job on the ship's work board has waited
// too long with nobody able to do it (everyone who would is laid up, called away or on work that can't be left),
// whoever that part of the ship belongs to brings it to anyone who lives aboard. The ship's logistics file says after
// how long (its "left waiting" line) and who brings what (its "who asks" line). Yes makes the job yours until it's
// due, and the crew keeps off it: carry the things into the store, mend the thing by hand, fight the fire, patch
// the hull, or do a job of the story's in its room, under You can. It's done once the job is over and you had a hand
// in it, and gone if someone got free and did it first.
// {trouble} is what's wrong, as a sentence without its full stop, and {task} what there is to do about it.
for: anyone aboard
about: a job nobody can do
asks: them
needs: see to it yourself
by: 3 hours

== ask
@{asker}: "{trouble}. Everyone I'd send is laid up, called away or on something that can't be left. Would you {task} yourself?"
> I'll do it -> yes
> Not now -> later
> It'll have to wait -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Then it's yours, and I'll keep the others off it. Thank you."

== later
@{asker}: "Soon, then. It isn't getting done by itself."

== no
@{asker}: "Then it waits till I've someone to send."

== done
@{asker}: "That's done, and by your own hands. I won't forget it."
* xp 10: A job left waiting

== failed
@{asker}: "It's still waiting. You said you'd see to it."

== gone
@{asker}: "Never mind. Someone got free and saw to it."
