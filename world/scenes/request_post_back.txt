# The post they lost
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. Someone relieved of a post in the last two weeks
// asks the captain for it back. A yes gives it back there and then (and {holder}, who has it now, goes back to a job,
// as when anyone's relieved); a no leaves it with {holder}, and they remember. {post} is the post they lost.
for: the captain
about: someone relieved
asks: them
needs: your word
by: 1 day

== ask
@{asker}: "Captain. You'll have heard I'm not the {post} any more. {holder} has it now. Give it back to me. I won't let it slip again."
> It's yours again -> yes
> {holder} keeps it -> no
> Let me think about it -> later

== yes
@{asker}: "You won't regret it."
* appoint {post} {asker}: You gave {asker} back the post of {post}

== no
@{asker}: "Then that's that."

== later
@{asker}: "Don't think about it too long."

== done
@{asker}: "I'll show you it was the right call."
* xp 5: The post they lost

== failed
@{asker}: "I waited for your answer, Captain."

== gone
@{asker}: "Never mind the {post}. It's been settled."
