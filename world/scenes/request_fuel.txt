# Fuel for the jumps
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-10, not yet edited by the owner. Whoever is chief engineer asks the captain when the jump fuel
// is under three: keeping station burns it whether the ship goes anywhere or not. It's done when the captain has
// bought three more at a port with a depot, and gone if the tank fills some other way.
when: captain and fuel < 3
asks: the chief engineer
needs: fuel in the tank +3
by: 5 days
gone when: fuel >= 6

== ask
@{asker}: "Captain. The tank's under three, and keeping station eats it whether we go anywhere or not. Get us to a depot and put three more in her, or we'll be sitting out here in the cold."
> I'll find fuel -> yes
> Not now -> later
> We stay where we are -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Three more in the tank and I'll stop counting. For a week."

== later
@{asker}: "Don't leave it till it's gone. I'm not pushing her by hand."

== no
@{asker}: "Then I'll start shutting the lower decks down. Tell people to find a coat."
* remember {asker} you -3: You told him to let the fuel run down

== done
@{asker}: "There's something in the tank again. I'll stop doing the sums in my sleep."
* xp 15: Fuel for the jumps
* remember {asker} you 8: You found fuel when the tank was nearly dry

== failed
@{asker}: "We're running on what's left in the lines, Captain. I told you days ago."
* remember {asker} you -6: You said you'd find fuel, and didn't

== gone
@{asker}: "Fuel's come aboard. Never mind what I said."
