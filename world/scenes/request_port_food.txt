# Short of food
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. At any port that's short of food (under 80, where
// 100 is what it usually has; world/daily.json moves the ports' supplies day by day), someone there with a name asks
// anyone who comes through to sell them three ration packs, at half again what they cost on the market here. Ration
// packs are on the market at Haven, Tessen, Bastion Nine, Cinder Reach, the Ghostlight, Maris Deep and the Sundial.
// Haven has its own, from Dockmaster Jessup. {place} is the port, {port} whoever holds it.
for: anyone
when: supply food < 80 and not at haven
asks: someone here
needs: sell 3 ration packs to {asker}, at half again the market price
by: 4 days
gone when: supply food >= 100 at {place}

== ask
@{asker}: "Food's short on {place}, and it's getting shorter. If you've ration packs, or can find some, I'll take three off you, and pay half again what they cost here."
> Here, take them -> given ? has ration_pack
> I'll find some -> yes
> Not now -> later
> I can't -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Three, then. I'll be here."

== given
@{asker}: "That's a weight off. Here's your money."

== later
@{asker}: "Don't leave it too long."

== no
@{asker}: "Then I'll ask someone else."

== done
@{asker}: "Three packs. My family will eat this week. I won't forget who brought them."
* faction {port} 3: You sold food on {place} when it was short
* xp 10: Short of food

== failed
@{asker}: "You said you'd bring them. I waited."

== gone
@{asker}: "The convoys are coming through again. Keep your packs."
