# Two more shifts
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. For anyone whose life began somewhere other than
// the Meridian, with someone to work for (the dock hand's is Dockmaster Jessup): their boss, where their work is, asks
// them to put in two more shifts in the next few days. A shift is the job's own button in What's here, under You can
// (Work a shift on the docks). It's a request, not an order: a no is remembered, but not held against them as much.
for: outsiders
asks: your boss
needs: work two shifts
by: 3 days

== ask
@{asker}: "There's more work than hands this week. Two more shifts out of you, in the next few days? I'd take it kindly."
> I'll do it -> yes
> Not now -> later
> I can't -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Good. You know where to find the work."

== later
@{asker}: "Think on it."

== no
@{asker}: "Suit yourself."

== done
@{asker}: "Two shifts. That's what I asked, and that's what I got."
* xp 10: Two more shifts

== failed
@{asker}: "You said two shifts. I'm still counting."

== gone
@{asker}: "Never mind the shifts. It's sorted."
