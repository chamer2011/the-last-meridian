// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. At any port that's short of medicine (under 80),

// market here. Medkits are on the market at Haven, Vantor Prime, Tessen, Anselm's Mercy, Bastion Nine and Lumen
// Sanctuary. {place} is the port, {port} whoever holds it.
for: anyone
when: supply medicine < 80
asks: someone here
needs: sell 1 medkit to {asker}, at half again the market price
by: 3 days
