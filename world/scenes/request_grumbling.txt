# Who's stirring it
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. While there's grumbling aboard (a mutiny's first
// stage), the first officer asks the captain to have a word with whichever officer is least loyal to them. Talking to
// them is the word; it moves their loyalty a little, and the grumbling dying down ends it. {about} is the officer.
when: captain and mutiny grumbling
about: the least loyal officer
asks: the first officer
needs: talk to {about}
by: 2 days
gone when: mutiny calm

== ask
@{asker}: "There's grumbling in the mess, Captain, and it's coming from somewhere. If I had to guess, {about}. Have a word, before it's more than words."
> I'll talk to {about} -> yes
> Let them grumble -> no
> Not now -> later

== yes
@{asker}: "Hear them out. That's half of it."

== no
@{asker}: "As you say. I'll keep my ears open."

== later
@{asker}: "Soon, Captain."

== done
@{asker}: "Whatever you said to {about}, the mess is a little quieter."
* loyalty {about} 4: The captain came and heard them out
* xp 10: Who's stirring it

== failed
@{asker}: "It's louder in the mess, not quieter."

== gone
@{asker}: "The grumbling's died down. Never mind what I said."
