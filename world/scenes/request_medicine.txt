# Medicine for the medbay
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-10, not yet edited by the owner. Whoever is chief medical officer asks the captain when the
// medicine cabinet is nearly empty, since only the captain buys for the ship. It's done when the captain has bought
// two crates from the Ship menu at a port, and gone if two more turn up some other way.
when: captain and stock medicine_crate < 2
asks: the chief medical officer
needs: medicine crates aboard +2
by: 5 days
gone when: stock medicine_crate >= 4

== ask
@{asker}: "A word, Captain. The medicine cabinet's down to its last crate, and I'm already choosing who gets it. Two more would carry us a while. Any port with a market should have some."
> I'll see to it -> yes
> Not now -> later
> We'll have to manage without -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Two crates. Thank you. I'll keep the lock off the cabinet till then."

== later
@{asker}: "Soon, then. The cabinet won't wait long."

== no
@{asker}: "Then I'll make what's left go as far as it can, and some people won't get any."

== done
@{asker}: "Two crates, and a lock on the cabinet again. That's people I won't have to turn away."
* xp 15: Medicine for the medbay

== failed
@{asker}: "There's been nothing to give since the last crate went. You said you'd see to it."

== gone
@{asker}: "Never mind the medicine. Someone found crates for the cabinet."
