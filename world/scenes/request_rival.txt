# Bad blood
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. Whoever holds a post that someone is working
// against (a rival, from the chain of command's reviews) asks anyone who lives aboard to have a word with them. Talking
// to the rival is the word. Saying no takes the rival's side, a little. {about} is the rival, {post} the post.
for: the captain
about: a rival
asks: them
needs: talk to {about}
by: 3 days

== ask
@{asker}: "{about} has been telling anyone who'll listen that I can't do this job. It's getting into the work. Would you have a word? It might come better from you."
> I'll talk to {about} -> yes
> Maybe {about} has a point -> no
> Not now -> later

== yes
@{asker}: "Thank you. Don't make a scene of it."

== no
@{asker}: "I see. Well. I know where I stand, then."
* remember {about} you 4: You wouldn't take {asker}'s side against them

== later
@{asker}: "It won't get better by itself."

== done
@{asker}: "Whatever you said to {about}, it's quieter. Thank you."
* rivalry ends {post}: You had a word with {about}
* remember {about} you -3: You took {asker}'s side
* xp 10: Bad blood

== failed
@{asker}: "{about}'s still at it. I thought you were going to have a word."

== gone
@{asker}: "{about} seems to have let it go by themselves. Never mind."
