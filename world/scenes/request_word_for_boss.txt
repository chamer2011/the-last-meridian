# A word for the boss
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. For anyone whose life began somewhere other than
// the Meridian: someone with a name where the player is (not their boss) asks them to pass a word on to their boss,
// who's easier for the player to reach. Talking to the boss is the word. {boss} is whoever the player works for there.
for: outsiders
asks: someone here
needs: talk to your boss
by: 2 days

== ask
@{asker}: "You work for {boss}, don't you? I can never get a word in. Tell them I was asking after them, and that I've something worth their while, if they've a minute."
> I'll tell {boss} -> yes
> Not now -> later
> Tell them yourself -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "I owe you one."

== later
@{asker}: "When you've a moment, then."

== no
@{asker}: "Fine. I'll keep trying."

== done
@{asker}: "{boss} came by. Whatever you said, it worked."
* xp 5: A word for the boss

== failed
@{asker}: "Nothing from {boss}. I suppose you forgot."

== gone
@{asker}: "Never mind. I caught {boss} myself."
