# Something broken
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-10, not yet edited by the owner. Whoever is chief engineer asks anyone who lives
// aboard when something has broken and there's no parts crate to mend it with. The player can bodge it by hand, which
// takes no crate: the part's own fix, in its window or on its right-click menu. The captain can leave it to the
// engineers instead, and the chief engineer's duty shows whether they managed (they need a crate to, so someone has to
// bring one aboard). {part} is what's broken and {room} where it is.
when: repairs waiting
asks: the chief engineer
needs: fix a part waiting for repairs
by: 2 days
handed to: the chief engineer

== ask
@{asker}: "The {part} in {room} has gone, and there's not a parts crate aboard to mend it with. It can be bodged by hand, if you've the knack. My techs are stretched as it is."
> I'll see to it -> yes
> Leave it to your engineers -> handed ? captain
> Not now -> later
> It'll have to wait for parts -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Good. Mind your fingers in there."

== handed
@{asker}: "Then we'll need a crate from somewhere. I'll keep looking."

== later
@{asker}: "Don't leave it long. Broken things have friends."

== no
@{asker}: "Then it stays broken till we've parts."
* remember {asker} you -2: You left the {part} broken till there were parts

== done
@{asker}: "The {part}'s working. Not how I'd have done it, but working."
* xp 20: Something broken
* remember {asker} you 6: You bodged the {part} back to working when there were no parts

== failed
@{asker}: "The {part} in {room} is still dead. I thought you were seeing to it."
* remember {asker} you -4: You said you'd fix the {part}, and didn't

== gone
@{asker}: "Never mind the {part}. A crate turned up, and it's fixed properly now."
