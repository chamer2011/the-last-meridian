# Something gone missing
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-14, not yet edited by the owner. Step 6 of the loot plan: a keepsake (an item whose
// file says keepsake: yes) taken from a container aboard that's someone's in particular is missed by them: whoever
// sleeps nearest the locker, or whoever holds the post a desk belongs to. Some hours later, while you still have it,
// they ask after it. {thing} is what's missing, {container} what it was in, and {room} where. They don't know you
// took it: they're asking whether anyone's come across it.
for: anyone aboard
about: a keepsake you took
asks: them
needs: give 1 {thing} to {asker}
by: 2 days

== ask
@{asker}: "Something's gone missing from my {container} in {room}: my {thing}. It's worth nothing to anyone else. You haven't come across it, have you?"
> Here. I came across it -> given
> I'll keep an eye out for it -> yes
> Not now -> later
> I can't help you with that -> no

== given
@{asker}: "That's it. That's really it." They turn it over in their hands for a while. "Thank you."

== yes
@{asker}: "Thank you. It matters more than it looks."

== later
@{asker}: "When you've a moment, then."

== no
@{asker}: "No. Of course. Sorry to bother you."

== done
@{asker}: "You found it. I won't forget that."

== failed
@{asker}: "I've stopped looking for it. It's gone, I suppose."

== gone
@{asker}: "It turned up again, back where it should have been. I must have looked straight past it."
