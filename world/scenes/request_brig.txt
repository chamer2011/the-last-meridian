# Days in the brig
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. Whoever's in the brig asks the captain, when the
// captain comes by, to be let out before their days are done. A yes lets them out there and then, a spare hand on the
// watch bill; a no leaves them to their days.
for: the captain
about: someone in the brig
asks: them
needs: your word
by: 1 day

== ask
@{asker}: "Captain. I've had time to think, in here. Let me out before my days are done, and I'll keep my head down. You have my word."
> Let them out -> yes
> You'll do your days -> no
> Not now -> later

== yes
@{asker}: "Thank you, Captain."
* release {asker}: The captain let them out early

== no
@{asker}: "Then I'll count the days."

== later
@{asker}: "I'm not going anywhere."

== done
@{asker}: "I meant what I said."
* xp 5: Days in the brig

== failed
@{asker}: "I asked you a simple thing, Captain."

== gone
@{asker}: "My days are done anyway."
