# A Quiet Word
// When a mutiny has been brewing against a captain who isn't the player, and the player's aboard, whoever leads it
// asks them in. {ringleader} is whoever leads it and {captain} the captain, both filled in by the game. If the officers
// relieved the player once and would have them back, it's the chair they're offered. Each ending is a * mutiny line:
// join, stay out or warn. Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-09, not yet edited by the owner.
start: start

== start
? mutiny offers the chair -> offer
{ringleader} steers you into a doorway, out of the traffic, and waits until the corridor's empty.
@{ringleader}: "The officers have had enough of {captain}. It happens in the next day or two, with you or around you. I'd rather it was with you."
> Join them -> join
> Stay out of it -> stay_out
> [! betrayal] Warn {captain} -> warn

== offer
{ringleader} steers you into a doorway, out of the traffic, and waits until the corridor's empty.
@{ringleader}: "We've had enough of {captain}, and half this ship remembers how it was with you in the chair. Say the word, and it's yours again."
> Take the chair back -> take_back
> Stay out of it -> stay_out
> [! betrayal] Warn {captain} -> warn

== join
* mutiny join: You joined the officers against the captain
@{ringleader}: "Good. Stay close to me tomorrow."
END

== take_back
* mutiny join: The officers gave you back the chair
@{ringleader}: "Then it's done. Welcome back, Captain."
END

== stay_out
* mutiny stay out: You kept out of the officers' business
@{ringleader}: "Then you didn't hear any of this."
END

== warn
* mutiny warn: You warned the captain
You find {captain} before the next watch, and tell them what you heard, and from whom.
END
