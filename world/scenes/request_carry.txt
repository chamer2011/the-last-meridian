# A hand with the carrying
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. When someone aboard has been lying where they fell
// for a quarter of an hour with nobody coming for them (every nurse and spare hand busy or asleep), a nurse asks anyone
// who lives aboard to carry them to the medbay: Carry them, on their right-click menu, then Put them down on a cot.
// {about} is who's lying there, and {room} where.
when: down any
about: someone down with nobody coming
asks: a nurse
needs: carry {about} to the medbay
by: 3 hours

== ask
@{asker}: "{about} is lying in {room}, and there's nobody free to fetch them. You've a pair of arms. Bring them here, to a cot, would you?"
> I'll bring them -> yes
> I can't -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Carefully. Pick them up, bring them here, and put them down on a cot."

== no
@{asker}: "Then I'll go myself when I can."

== done
@{asker}: "There. Put them down gently. I'll take it from here."
* xp 10: A hand with the carrying

== failed
@{asker}: "Nobody brought {about} in. They lay there all that time."

== gone
@{asker}: "{about}'s been seen to. Never mind."
