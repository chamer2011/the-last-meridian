# The Delegation
// When a mutiny has been brewing long enough (world/command/meridian.txt says how long), the officers come to the
// captain together. {ringleader} is whoever leads them and {captain} the captain, both filled in by the game. Each
// ending is a * mutiny line: give ground, calmed, arrest (offered only while the chief of security is still loyal),
// step down, crushed, taken or refused. The fight is a fight like any other, with talking, threatening, paying and
// lying in it. Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-09, not yet edited by the owner.
start: start

== start
They come together, which is the point of it. {ringleader} first, and four officers behind, standing the way people stand when they've agreed on something in a corridor an hour ago.
@{ringleader}: "Captain. We're here about how this ship is being run. All of us, so you know it isn't just me."
> Hear them out -> demands
> [! security] Have the chief of security take {ringleader} away -> arrest ? mutiny can arrest
> Tell them to get back to their posts -> refuse

== demands
@{ringleader}: "Full rations, before anyone else goes to sleep hungry. Stop moving people about like crates. And talk to us before you decide things, not after."
> Give them what they want -> give_ground
> [persuasion:hard:{ringleader}] Talk them round -> talked | refuse
> [! security] Have {ringleader} arrested -> arrest ? mutiny can arrest
> Step down, and let {ringleader} have the chair -> step_down
> [! fight] Throw them out yourself -> fight

== give_ground
* mutiny give ground: The captain heard the delegation out and gave them what they asked for
@{ringleader}: "That's all we wanted, Captain." The others file out after them, and nobody quite looks at you.
END

== talked
* mutiny calmed: The captain talked the delegation round
@{ringleader}: "All right. We'll give it a week, Captain. One week."
END

== arrest
* mutiny arrest: Arrested at the captain's word, with the delegation watching
The chief of security's people are in the doorway before {ringleader} has finished turning round. Nobody else moves.
END

== step_down
* mutiny step down: The captain stepped down rather than split the ship
You take the insignia off and put it on the table between you. {ringleader} looks at it for a long moment before picking it up.
END

== fight
FIGHT {ringleader}
  lead_in: You come round the table. {ringleader} doesn't step back.
  why: cause
  reason: They believe the ship will die under you, and they've said so in front of witnesses.
  won -> crushed
  lost -> taken
  talked -> talked

== crushed
* mutiny crushed: The captain put the delegation down
When it's over, the others are already backing out of the door. {ringleader} goes to the brig without another word.
END

== taken
* mutiny taken: The officers took the ship
* time 72 hours rest: Three days under guard in the brig
They take you down to the brig, and the door stays shut for three days. On the fourth morning it opens, and nobody salutes.
* mutiny released: Let out of the brig, as crew
END

== refuse
* mutiny refused: The captain sent the delegation away
@{ringleader}: "Then we'll do it without you, Captain." They leave the door open behind them.
END
