# An extra shift
// Draft by the AI (Claude), 2026-11-11, not yet edited by the owner. For crew aboard the Meridian: when the job the
// player does on the watch bill is short of hands (someone hurt, laid up, in the brig or dead), the officer over that
// job orders them to put in a shift today. It's an order, so refusing it costs more than a plain no, and the officer
// says so for a week. Working the shift is the job's own button in What's here, under You can.
for: crew
as: an order
when: short your job
asks: your officer
needs: work a shift
by: 1 day
gone when: not short your job

== ask
@{asker}: "We're a pair of hands short, and the work doesn't care. I need you on a shift today. That's not a question."
> Yes, I'll take it -> yes
> I can't today -> no

== yes
@{asker}: "Good. Get to it."

== no
@{asker}: "I'll remember that."

== done
@{asker}: "That's the shift covered. Thank you."
* xp 10: An extra shift

== failed
@{asker}: "The day's gone, and so was the shift. I told you we were short."

== gone
@{asker}: "We've got the hands back. You're off the hook."
