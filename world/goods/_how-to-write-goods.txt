HOW TO WRITE THE WORLD'S GOODS
==============================

Every file in this folder is a tree of goods: food, fuel, medicine, parts, water and air, comforts. A tree
says what goods there are of that kind, each at its step of the tree: basic goods out of the ground, refined
once, developed into what people use, and one advanced good on top. The ports' files, in world/ports, say how
many of each a port holds, makes and uses, and name a good by the name it has here.

So far only the Developed and Advanced steps are played. The Refined and Basic steps, and what each good is
made from, come with the next parts of this step.

A file's name is the tree's id, in small letters: food.txt. Files that start with an underscore, like this one,
aren't read.

    # Food
    draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
    stands for: food, by its ration crates

    Developed:
    - ration crates [ration_crate]: The crates a pantry holds, two hundred sittings each.
    - ration packs, by the case [ration_pack]: Packs and tins for one person.

    Advanced:
    - provisions, by the hamper: Fresh bread, honey, sausage.

THE TOP

The first line is the tree's name, after a #.

draft:        who wrote it. The game doesn't read it.

stands for:   which of the four supplies every port already has a word for (food, fuel, medicine, parts) this
              tree stands behind, and by which of its goods: food, by its ration crates. That good's amount is
              then what the star map and the Market count for the supply: how many of it a port has, against
              what it usually holds, is its plenty, normal, short or scarce, and what things cost follows from
              that as before. A tree without this line (water and air, comforts) has amounts and no word.

A GOOD

A line under a step, starting with a dash:

    - ration packs, by the case [ration_pack]: Packs and tins for one person.

its name      as it's counted, in the plural: ration crates, medkits, machines. This is what a port's file
              calls it, and what the game says: "1,180 ration crates".

, by the ...  how it's counted, when its name isn't a count: drinking water, by the cask ("150 casks of drinking
              water"); jump fuel, by the measure. Leave it out for a name that counts itself.

[item]        the game's own item, by its id in world/items, if the good is one: [ration_crate]. Leave it out
              if it isn't. Nothing is done with it yet; it's for the steps where the ship buys and carries the
              goods themselves.

: words       a few words about it, for whoever reads the file and the Story board.

A good's name is used once in all the trees. Take a good out, or change its name, and the ports' files that
name it are wrong: npm run check-story says which lines.

CHANGING THEM IN THE GAME

The Story board has Goods: each tree as boxes by step, with the ports that make each good and how many are made
and used a day across the map. Click a box to change its line, then Keep and Save.
