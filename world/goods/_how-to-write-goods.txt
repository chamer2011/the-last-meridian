HOW TO WRITE THE WORLD'S GOODS
==============================

Every file in this folder is a tree of goods: food, fuel, medicine, parts, water and air, comforts. A tree
says what goods there are of that kind, each at its step of the tree: basic goods out of the ground, refined
once, developed into what people use, and one advanced good on top. It says what each good is made from, too.
The ports' files, in world/ports, say how many of each a port holds, makes and uses, and name a good by the
name it has here.

All four steps are played. Only the basic goods come out of the ground, at the ports that have ground for
them, and nothing else is made from nothing: every other good is made from the goods its line names.

A file's name is the tree's id, in small letters: food.txt. Files that start with an underscore, like this one,
aren't read.

    # Food
    draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
    stands for: food, by its ration crates
    hands need: ration crates

    Basic:
    - grain, by the load: Barley, wheat and beans off a field.
    - kelp, by the load: Cut from the lines at slack water.

    Refined:
    - meal, by the sack, made from grain: Grain dried and milled.
    - kelp cake, by the block, made from kelp: Kelp pressed and dried on the rafts.

    Developed:
    - ration crates [ration_crate], made from meal, kelp cake: The crates a pantry holds.
    - ration packs, by the case [ration_pack_case], made from meal: Packs and tins for one person.

    Advanced:
    - provisions, by the hamper, made from ration crates, fruit and honey: Fresh bread, honey, sausage.

THE TOP

The first line is the tree's name, after a #.

draft:          who wrote it. The game doesn't read it.

stands for:     which of the four supplies every port already has a word for (food, fuel, medicine, parts) this
                tree stands behind, and by which of its goods: food, by its ration crates. That good's amount
                is then what the star map and the Market count for the supply: how many of it a port has,
                against what it usually holds, is its plenty, normal, short or scarce, and what things cost
                follows from that as before. A tree without this line (water and air, comforts) has amounts
                and no word.

Three more lines say what a port's makers need besides the goods they make things from. Each names one of this
tree's goods, and a tree has a line only if it's that kind of tree:

hands need:     a port that's short of this good has fewer hands at its makers, and at its ground. With all it
                usually holds, or four fifths of it, every hand is at work; below that fewer and fewer, down
                to three quarters of their work when there's none. Food has it for its ration crates, water
                and air for its drinking water. Where two trees have one, the port goes by the one it's worse
                off for.

the sick need:  a port that's scarce of this good has more of its people laid up. With half of what it usually
                holds or more, nobody extra is; below that more and more, down to 85 hundredths of their work
                when there's none. Medicine has it, for its medicine crates. It counts on top of the hands.

power is:       what every port's makers run on. With half of what it usually holds or more, they have all
                the power they want; below that less and less, down to four tenths of their work when there's
                none, which is what can be done by hand. Fuel has it, for its fuel cells. The makers of that
                same tree don't need it: a refinery runs on what it makes, or no port could ever get its
                power back. The ground doesn't need it either: a field gives the same in the dark.

A port that holds none of a good isn't short of it: Foundry Seven's machines neither eat nor drink.

THE STEPS

Basic:          what comes out of the ground: grain, sour gas, ore, ice, night flowers, wrecks, and blood,
                which on Anselm's Mercy is how care is paid for. A basic good isn't made from anything, so
                its line has no "made from", and no maker makes it: a port has it from its own ground (the
                Ground part of its file), or sends to another port for it.

Refined:        a basic good worked up once: meal from grain, refined fuel from sour gas or ice, scrap from
                wrecks.

Developed:      what people use: ration crates, fuel cells, medicine crates, parts crates, drinking water,
                clothes.

Advanced:       one step further, for a few: provisions, jump fuel, surgeon's stores, machines.

Every refined, developed and advanced good has to say what it's made from. npm run check-story says which
one doesn't, and which basic good wrongly does.

A GOOD

A line under a step, starting with a dash:

    - ration packs, by the case [ration_pack_case], made from meal: Packs and tins for one person.

its name        as it's counted, in the plural: ration crates, medkits, machines. This is what a port's file
                calls it, and what the game says: "1,180 ration crates".

, by the ...    how it's counted, when its name isn't a count: drinking water, by the cask ("150 casks of
                drinking water"); jump fuel, by the measure. Leave it out for a name that counts itself.

[item]          the item one of this good is when it's carried, by its id in world/items: [ration_crate] for a
                ration crate, [meal] for a sack of meal. It's what rides in the Meridian's freight hold when she
                carries the good or buys it by the load, and its file gives its price, its weight and how many
                go to a square. A good with no item can't be carried: a dead ship is the only one.

, made from ... the goods it can be made from, with commas between them: made from meal, kelp cake. Never an
                and: some goods have one in their own name (plasma and dressings). They can be goods of
                another tree: a fuel cell's casing comes out of a parts crate, and refined fuel can be drained
                from a wreck. This is the list a port's maker picks from: a maker says how many of which it
                takes (world/ports), and one that takes a good that isn't in this list is something npm run
                check-story says. A basic good has none.

: words         a few words about it, for whoever reads the file and the Story board.

A good's name is used once in all the trees. Take a good out, or change its name, and the ports' files that
name it are wrong: npm run check-story says which lines. It also says when the goods go round in a ring (one
made from another that's made from the first), since then neither could ever be made.

CHANGING THEM IN THE GAME

The Story board has Goods: each tree as boxes by step, from the ground on the left to the advanced good on
the right, with an arrow from each good to what's made from it, the ports that make each good or have ground
for it, and how many are used, go into other things and are made a day across the map. Click a box to change
its line, then Keep and Save. A tree's own box has what hands, the sick and power need.
