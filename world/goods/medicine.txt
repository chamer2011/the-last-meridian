# Medicine
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
stands for: medicine, by its medicine crates
the sick need: medicine crates

Basic:
- night flowers, by the basket: Picked after dark, for what's distilled from them.
- reagents, by the barrel: Drawn off a refinery: what a laboratory makes its compounds from.
- blood, by the unit: Given, or paid: on Anselm's Mercy it's how care is paid for.

Refined:
- extracts, by the flask, made from night flowers: Distilled from what grows: Lumen's night flowers, a monk's garden.
- compounds, by the jar, made from reagents: Made up in a laboratory, from what a refinery draws off.
- plasma and dressings, by the case, made from blood: Blood spun down, and linen boiled and cut: what a ward gets through first.

Developed:
- medicine crates [medicine_crate], made from extracts, compounds: Twenty doses to a crate: what a medbay's cabinet holds.
- medkits, by the case [medkit], made from compounds, plasma and dressings: Kits for one wound, on the spot, a case of them at a time.

Advanced:
- surgeon's stores, by the case, made from medkits: Surgical kits and synthskin, for the worst cases.
