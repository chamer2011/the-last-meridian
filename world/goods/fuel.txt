# Fuel
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
stands for: fuel, by its jump fuel
power is: fuel cells

Basic:
- sour gas, by the canister: What the wells under a rock give up, and what a skimmer takes out of a cloud.

Refined:
- refined fuel, by the drum [refined_fuel], made from sour gas, ice, wrecks: Gas and ice worked into something that burns clean, or drained from a dead ship's tanks: what cells are charged from and jump fuel is made of.

Developed:
- fuel cells [fuel_cell], made from refined fuel, parts crates: The cells a generator burns. One runs the Meridian for eight hours. The casings come out of a parts crate.

Advanced:
- jump fuel, by the measure, made from refined fuel: What a jump burns: one to three measures from the tank.
- hot cells, made from fuel cells: Cells packed tighter, for a rig and whatever else needs more than a cell gives.
