# Water and air
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
hands need: drinking water

Basic:
- ice, by the load: Cut from a ring, a comet or a cloud. Water starts here, and so does some fuel.
- well and sea water, by the tank: Drawn from under the ground, caught off the rain, or pumped out of a sea.

Refined:
- water, by the tank, made from ice, well and sea water: Melted, drawn or caught where it's found, and filtered once.

Developed:
- drinking water, by the cask, made from water: Filtered and casked. People drink before they eat.
- air bottles [air_bottle], made from water: Air split from water and bottled: an hour outside in a suit, or a room's worth on a bad day.

Advanced:
- reserve tanks, made from air bottles: Air stored by the tank, to carry a ship or a station through a breach.
