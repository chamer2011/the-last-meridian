# Comforts
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner

Refined:
- mash, by the vat: Fruit, grain or honey set to work in a vat.
- cloth, by the roll: Wool and kelp thread, woven.
- cured leaf, by the brick: Tea and tobacco leaf, dried and pressed into bricks.

Developed:
- beer and spirits, by the keg, made from mash: Stone ale, ship's beer and worse. A crew with something to drink holds together.
- clothes, by the bale, made from cloth: What people wear and mend, by the bale.
- tea and tobacco, by the chest, made from cured leaf: Tea, coffee and tobacco, for a hard day.

Advanced:
- fine things, by the case, made from beer and spirits, clothes: Vantor red, an evening gown: what people pay dear for, and give as gifts.
