# Food
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
stands for: food, by its ration crates
hands need: ration crates

Basic:
- grain, by the load: Barley, wheat and beans off a field, or off a strip of soil under a lamp.
- kelp, by the load: Cut from the lines at slack water, wet and heavy.
- greens, by the crate [produce]: Leaves and roots fresh from a bed, eaten where they grow.

Refined:
- meal, by the sack, made from grain: Grain dried and milled: what crates and packs are packed from.
- kelp cake, by the block, made from kelp: Kelp pressed and dried on the rafts. It keeps for years, and tastes like it.

Developed:
- ration crates [ration_crate], made from meal, kelp cake: The crates a pantry holds: two hundred sittings each, a day's food for a hundred people.
- ration packs, by the case [ration_pack], made from meal: Packs and tins for one person, a case of them at a time.

Advanced:
- provisions, by the hamper, made from ration crates, fruit and honey: Fresh bread, honey, sausage: a table worth sitting at.
