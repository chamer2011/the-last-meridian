# Parts
draft: the AI's, not yet edited by the owner
stands for: parts, by its parts crates

Basic:
- ore, by the load [ore]: Rock with metal in it.
- wrecks: Dead ships, towed in or cut up where they lie: scrap in their hulls, and fuel still in some of their tanks.

Refined:
- metal stock, by the load [metal_stock], made from ore: Ore smelted into plate and bar.
- scrap, by the load [scrap_load], made from wrecks: Dead ships cut up and sorted, by the load.

Developed:
- parts crates [parts_crate], made from metal stock, scrap: A crate of spares: one repair, or half of an artificial limb.
- circuit boards, by the case [circuit_board_case], made from scrap: Boards stripped and re-flashed, for anything that thinks.

Advanced:
- machines [machines], made from parts crates, circuit boards: Whole machines built from crates and boards: a rig's parts, weapons, a port's own plant.
