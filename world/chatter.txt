# CHATTER: what people say to nobody in particular
#
# This is Claude's draft, not yet edited.
#
# Now and then, someone near you in the room says something under their breath, and it hangs over their head for a
# moment: a line from this file, from one of the moments that's true for them right now. Nobody answers, and nothing
# changes because of it. It's the ship talking to itself.
#
# Each part below is a moment: its name and a colon, then its lines, one to a line, each starting with "- ". Change any
# line, add more, take some away. Keep each one short, since it hangs over someone's head: 60 letters at most. A part
# can be left with no lines, and then that moment says nothing.
#
# The moments, and when each is true:
#   Fire here           the room they're in is on fire
#   The alarm           the alarm is ringing
#   Hull breached       there's a hole in the hull somewhere aboard
#   Fire aboard         something's burning somewhere aboard
#   No power            the room they're in has no power
#   In the dark         the room they're in has its lights out
#   Someone died        someone aboard has died in the last three days
#   After the mutiny    the five days after the mutiny was fought
#   Grumbling           a mutiny is brewing
#   A good table        a good table was laid this evening, after a hard day
#   Hungry              the kettle ran dry before everyone had eaten, today or yesterday
#   Short of food       the ship's food has run out
#   Short of medicine   the ship's medicine has run out
#   Hurt                they're hurt
#   Under way           the ship is jumping
#   Low morale          the ship's morale is under 35
#   High morale         the ship's morale is 70 or more
#   Eating              they're eating
#   At work             they're at work, in the day
#   No tea              the crew has run out of tea and tobacco
#   No drink            the crew has run out of drink
#   At night            it's between ten at night and six in the morning, and they're awake
#   Anywhere            always
#
# Someone says something from the first moment on that list that's true for them, or the next: whoever's in a burning
# room talks about the fire before the soup. Nobody asleep, laid up or talking to you says anything.
#
# After changing this file, run "npm run check-story", and close and reopen the game.

Fire here:
- Get out! Get out of here!
- Grab an extinguisher or get clear!
- Can't breathe in here!
- It's spreading! Move!

The alarm:
- That's the alarm. That's the real alarm.
- Stations! Everyone to stations!
- Is it a drill? Tell me it's a drill.
- What now? What is it now?

Hull breached:
- Feel that draught? That's the void.
- Seal the hatch! Seal it!
- Don't go near the lock.
- My ears keep popping.

Fire aboard:
- Smell that? Something's burning.
- Fire crews are moving. Stay clear.
- If it reaches the stores, we're done.
- Keep the doors shut. Starve it.

No power:
- Everything's dead in here.
- No power, no screens, no idea.
- Engineering had better be on this.
- The cold's creeping in already.

In the dark:
- Who turned out the lights?
- Mind your feet, there's stuff everywhere.
- I can't see my own hands.
- Somebody find the switch. Or a torch.
- Dark like this, you hear everything.

Someone died:
- Did you hear who we lost?
- Another name for the bulkhead.
- I keep thinking it could've been me.
- Light a candle if you've got one.
- They were here yesterday. Just yesterday.

After the mutiny:
- I knew some of them. That's the worst part.
- There's still blood on deck one.
- Who's next, if the captain falls?
- The brig's never been this full.
- Keep your voice down. People are jumpy.
- They thought they could take the ship.
- My hands haven't stopped shaking.

Grumbling:
- Somebody should say something to the captain.
- Who decided we'd go hungry? Not us.
- Quiet. Walls have ears on this ship.
- If the officers won't act, someone will.
- Things can't go on like this.

A good table:
- Bread. Real bread, still warm.
- Honey. I'd forgotten honey.
- Sausage! Don't ask, just eat.
- After today, we needed that.
- Somebody remembered we're people.
- The quartermaster opened the hampers.

Hungry:
- The kettle was empty when I got there.
- Missed the sitting. Again.
- My stomach's louder than the engines.
- Somebody's eating better than us.
- Half rations of half rations.
- Dreamt about bread. Just bread.

Short of food:
- The pantry shelves echo now.
- How long do the crates last? Really.
- The quartermaster won't look anyone in the eye.
- We're counting beans. Actual beans.

Short of medicine:
- No painkillers left. Grit your teeth.
- The doc's rationing bandages now.
- Don't get hurt. That's the whole plan.
- The cabinet's empty. I looked twice.

Hurt:
- Don't make me laugh, it pulls the stitches.
- The doc says rest. The doc doesn't do my shifts.
- It's nothing. It's mostly nothing.
- Mind the arm. The left one. Both, really.
- I've had worse. I can't remember when.

Under way:
- Hold on to something.
- Jumping again. My stomach hates this.
- Hope the plot's right this time.
- Feel the deck shake? We're moving.

Low morale:
- What's the point of any of it?
- Nobody's told us anything in days.
- Keep your head down and your mouth shut.
- Everyone's tired. Everyone's angry.
- I used to whistle on shift.
- This ship's running on spite.

High morale:
- Feels like we might actually make it.
- Heard a joke in the mess. Real laughing.
- Whoever fixed the heaters, I love you.
- Good shift. Don't jinx it.
- The captain's got a plan. Probably.

Eating:
- Soup again. Bold choice.
- It's warm. That's the nicest thing I can say.
- Who keeps salting it? Stop salting it.
- Eat slow. Makes it feel like more.
- Trade you my bread for a quiet hour.
- Something from the garden today. Practically a feast.
- The cook says it's a stew. I believe the cook.

At work:
- Hand me the long spanner. No, the other one.
- If it hums, leave it. If it clanks, call me.
- Logged it. Logged it again. Nobody reads the log.
- Two more hours and I'm horizontal.
- This panel's been patched more than my boots.
- Who signed off on this weld?
- Check it twice. The ship doesn't forgive.

No tea:
- The urn's dry again.
- Hot water. That's the tea now.
- I'd trade a boot for a smoke.
- No tea, no tobacco. No patience.
- I've been chewing the old leaves.
- Somebody find us a port with tea.

No drink:
- Not a drop aboard. Not one.
- Even the bucket's empty.
- I miss the bad beer. That's how bad.
- A dry ship's a short-tempered ship.
- The still can't keep up with us all.
- Water again. Lovely.

At night:
- Can't sleep. The hull's ticking again.
- Night shift. The ship talks to itself.
- Keep it down, people are trying to dream.
- Quiet tonight. I don't trust quiet.

Anywhere:
- Nine hundred souls and one coffee pot.
- Did anyone else feel that shudder?
- Same corridor, same faces, same hum.
- I keep counting the days. It stopped helping.
- My bunkmate snores in three languages.
- Somebody keep an eye on the gauges.
- Wonder what the stars look like from home.
- Don't touch that, it's holding something up.
