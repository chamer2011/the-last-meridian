What the player sees:
A records terminal beside the scan bed, with the register of everyone aboard on it: who they are, where they sleep, and how they are. Dr. Aduro keeps it up to date at three in the morning, when nobody's queueing.

Files:
triage.txt: The doctor's standing orders, typed and then corrected in pen. The serious ones get a dose first; the coughs sit it out; nobody who can walk takes a cot. Underneath, in a different pen: "when the cabinet's empty, the fevers kill. Get me medicine."
