What the player sees:
A grey cabinet beside the reactor with no keyboard at all: a row of interlock lamps, and a label that says CONTROLLED FROM THE GAUGES. Someone has written "and only from the gauges" under it in grease pencil, and underlined it twice.

Files:
interlocks.txt: The safety interlocks, one a line: what each one watches, the number that trips it, and what it does when it trips. Three have been bypassed with a jumper wire, and each has Marrow's initials beside it.

Programs:
Scram the reactor: The rods drop with a sound you feel in your teeth, and the drive winds down to nothing. Until someone brings it back up, the Meridian goes nowhere. -> * engines off at meridian; * flag reactor_scrammed: The Meridian's reactor was scrammed from the safety controller ? not flag reactor_scrammed
Bring the reactor back up: The rods come out a finger's width at a time, and the drive comes back with a long, rising hum. -> * engines on at meridian; * unflag reactor_scrammed ? flag reactor_scrammed
