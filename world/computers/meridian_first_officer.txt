What the player sees:
A crew terminal in the chart room, a step from the bridge, its keys worn smooth in the pattern of the watch bill. Voss works here when she isn't on the bridge, and there's a cup of tea gone cold on the shelf above it, as there always is.

Files:
watch_bill.txt: Who stands which watch, in Voss's hand: names crossed out and written back as people are hurt, moved or relieved. The column headed SPARE HANDS is never long enough.
officers.txt: A line on each officer, dated and initialled. Most are about the work. A few are not.
