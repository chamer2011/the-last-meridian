What the player sees:
The navigation core under the plot table: no screen or keys of its own, only the boards that plot every jump, and a cable to the comms relay, where the charts come in. Whatever reaches it comes down that cable, or through the table above.

Files:
jump_log.txt: Every jump the Meridian has made since the Fall: where from, where to, how long the core took to plot it, and how close the ship came to the line it drew.
charts.txt: The charts the relay has fed it, the old Concord ones and the ones bought since, with the dates they stopped being true.

Logs:
- news

Programs:
Lock the navigation: The core's boards go still, and the line on the plot table stops at the edge of the glass. Until it's unlocked, every jump is plotted by hand. -> * navigation off at meridian; * flag navigation_locked: The Meridian's navigation was locked from its core ? not flag navigation_locked
Unlock the navigation: The boards wake one by one, and the table's line comes back across the glass. -> * navigation on at meridian; * unflag navigation_locked ? flag navigation_locked

Network:
meridian_plot_table: The table above it.

Trusts:
- the comms relay: programs
- meridian_plot_table: reading
